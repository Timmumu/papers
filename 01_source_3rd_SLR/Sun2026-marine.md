## Abstract summary
Condition monitoring and fault diagnosis of marine machinery are essential for ensuring the safety and stability of offshore operations. Traditional approaches, which typically rely solely on either physical mechanisms or large volumes of data, often struggle to achieve both high accuracy and adaptability to component degradation. To address this issue, this article proposes a novel digital twin framework based on deep multimodal information fusion (DMIF-DT) that integrates the complementary strengths of a physics-based model (PBM) and a data-driven model (DDM). An attention-enhanced multiscale convolutional network is developed to extract features from sensor measurements and calibrated simulation data.
