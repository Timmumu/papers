## Abstract summary
Structural health monitoring is vital for the safety and longevity of infrastructure, particularly in seismic zones. This study focuses on identifying the dynamic properties of a reinforced concrete building in Chile's Valpara & iacute;so region. Using an experimental approach, the study compares ambient vibration records, seismic events (moment magnitude > 4), and data collected during adjacent construction activities. Force-balanced accelerometers were used for vibration measurements. The analysis employs the Stochastic Subspace Identification with Covariances (SSI-COV) method within an operational modal analysis framework to extract the building's modal parameters without requiring artificial excitations.
