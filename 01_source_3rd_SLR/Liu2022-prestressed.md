# Liu et al. (2022) — Intelligent Analysis Method for Prestressed Steel Structure Construction Safety Based on Digital Twin

- **ID:** 3597 | **Source:** Scopus | **DOI:** 10.19815/j.jace.2021.07126
- **Journal:** Journal of Architecture and Civil Engineering
- **Authors:** Liu, Z. S.; Shi, G. I.; Jiao, Z. D.

## Abstract summary
Aiming at the problem of low accuracy of structural safety performance analysis caused by time-varying mechanical parameters in the construction process of prestressed steel structure, an intelligent analysis method of prestressed steel structure construction safety based on digital twin was proposed. Combined with the structure and construction characteristics of spoke cable truss, the characteristics of structural safety performance during construction were summarized, the modeling method of digital twin was introduced, and the construction and application process of digital twin were refined. On this basis, the twin model with high fidelity was corrected and obtained by integrating intelligent algorithms.
