# Apolinário et al. (2024) — FingerCI: Writing industrial process specifications from network traffic

- **ID:** 4020 | **Source:** Web of Science | **DOI:** 10.1016/j.ijcip.2024.100725

- **Authors:** Apolinário, F.; Escravana, N.; Hervé, É; Pardal, M. L.; Correia, M.

## Abstract summary
Critical infrastructures (CIs) are often targets of cyber-attacks, requiring accurate process specifications to identify and defend against incidents. However, discrepancies between these specifications and real-world CI conditions arise due to the costly process of manual specification by experts. This paper introduces FINGERCI, a method for automatically generating CI process specifications through network traffic analysis and physical behavior modeling. By defining a Specification Language that integrates with existing systems, FINGERCI extracts industrial process specifications without infrastructure changes or downtime. The specifications include a behavior model that validates physical correctness.
