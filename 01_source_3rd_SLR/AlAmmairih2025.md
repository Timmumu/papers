## Abstract summary
Ensuring the safety and resilience of critical civil and transportation engineering infrastructure requires real-time, intelligent monitoring systems capable of detecting early signs of deterioration. Traditional Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) methods—primarily reliant on manual inspections or threshold-based sensor alerts—struggle to deliver the responsiveness, adaptability, and scalability demanded by modern urban environments in the fields of civil and transportation engineering. This paper introduces a hybrid Artificial Intelligence (AI) framework that integrates machine learning (ML), deep learning (DL), and rule-based reasoning within an edge–cloud architecture for real-time infrastructure monitoring.
