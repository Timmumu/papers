## Abstract summary
The development of smart cities is being driven by consumer electronics-grade digital twin led by a robust combination of advanced artificial intelligence (AI) technologies. AI's capabilities for complex data analysis and pattern recognition provide a solid foundation for real-time data transfer and feedback in digital twin, enhancing simulation and analytics capabilities. The security and privacy of consumer electronics-grade digital twin are critical for smart cities, especially in the context of Internet of Things security, as they simulate and control real-world critical infrastructure. The combination of sophisticated AI algorithms for network security and threat detection can effectively prevent potential network attacks and data breaches, ensuring the safety and reliability of digital twin systems.
