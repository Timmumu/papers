# Ullah et al. (2026) — A scoping review of green Internet of Things in construction and smart cities: current applications, adoption strategies and future directions

- **ID:** 3494 | **Source:** Scopus | **DOI:** 10.1108/SASBE-11-2023-0349
- **Journal:** Smart and Sustainable Built Environment
- **Authors:** Ullah, F.; Olatunji, O.; Qayyum, S.

## Abstract summary
Purpose – Contemporary technological disruptions are espoused as though they stimulate sustainable growth in the built environment through the Green Internet of Things (G-IoT). Learning from discipline-specific experiences, this paper articulates recent advancements in the knowledge and concepts of G-IoT in relation to the construction and smart city sectors. It provides a scoping review for G-IoT as an overlooked dimension. Attention was paid to modern circularity, cleaner production and sustainability as key benefits of G-IoT adoption in line with the United Nations’ Sustainable Development Goals (UN-SDGs). In addition, this study also investigates the current application and adoption strategies of G-IoT.
