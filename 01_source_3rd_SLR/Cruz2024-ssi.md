## Abstract summary
The soil-structure interaction (SSI) is an integral part of the seismic response of structures. The knowledge about its effects is based largely on numerical simulations involving simplified models. In this paper, we examine the consequence of several assumptions in SSI models of buildings, with emphasis on the rigid foundation assumption. To this end, we analyze several variants of a linear three-dimensional finite element model of the structure and soil corresponding to a uniquely instrumented 50-story skyscraper, with a basement on a pile foundation, located in southwest China. The models are excited by triaxial motion due to a vertically incident plane wave.
