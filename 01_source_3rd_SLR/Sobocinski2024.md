## Abstract summary
Digital twins (DTs) can connect inspection data with product models to support safer, more efficient lifecycle decisions. This paper proposes a CAD-native workflow for implementing a digital twin that visualizes and manages non-destructive testing (NDT) results directly on a 3D model. The method supports over-the-surface data (ultrasonic C-scans, UT) via UV mapping and projected images (thermography, TT) via planar projection, both executed in Siemens NX with custom macros for point localization and on-surface measurement. We validate the approach on a bottom nacelle panel from a Honeywell HTF7000 turbofan engine, acquired via 3D scanning and reverse engineering.
