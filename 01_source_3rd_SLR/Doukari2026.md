## Abstract summary
Achieving the EU’s energy and decarbonisation goals requires more efficient management of building renovation projects. Whilst digital twin (DT) technology is increasingly adopted across the built environment, most existing research and practice focus on its application during the operational phase of built assets such as energy monitoring, space and occupancy management, and predictive maintenance. However, there remains a gap in leveraging DT potential in renovation projects, supporting multi-domain performance tracking. This study presents a pilot implementation and preliminary evaluation of a Renovation Digital Twin (RDT) platform tailored to the specific challenges of renovation projects.
