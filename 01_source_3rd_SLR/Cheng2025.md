## Abstract summary
Horizontal swivel construction, characterized by minimal disruption to existing railways and highways, has become a critical method for constructing long-span bridges over operational railways. However, swivel construction presents challenges, such as heavy loads, elevated overturning risks, and rapid state transitions. Therefore, effective monitoring and control are essential to ensure safety, structural stability, and precision. This study clarifies the primary and secondary objectives of swivel-monitoring feedback control and establishes their logical interconnections. The " traction-rotation" process was decomposed into five monitoring components: support system, traction system, swivel posture, structural stress, and surrounding environment.
