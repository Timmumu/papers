## Abstract summary
The adoption of the Internet of Things (IoT) in the construction industry has garnered significant academic interest, with a notable increase in research over the past decade. This study conducts a scientometric review of 428 papers published between 2010 and 2024, aiming to identify key trends, highlight emerging themes, and offer directions for future research to avoid redundancy. Through bibliographic coupling and scientific network analysis, the review uncovers prominent co-occurring keywords and themes, such as the integration of IoT with artificial intelligence, blockchain, cloud and edge computing, Building Information Modelling (BIM), and Digital Twins. A major finding is that improving safety, particularly in worker health and underground construction, is the most frequently addressed objective.
