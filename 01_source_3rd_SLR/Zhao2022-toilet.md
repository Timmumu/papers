# Zhao et al. (2022) — Digital Twin Evaluation of Environment and Health of Public Toilet Ventilation Design Based on Building Information Modeling

- **Authors:** Zhao, L.; Zhang, H.; Wang, Q.; Sun, B.; Liu, W. H.; Qu, K. C.; Shen, X. M.

## Abstract summary
Poor indoor air quality reduces the comfort experienced in the environment and can also harm our physical health. Mechanical ventilation design plays an important role in improving the indoor environment and the safety of public toilets. Therefore, in this study, we aimed to evaluate public toilet ventilation design schemes through a digital twin to determine the most effective scheme for reducing indoor pollutant concentrations. In this study, we used Autodesk Revit to create a digital twin BIM of different ventilation systems. We simulated the diffusion of pollutants in these models using computational fluid dynamics (CFD)-based methods, and we used DesignBuilder to simulate building energy consumption.
