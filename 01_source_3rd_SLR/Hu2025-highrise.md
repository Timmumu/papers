## Abstract summary
The safety performance of the numerous high-rise building structures of China has a significant impact on urban security. To address the challenges in evaluating engineering design verification and in-service performance of high-rise building structures，this study integrates structural monitoring，finite element model updating，and building information modeling（BIM）technologies to establish a digital twin model for high-rise building structures. A method for engineering design verification and in-service performance evaluation of high-rise buildings was proposed，with a particular focus on engineering design parameters such as the inter-story drift ratio specified in the design codes.
