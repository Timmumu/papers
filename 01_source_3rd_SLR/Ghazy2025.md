# Ghazy et al. (2025) — Federation of non-destructive testing data for bridge asset management

- **ID:** 3962 | **Source:** Web of Science | **DOI:** 10.1680/jcien.25.00463

- **Authors:** Ghazy, S.; Mundell, C.; Argyle, T.; Hendy, C.

## Abstract summary
The application of data federation to enhance bridge infrastructure asset management through the integration of non-destructive testing (NDT) data was investigated. Focusing on ultrasonic testing, ground-penetrating radar and visual inspection methods, the research addressed the challenge of unifying diverse datasets into a coherent three-dimensional digital environment. A key contribution is the development of standardised workflows for importing and visualising NDT data within Autodesk Revit, enabling improved spatial representation, coordinate alignment and data layering. While the approach demonstrates significant potential for enhancing inspection and maintenance decision making, limitations related to proprietary software and data accessibility were identified.
