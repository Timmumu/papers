## Abstract summary
Intelligent construction is revolutionizing how bridges are designed, constructed and maintained. The key technologies, such as building information modeling(BIM), the Internet of Things (IoT), AI in construction, etc., are changing traditional construction procedures into extremely productive, economical, and environmentally friendly processes. This study categories the key common technologies in the intelligent construction of long-span bridges into four modules; parametric design, virtual construction, intelligent construction and equipment, and intelligent monitoring and control. In parametric design, the utilization of a data-driven BIM forward design method, parametric digital modelling and light weighting technologies improves the design efficiency.
