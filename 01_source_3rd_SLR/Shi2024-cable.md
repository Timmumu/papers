## Abstract summary
The construction process of cable structures is complicated, and the construction errors are highly unpredictable, which has a negative impact on the structural safety. Existing research of the influence of construction error on structural mechanical performance has issues such as a lack of consistency in the model, insufficient accuracy and efficiency. Therefore, twinning simulation and model test of construction error assessment for cable truss structure were carried out. Parametric model and construction error space model of cable truss were developed, and the nonlinear mapping relationship between influencing factors and structural mechanical properties was obtained.
