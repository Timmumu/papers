## Abstract summary
The adoption of digital twin technology (DTT) for enhancing construction project performance, sustainability, and safety of construction workers, among others, is common practice in developed countries. Meanwhile, construction projects in developing nations are suffering from poor outcomes, which could benefit from DTT. Therefore, this research investigates the drivers of the adoption of DTT implementation in the Nigerian construction industry. Close-ended questionnaires were administered to digitally inclined professionals using purposive and snowballing techniques to elicit necessary information on the drivers of DTT implementation. These respondents include architects, engineers, quantity surveyors, and builders in the study area.
