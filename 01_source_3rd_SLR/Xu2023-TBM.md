## Abstract summary
Digitization and intelligence are the future development directions of tunnel engineering.To achieve digital safety construction, and disaster prevention and control of Tunnel Boring Machine (TBM) tunnels, we proposed a digital twin framework for TBM tunnel excavation based on intelligent geological perception technology and digital twin theory.
