## Abstract summary
The technology of unmanned autonomous excavators focuses on four core areas: environmental perception, task decision-making, trajectory planning, and motion control. This technology integrates advanced sensor systems, intelligent algorithms, and autonomous control strategies to achieve efficient operation of excavators under unmanned operation. In terms of environmental perception, unmanned autonomous excavators adopt multimodal sensor fusion technology, which achieves high-precision recognition and understanding of complex work scenes through various sensors such as laser radar and visual cameras, and provides reliable data support for their subsequent decision-making and planning.
