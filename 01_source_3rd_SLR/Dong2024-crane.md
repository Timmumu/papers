## Abstract summary
In the current intrinsic safety of cranes , poor monitoring efforts, single performance indicators and low data utilization lead to low efficiency and incomplete evaluation results in carrying capacity evaluation. Guided by the i-dea of virtual and real mutual control, mutual adjustment and complementarity' , the digital twin system construction method for evaluating the load carrying capacity of crane structures was proposed. The digital twin framework for the load carrying capacity of crane structures was designed by considering equipment service scenarios and utilizing a digital twin five-dimensional model.
