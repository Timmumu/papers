## Abstract summary
Intelligent mine ventilation systems are critical infrastructures for the intelligent transformation of mining operations. They are moving toward adaptive optimization of ventilation networks in complex environments through real-time multi-source sensing and algorithm-driven decision support; fully autonomous, safety-certified closed-loop control remains a long-term goal. Traditional ventilation systems, constrained by static design and passive response mechanisms, often show low resilience and slow recovery in emergencies such as gas outbursts and roadway collapses, which can accelerate accident escalation and increase risks to personnel and equipment. Drawing on resilience paradigms from power systems and urban infrastructure, this study proposes a "Resilient Mine Ventilation Network".
