# Samuel et al. (2022) — Defect-oriented supportive bridge inspection system featuring building information modeling and augmented reality

- **ID:** 4043 | **Source:** Web of Science | **DOI:** 10.1007/s41062-022-00847-3
- **Journal:** INNOVATIVE INFRASTRUCTURE SOLUTIONS
- **Authors:** Samuel, I. J.; Salem, O.; He, S.

## Abstract summary
Bridges are indispensable links of transportation infrastructure systems, and inspections play a critical role in maintaining bridge components in the state of good repair. Through a survey of bridge inspectors, the authors revealed that visual inspection techniques are the prominent inspection method but result in inaccuracy and ambiguity due to high variances among inspection results; modern inspections using drones and robots could improve efficiency but pose new challenges and do not reduce subjectivity. As a result, a novel, building information modeling- and augmented reality-based supportive inspection system (BASIS) that objectively captures bridge defects is proposed and validated.
