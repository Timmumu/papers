# Liu et al. (2023) — Supply chain management for modular construction using building information modeling

- **ID:** 3898 | **Source:** Web of Science | **DOI:** 10.15554/pcij68.5-02
- **Journal:** PCI JOURNAL
- **Authors:** Liu, X. F.; Zhao, J.; Sun, G. Z.; Zhou, G. S.; Han, X. D.

## Abstract summary
This paper presents an example of an integrated construction system that uses building information modeling (BIM), the internet of things (IoT), and geographic information system (GIS) technology to improve supply chain logistics management in construction projects, thereby increasing project efficiencies and controlling construction costs. The authors developed and tested a digital twin platform for the proposed system, which allows real-time simulation of logistics in modular construction. The proposed digital twin platform uses BIM and GIS as its foundation because BIM includes information on the plans, detailed geometry, properties, and quantities of modules, whereas GIS provides geospatial data for the modules along with vehicular information during transport. This system with integrated BIM, IoT, and GIS modules was applied in a new manufacturing company within its first five years…
