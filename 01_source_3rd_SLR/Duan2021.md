## Abstract summary
The Selective Laser Melting (SLM) of metal is developing towards higher precision, higher efficiency, and intelligence. Due to the extremely hot and cold characteristics of the laser melting process, the quality defects of the built parts have always hindered the wide application of SLM technology. To overcome these shortcomings and manufacture high-quality parts, an on-line monitoring and process control of the machining process was carried out. A digital twin-driven on-line monitoring method of metal SLM forming process was proposed, which was used to establish an intelligent building system and conduct case analysis.
