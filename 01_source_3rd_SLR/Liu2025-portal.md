## Abstract summary
The Jingkou Double-Track Railway Bridge carrying a section of the Chengdu-Chongqing Middle Line High-speed Railway intersects with piers No. 23 and No. 24 of the existing Shanghai-Chengdu Railway, The design adopts the plan of girder jack-up and pier replacement, which constructs new portal piers at the original pier location of the existing Shanghai-Chengdu Railway to support, remove, and replace the existing piers.
