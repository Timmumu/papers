## Abstract summary
The prefabricated building is one important way to transform traditional construction mode to modern industrial mode. Digital twinning can be used as the key technology to realize the goal of intelligent construction. Applying digital twin to the construction stage is the key strategy to improve the informationization and intelligence of construction process of prefabricated building. For this reason, an implementation of digital twin framework in the construction management was proposed. From the perspective of physical construction site entity modeling, digital twin virtual body modeling and virtual-real interaction modeling, the spatial dimension modeling theory of assembly construction process based on digital twin was put forward.
