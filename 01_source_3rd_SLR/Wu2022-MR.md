# Wu et al. (2022) — Real-time mixed reality-based visual warning for construction workforce safety

- **ID:** 3960 | **Source:** Web of Science | **DOI:** 10.1016/j.autcon.2022.104252
- **Journal:** AUTOMATION IN CONSTRUCTION
- **Authors:** Wu, S. Z.; Hou, L.; Zhang, G. M.; Chen, H. S.

## Abstract summary
Spatial locations of personnel, equipment, and materials are constantly changing as construction projects progress. The dynamic nature of the construction industry affects workers' performance of identifying hazards. Even though a great deal of effort has been made to improve construction safety, the construction industry still witnesses a high accident rate. In order to complement the existing body of knowledge relating to construction safety, this paper integrates Digital Twin (DT), Deep Learning (DL), and Mixed Reality (MR) technologies into a newly developed real-time visual warning system, which enables construction workers to proactively determine their safety status and avoid accidents.
