## Abstract summary
Spatial locations of personnel, equipment, and materials are constantly changing as construction projects progress. The dynamic nature of the construction industry affects workers' performance of identifying hazards. Even though a great deal of effort has been made to improve construction safety, the construction industry still witnesses a high accident rate. In order to complement the existing body of knowledge relating to construction safety, this paper integrates Digital Twin (DT), Deep Learning (DL), and Mixed Reality (MR) technologies into a newly developed real-time visual warning system, which enables construction workers to proactively determine their safety status and avoid accidents. **Key evidence (AG):** Real-time mixed reality-based visual warning system for construction workforce safety (RMIT U): integrates BIM + CV + DL; VCS simulates construction activities, identifies hazards from BIM models + real-time camera info; wearable MR devices present hazard information to workers for proactive actions; three outdoor tests; 96.1% hazard identification accuracy, 424ms delay, 72m² effective area. One-way monitoring→hazard identification→warnings; no actuation and no learning loop.


**Strength & gap filled (AH):** Delivers hazard warnings through ambient vision rather than the occupied focal channel: YOLOv4-tiny with Deep SORT tracks objects from CCTV streams, a 4D BIM-based virtual construction site computes red, yellow, and blue danger zones, and Microsoft HoloLens 2 overlays them in real time, tested in three quasi-on-site scenarios with detection accuracy above 96.1 percent, a 72 square meter effective area, and 11.47 cm average alignment error.

**Deficiencies & future work (AI):** Object localization suffers uncorrected perspective and lens distortion effects, virtual-to-real alignment is manual, the detector covers only person and truck classes from MS-COCO, and turning vehicles expose uncovered red zones. Future work includes distortion correction, image-based prediction for fast-moving hazards, additional IoT sensors, spatial-anchor based automatic alignment, and cognitive ergonomics evaluation of user workload.

**Fusion evidence (AC2):**
- **Data streams:** CCTV video detections (YOLOv4-tiny + DeepSORT) + 4D BIM geometry/state (three quasi-on-site tests).
- **Integration mechanism:** detected objects are mapped into the BIM-anchored virtual construction site which computes red/yellow/blue danger zones overlaid in HoloLens 2 (96.1% accuracy, 424 ms delay, 11.47 cm alignment error).
- **Why this code:** the danger-zone output depends on live detections and BIM geometry simultaneously, so Fused.
