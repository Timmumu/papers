## Abstract summary
Electrical safety is a critical aspect in design, operation and maintenance of all electrical installations. From planning stage, it is necessary to strictly adhere to the regulations that provide for the complete safety of the installation and to establish an adequate maintenance plan for the duration of its life. However, there are unpredictable situations, such as faults, that can potentially risk the safety of people and equipment installation, leading in some extreme cases to major incidents such as blackouts and fires. The digitalization is leading to huge benefits in every application area, and electrical safety is one of the aspects that could gain the greatest advantage from it.
