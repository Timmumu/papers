## Abstract summary
Construction load of adjacent works is the additional load imposed on existing underground structures due to the disturbance of the adjacent works construction. Such load may cause structural damages or even failure of existing underground structures, threatening the urban safety. However, it is still unclear how the construction load of adjacent works evolves. To probe this problem, it is required to uncover the coupling mechanism among the adjacent works, stratum, and existing structures, but an effective experimental method is still unavailable up to now. Hence, a digital twin-based experimental method was proposed for construction loads of adjacent works.
