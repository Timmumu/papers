## Abstract summary
This study introduces an 8D BIM-driven framework that transforms the digital twin into a proactive compliance engine. By integrating OSHA, NFPA, IBC, and FIDIC clauses into Solibri and BIM, the framework enables early stage design verification, significantly reducing design errors and accelerating corrective actions. The approach incorporates automatic regulatory hazard detection, multiple safety simulations, and legal compliance corrections, facilitating the identification and prevention of hazards like fall risks and fire egress issues before construction begins. By its application in a high-rise residential project, the framework showed how it improves safety in terms of early identification of hazards and boosts adherence to legal standards.
