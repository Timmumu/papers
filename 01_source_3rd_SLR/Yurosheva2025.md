## Abstract summary
Report. In modern construction, the technology of building information modeling (BIM) has proven its effectiveness in optimizing design work and increasing the level of automation of processes. However, in the mining industry, the potential of BIM technologies remains unrealized due to the specific features of mining operations, including their scale, complexity and heterogeneity of geological conditions. The purpose of the study. The main purpose of the work is to analyze the possibilities of implementing BIM technologies in the mining industry, with a special focus on developing an information model of a mine (MIM). The research is aimed at bridging the existing technological gap in the process of digital transformation of the industry. Scientific novelty.
