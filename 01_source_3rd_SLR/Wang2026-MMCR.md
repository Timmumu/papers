## Abstract summary
Despite recent advances in automation, the AECO industry still faces inefficiencies, high costs, and safety risks. To address these, we present MMCR, a mixed-reality (MR) and machine-learning (ML) guided cable-robotic system for prefabricated construction. A HoloLens 2 MR interface delivers spatially anchored guidance and risk alerts, while a Unity ML agent enables autonomous navigation, obstacle avoidance, grasping, and placement. In real-world tests, the ML-enabled cable robot manipulated precast components in real time, reducing manual intervention and cognitive load. Compared with manual and MR-only baselines, MMCR achieved a 4 & times; reduction in positioning error, 2.8 & times; faster task completion, and 88.9% fewer operator interventions.
