# Kang et al. (2025) — Generation and Verification of CFD-based ROM for Real-Time Thermal Flow Analysis in the Reactor Containment Building of Nuclear Power Plants

- **ID:** 3608 | **Source:** Scopus | **DOI:** 10.3795/KSME-B.2025.49.3.147
- **Journal:** Transactions of the Korean Society of Mechanical Engineers, B
- **Authors:** Kang, S.; Choi, D. K.; Son, S. M.; Choi, C.

## Abstract summary
This study develops a Reduced Order Model (ROM) based on computational fluid dynamics (CFD) for real-time temperature control within nuclear power plant containment buildings to integrate it with a digital twin system. Utilizing 57 CFD simulation cases under various operating conditions, the ROM was constructed using ANSYS Twin Builder and Twin Deployer for efficient modeling and validation. Verification results demonstrate that the ROM closely replicates CFD outcomes while significantly reducing computation time from 24 hours to under 3 seconds. This enhancement enables real-time monitoring and control of thermal dynamics within the containment structure, ensuring optimal performance and heightened safety.
