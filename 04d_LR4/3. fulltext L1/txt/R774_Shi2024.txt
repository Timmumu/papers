ABSTRACT
The large-span cable structure has the problems of complex component connection, cumbersome demolition
process and difficult process coordination, which lead to high construction safety risk and low efficiency.
Moreover, the optimization of the construction process scheme is difficult, and the convergence and visualization
of the finite element analysis are insufficient, which hinders the construction efficiency. This study proposes a
cable structure demolition optimization method driven by the digital twin (DT) evolution model. From the
perspective of virtual-real mapping, the DT evolution process of cable structure demolition is clarified. The
virtualization method of physical demolition process is formed by integrating visual modeling, simulation
modeling and data modeling. Taking full account of the temporal and spatial evolution of the demolition process,
a DT evolution model of the demolition process is established. Starting from the information flow, component
flow, control flow and their relationship, the problems existing in the process of demolition execution are
analyzed. Driven by the twin evolution process, a data mapping method for the demolition process is proposed.
The safety risk is characterized by the mechanical properties (MPs) of the structure, and the expression language
of the data association relationship is established. The particle swarm optimization back propagation neural
network (PSO-BPNN) is improved, and an intelligent analysis method of MPs in the whole process of demolition
is proposed. The best construction scheme is obtained by integrating the changes in MPs between the various
demolition steps. The information of the demolition evolution process is divided into functional modules, and a
DT platform is developed to efficiently guide the actual construction. Taking the demolition process of a cable
truss experimental model as an example, the effectiveness of the proposed method is verified. The research
results show that the DT evolution model can accurately map the actual demolition process. The deep learning
method analyzes the mechanical properties of the structure and obtains the best construction scheme quickly and
accurately. The development and application of the twin platform improves the degree of visualization and
realizes the efficient guidance of the virtual model to the actual construction.
1. Introduction
1.1. Background
The construction period of the cable structure is short and the shape
plasticity is strong. The cable member only bears the axial tension, the
material strength utilization rate is high, and the structural force is
reasonable. Cable structure is widely used in large-span engineering [1] .
The cable structure is mostly used in public buildings such as stadiums
and exhibition centers. Taking the cable component as the key stress
component, the structural systems such as cable truss [2] , cable dome
[3] and chord-supported structure [4] are formed. Due to the change of
building performance and structural safety performance, the cable
structure often needs to be reformed and dismantled. The cable structure
has many components and complex forces, which leads to complicated
construction process, difficult coordination and high safety risk [5,6] . In
the process of structural demolition, due to the unreasonable resource
allocation and construction scheme and the low degree of information
sharing, it is easy to lead to the occurrence of construction safety acci-
dents, which seriously threatens the life safety of construction personnel

In the process of structural construction, the cable force is an
important basis for measuring the safety state [8]. It is necessary to
clarify the development of each cable force to ensure that the cable force
can develop smoothly and evenly during the demolition process [9]. In
the process of demolition, according to the development of cable force,
the best demolition scheme needs to be obtained, and the efficient
guidance of the construction process are the focus of this study. In this
process, it is necessary to clarify the batching, grading and cable relax-
ation degree at all levels of the demolition process [10]. Many scholars
have proposed new methods and techniques in structural optimization.
Saleh et al. [11] proposed an optimization method for the mechanical
properties of shear members based on topology optimization. Through
numerical analysis, it was found that the optimized connection showed
superior performance, and showed higher shear strength and energy
dissipation capacity under monotonic and cyclic loading. Ghabussi et al.
[12] used steel curved dampers to optimize the seismic performance of
symmetrical and single-slope portal frames of variable-slope roofs.
Based on the finite element (FE) analysis method, the influence of the
curve damper on the energy dissipation, frame strength and stiffness of
the portal frame is evaluated. Ghabussi et al. [13,14] used the FE
analysis method to evaluate the seismic performance of the cold-formed
hot-rolled steel wall system with curved steel dampers. Considering the
strain energy, geometric comprehensive index and ultimate bearing
capacity, Cao et al. [15] optimized the design of free-form cable--
supported reticulated shell. The FE analysis verifies that the designed
structure has better mechanical properties and mesh quality. The above
optimization methods provide reliable technical support for the opti-
mization of mechanical properties of steel structures and composite
structures. Moreover, most of the above optimization methods use the
FE numerical simulation method, which also provides a reference for the
optimization of the structural construction scheme. In the analysis of
construction scheme, the traditional analysis method is to set the
boundary conditions and working conditions in the FE model [16], and
then obtain the change trend of cable force in the process of structural
demolition. There are many uncertain factors such as temperature and
wind load in the process of structural demolition. The traditional anal-
ysis method cannot accurately obtain the best demolition scheme. In
addition, the FE analysis method usually obtains the mechanical
response of the structure according to the specific construction in-
structions to judge the rationality of the scheme. This method is discrete
and cannot realize the continuous dynamic analysis of the construction
process. Therefore, the optimal selection of construction scheme cannot
be realized in the process of finite element calculation. FE analysis fo-
cuses on the mechanical information of the structure. However, in the
actual construction process, how to realize the efficient allocation of
resources in the construction process is the key to guiding the con-
struction. Therefore, the construction process should be further visual-
ized on the basis of FE calculation. The tooling configuration, action
position and specific process parameters of each construction step are
clarified. The process operation in the process of structural demolition
often relies on empirical knowledge and subjective judgment, or needs
to manually check the existing construction standards or specifications.
Therefore, the current technical methods lead to the low level of con-
struction management, which seriously affects the efficiency and accu-
racy of demolition construction.
Under the background of the rapid development of the new gener-
ation of information technology, intelligent construction is reshaping
the mode of MPs analysis and construction scheme control [17].
Through the cross-integration of new generation information technolo-
gies such as digital twin (DT), artificial intelligence, building informa-
tion modeling (BIM), and Internet of Things, solving various problems in
engineering construction has become the focus of realizing the intelli-
gence of the construction industry [18–20].
DT is a technology that makes full use of models and their data to
achieve real-time synchronization, virtual-real mapping, and digital-
analog linkage [21]. As an artificial intelligence method that can learn
the deep features of data, deep learning can directly extract high-level
features from the original data for perceptual decision-making and
efficiently process state data [22]. The combination of sample data and
deep learning, that is, the combination of knowledge segmentation
method and data-driven deep learning, is conducive to accurate acqui-
sition of optimized construction schemes [23,24].
1.2. Research significance and article structure
The current research results for the analysis of cable structure de-
molition are relatively scattered. Many scholars pay attention to the
collapse resistance of cable structures after members failure [25]. There
is a lack of systematic and unified modeling simulation and application
method system. The following questions need to be further answered for
the transformation and demolition of the cable structures. (1) How to
quickly establish a simulation model in the information space to realize
the unified expression and virtual reconstruction of the actual demoli-
tion process of the cable structure. (2) How to establish a suitable
simulation mechanism and simulation strategy to drive the virtual
model to accurately simulate and analyze the actual demolition
behavior. (3) How to integrate the virtual and real data of virtual space
and physical space to realize the closed-loop control of the scheme
optimization and structural safety state of the demolition execution
process in virtual space. (4) Deep learning algorithms can usually
establish regression prediction models, and there is a lack of research on
the optimal selection of results.
Aiming at the demolition process of cable structure, this study pro-
poses a DT evolution model-driven optimization method for cable
structure demolition. Firstly, the current research status of cable struc-

investigated, and the support technology for cable structure construc-

oped to improve the sharing efficiency of construction information and
realize the efficient guidance of the demolition process. The main con-
tributions of this study are as follows:
(1) The DT process of cable structure demolition is clarified, and the
unified expression and virtual reconstruction of the construction

is established, which significantly improves the level of con-

2. Literature review
This study focuses on the scheme optimization and construction
guidance of the demolition process of cable structure. According to the
research background and the key problems to be addressed, this section
conducts literature research from three aspects: cable structure demo-
lition, construction scheme optimization and DT. On the one hand, the
key technologies and shortcomings of the current cable structure de-
molition are obtained. On the other hand, based on scheme optimization
and DT, the method to optimize the cable structure demolition scheme
and guide the construction efficiently is found.
2.1. Demolition of cable structure
With the development of urbanization, some completed buildings
need to be demolished due to insufficient function and expiration of
service life [26]. In the process of removing the cable structure, many
scholars have analyzed the influence of the removal of different types of
cable members on the MPs of the structure. Yang et al. [27] studied the
demolition method of spoke cable-strut tension structure. The effec-
tiveness of the proposed new dismantling method is verified by exper-
iments, and the influence of the dismantling of the struts on the MPs of
the cable is obtained. Liu et al. [28] proposed a new analysis method for
the influence of component failure on the MPs of spoke cable truss
structures. This study accurately obtained the internal force redistribu-
tion and reliability of the structure after the failure of the radial cable
and the circumferential cable. Zhao et al. [29] studied the MPs of
suspend-dome after cable fracture. The study obtained the cable
breaking path and the collapse resistance of the remaining structure. Liu
et al. [30] established the calculation formula of progressive collapse
bearing capacity and deflection of truss string structure under the con-
dition of cable fracture. This study accurately and effectively evaluates
the MPs of the structure after component removal, and greatly improves
the computational efficiency. Chen et al. [31] analyzed the collapse
resistance of double-layer grid structure under different demolition load
schemes. Through FE calculation and experimental verification, the in-
ternal force redistribution law of the structure is obtained.
For the demolition analysis of cable structures, scholars have focused
on the influence of component failure and cable fracture on the MPs of
structures. According to the technical regulations of cable structure
[32], in the normal use stage and the demolition construction process,
the structure should meet the stress requirements and ensure the reli-
ability of the structure. During the service period of the structure, the
cable stress does not exceed 0.4 times of the ultimate tensile strength.
The maximum displacement of the nodes should not exceed 1/250 of the
structural span. In the process of dismantling the structure, the stable
development of the cable force should be ensured, and the gap between
the cable forces should not be too large. However, there are few studies
on how to solve the problem of high safety risk and low efficiency in the
demolition process due to the cumbersome structure and difficult co-
ordination. The traditional FE analysis method needs to modify the
parameters such as boundary conditions, which leads to a certain lag in
the information interaction of the demolition process, which is not
conducive to engineering application. In addition, the FE analysis
method cannot realize the continuous dynamic analysis of the con-
struction process. Due to the lack of analysis of the mapping relationship
between each construction scheme and performance parameters, the
effective optimization of the construction scheme cannot be realized.
Therefore, it is urgent to establish an effective optimization method for
the demolition process scheme, so as to realize the efficient resource
scheduling of the whole demolition process and ensure the reasonable
force of the structure.
2.2. Construction scheme optimization method
The optimization of the construction scheme can not only ensure the
safety performance of the structure, but also save the cost. Many
scholars have applied artificial intelligence algorithms to obtain the site
layout and process planning of the construction process. Wu et al. [33]
proposed a space-time planning model for tower cranes, which signifi-
cantly saves the cost of construction. El-Abbasy et al. [34] developed an
automated system that can optimize different construction management
scheduling problems. Chen et al. [35] established a construction scheme
optimization model based on genetic algorithm, which effectively
reduced the probability of rework. Tomczak et al. [36] used PSO-BPNN
to coordinate multiple processes in the construction process, effectively
shortening the construction period of the project. Based on PSO-BPNN,
Ma et al. [37] optimized the construction quality, which effectively
reduced the construction error.
Most of the current research on construction scheme optimization
focuses on construction path planning and site layout. There is a strong
process correlation in the construction process of cable structure. Due to
the interaction between multiple construction steps, few studies have
comprehensively considered the correlation and mechanical properties
to optimize the construction scheme. In this study, the optimal demo-
lition scheme of the structure is obtained by establishing the mapping
relationship between the demolition scheme and the MPs. In addition,
deep learning algorithms can usually establish regression prediction
models, and there is a lack of research on the optimal selection of results.
In this study, the activation function is introduced to improve the PSO-
BP neural network to establish the optimization model of the demolition
scheme. Based on neural network for data processing, the self-renewal of

2.3. DT
In the construction industry, the application of DT has greatly
improved the visualization and efficiency of construction management
[38] . DT can realize the integration of modeling technology and data
processing technology [39] . Jiang et al. [40] constructed the overall
architecture of the construction management and control system driven
by BIM and DT. In the construction process, the stress and operation
state of the structure were timely understood. Ritto et al. [41] con-
structed an intelligent analysis model based on DT for structural damage
identification. The combination of this model and machine learning
provided a reliable reference for related engineering decisions. Li et al.
[42] proposed a DT -driven virtual sensor architecture for the con-
struction safety of trailing suction hopper dredgers. In the virtual model,
the operation indexes of mechanical equipment were predicted and
evaluated. The virtual sensor assisted the physical sensor to diagnose
accurately and timely, and further warned of potential mechanical
faults. Teizer et al. [43] outlined the concept of construction safety DT
(DTCS) and defined three basic steps in the DTCS workflow. DTCS
should be considered as a system-based approach to improve the overall
safety performance of the construction process. Wang et al. [44] pro-
posed an interactive and immersive process-level DT system in virtual
reality, which integrated visualization and supervision, task planning
and execution, and two-way communication to achieve human-machine
collaborative construction. Zhang et al. [45] used DT to expand the
existing level of detail of the BIM for construction site management. The
proposed framework can support construction site monitoring and
management, improve quality and efficiency, and improve construction
safety.
DT provides model support for dynamic simulation and data
extraction of the whole process of demolition. Driven by DT, this study
establishes a virtual model of the actual demolition process to realize
dynamic simulation and optimal control of the physical space. In addi-
tion, the virtual-real mapping and construction scheme optimization are
integrated to develop a DT control platform to efficiently guide the
whole construction process.
3. DT evolution process of cable structure demolition
In the process of dismantling the cable structure, the MPs are used as
the index to judge the feasibility of the scheme [46] . According to the
characteristics of virtual and real interaction of DT, the twin evolution
modeling methods of physical process virtualization and demolition
process are analyzed. Taking the actual demolition as the core, the visual
model, simulation model and data model are established. The optimal
scheme is obtained by systematically analyzing the demolition execu-
tion process. Based on the analysis of theory and method, the DT plat-
form is developed to guide the demolition process efficiently and
accurately. The DT optimization and control framework for structural
demolition is shown in Fig. 1 .
3.1. Virtualization of physical demolition process
Fig. 2 describes the physical space and virtual space of the demoli-
tion process. The demolition process of cable structure is a hierarchical
transformation system from component unit, equipment unit to the
whole structure. As shown in Fig. 2 (a), the structural member units and
equipment units are classified as field control layers. The specific de-
molition process acts on a specific component unit by assigning con-
struction information to the equipment unit. In the process of
demolition, the MPs of the structure are the key indicators to judge the
rationality of construction [47] . In the twin system, on the one hand, the
measured data of the site are collected, and on the other hand, the
simulation data are compared to ensure the construction accuracy [48] .
For the whole structural system, the stress state of the components in
each demolition stage is optimized. Therefore, in the actual demolition
process, unit level, system level and structure level are divided from
micro to macro. In order to facilitate the expression of virtual space, the
field control layer, data acquisition and monitoring layer and optimi-
zation layer are defined from three levels. As shown in Fig. 2 (b), three
axes are divided based on the three requirements of DT: presentation,
mapping and optimization. The three axes describe the physical space
from three dimensions: visual model, simulation model and data model.
From the axial point of view, three different dimensions realize the
mapping of all levels of physical space. Concomitantly, different scales
are divided within each dimension to form three closed loops. For
example, starting from a single axis, the visualization of objects, in-
dicators and processes characterizes the control layer of the site and acts
on the specific demolition component unit. From the perspective of the
ring, taking the basic component unit as an example, the component
modeling is carried out in the visual model. The MPs of the basic units in
the simulation model are analyzed by assigning physical properties to
the components. The component parameters are extracted and the stress

(1) Visual modeling.
Through the visualization model, a virtual space that is highly

levels: object visualization, index visualization and process visualiza-
tion. Among them, the visualization of objects and indicators describes
the static scene of virtual space, and the visualization of process con-

1) Object visualization. In order to realize the accurate characterization
of structural components and demolition equipment, the visualiza-

and spatial location of components and devices. The operation at-

demolition process and the operation process of the equipment.
2) Indicator visualization. The MPs of the structure are the key in-
dicators of demolition control. By analyzing the stress state of each
component during the demolition process, the process attributes are
expressed in a visual form.
3) Process visualization. By using three-dimensional animation tech-

site layout, component demolition process and equipment transfer
process is realized.

The simulation model includes the modeling and analysis of the basic

1) Basic unit modeling. In the simulation model, the FE model of the

2) MPs analysis. On the basis of structural modeling, the stress state of

3.2. DT evolution of demolition process
Based on the virtual modeling of the physical demolition process, the
twin model simulation, analysis, optimization and control are carried
out. The DT evolution model is the engine that drives this process (as
shown in Fig. 3 ). The DT evolution model includes four aspects: basic
model, correlation relationship, fusion process and evolution process.
The basic model includes physical entity (actual demolition) and virtual
model (visual model, simulation model and data model). Physical entity
is the object of modeling, analysis, optimization and control. The three
sub-models in the virtual model realize the mapping of physical entities
from different dimensions such as visual presentation, simulation, and
optimization decision. The evolution process divides the application
process of DT into three progressive stages: digital model, digital pro-
jection and twin mapping. In the spatial dimension, the relationship
represents the mapping relationship between the virtual model and the
physical entity. The fusion process describes the typical applications
between virtual models and physical entities in different evolution
stages from the application dimension.
By integrating physical entities, visual models and simulation
models, a digital model space can be constructed and applied to the
simulation analysis before demolition and the verification of construc-
tion schemes. Based on the digital model, the integrated data model can
construct the digital projection space, and realize the automatic
collection of physical space construction information and the moni-
toring and control in the process. On the basis of digital model space and
digital projection space, the demolition scheme is optimized based on
historical and real-time data, and the feasibility and scientificity of de-
molition are continuously improved. Through the integrated simulation
and visualization model, the optimization results are directly fed back to
the actual demolition process.
The DT evolution model of the demolition process ensures the ac-
curacy and efficiency of the actual construction process. From the time
dimension, the model describes the closed-loop process from the un-
derstanding of the demolition process (digital model), to the perception
of the construction process (digital projection), to the optimization of
demolition behavior based on data simulation and mining (twin map-
ping). From the perspective of spatial dimension, the model character-
izes the fusion process of physical space and virtual space from relative
separation, one-way interaction to two-way interaction. From the
perspective of application dimension, the model depicts the whole
application process of actual demolition from simulation planning to
real-time monitoring and then to optimal control.

5. Case study
In order to verify the feasibility of the proposed theoretical method, a
DT platform is developed to guide the demolition process based on the
cable truss experimental model. The visualization model, simulation
model and data model are integrated in the twin platform. The opti-
mized demolition scheme is output in the platform, which accurately
and effectively guides the whole construction process.
5.1. Experimental design
The cable truss span of the experimental model is 6 m, which is
composed of 12 cable trusses and inner ring beams, flying columns,
outer ring beams and outer columns. The experimental model and visual
model are shown in Fig. 7 . The cable adopts the sealing cable with
tensile strength of 1670 MPa, The elastic modulus is 2.05 × 10
11
Pa. And
other components adopt Q345B steel. In the analysis of the construction
process of the structure, the cable member adopts the linear elastic
material model, and the steel member adopts the elastic-plastic material
model [54] . According to previous study [55] , in the design process of
the experimental model, the influence of cable size, initial tension and
other factors on the internal force and configuration of the structure is
mainly investigated. Through parameter analysis and optimization se-
lection, the best design parameters are obtained to ensure the rationality
of the structural force. Moreover, in Reference [56] , the mechanical
response analysis of various static and dynamic loads is carried out,
which meets the requirements of structural performance. The radial
cable is divided into two layers, namely the upper and lower radial cable
(URC, LRC). The diameter of the radial cable is 9 mm, and the
morphological cable forces of the URC and LRC are 15 kN and 16 kN,
respectively. The URC and LRC are connected by two struts. The inner
ring beam includes the upper ring beam and the lower ring beam, which
are connected by the flying column. The height of the flying column is
0.75 m, and the radius of the inner ring beam is 0.5 m. The height of the
outer column is 1.5 m.
According to the characteristics of the experimental model, the de-
molition process is divided into two steps: the removal of the counter-
weight block and the relaxation of the cable. In the process of removing
the counterweight block, the cable force of each radial cable is obtained
according to the load of each node of each cable truss. In the process of
removing the counterweight block, the change rate of cable force will
affect the deformation of the structure, and there is the safety risk. If the
cable force gap is too large, it will lead to the dislocation of the com-
ponents and lead to serious safety accidents [57] . The best relaxation
scheme is obtained by taking the change rate of cable force in each
relaxation stage and the cable force gap between each cable as the
evaluation index. When the weight block is removed and the cable
relaxation is completed, the demolition process of the structure is
completed. The arrangement of outer ring beams and columns in the
structure is completely symmetrical, and only bears the action of grav-
ity. Therefore, the demolition scheme of the two types of components
was not optimized in the experiment.
