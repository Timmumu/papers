ABSTRACT 
Accidents tend to happen during tunnel construction due to the harsh environmental and geological conditions. 
Towards high safety risk during tunnel construction, a digital-twin based multi-information intelligent early 
warning and safety management platform is proposed in this study. At first, a communication line is built inside 
tunnel through multifunctional base stations. Multi-source information from tunnel construction could be 
collected and transmitted automatically. Then, dynamic tunnel construction information and surrounding rock 
conditions are virtual simulated. Meanwhile, four-level early warning and emergency response plan are built to 
provide judgement on the construction status. At last, application effects of the platform are validated in a deep 
buried tunnel project, Dongtianshan Tunnel in China, which has extreme weather, harmful gas, and bad 
geological conditions. A collapse accident of tunnel excavation face was successfully predicted by the platform. 
Early warning and appropriate emergency measures were taken in time, and the personnel safety was ensured. 
1. Introduction 
With the increase of tunnel numbers and capital investment, more 
deep-buried and extra-long tunnels were constructed ( Ou et al., 2021 ). 
However, due to the bad construction environment ( Zheng et al., 2012 ), 
complex geological conditions ( Xu et al., 2020 ), and inadequate con -
struction organization and management ( Zhou and Ding, 2017 ), tunnel 
construction accidents happen frequently ( Ding and Zhou, 2013; Zhou 
et al., 2015 ). For example, a water inrush accident occurred during 
tunnel construction with 14 persons dead, as shown in Fig. 1 a. A gas 
explosion accident occurred during the railway tunnel construction, 
resulted in 12 deaths and 12 injured in the main tunnel, as shown in 
Fig. 1 b. 
To reduce the occurrence of construction accidents and avoid the 
casualties and property losses during tunnel construction, early warning 
is proposed and utilized in the safety management of underground en -
gineering project ( Lan et al., 2017; Salzano et al., 2009; Xing et al., 
2019 ). Development of the technique of tunnel construction safety early 
warning mainly depends on two aspects: (i) monitoring means for the 
real-time safety information acquisition; (ii) information technology for 
dynamic multi-information transmission and fusion. 
With the development of monitoring technique, the traditional 
manual monitoring and management methods are no longer suitable for 
the study of construction safety of underground engineering, instead, 
more technological means are involved ( Xing et al., 2019; Jiang et al., 
2022; Chen et al., 2020 ). Various monitoring means ( Li et al., 2021; 
Wang et al., 2020 ), such as total stations ( Zhou et al., 2020a ), 
three-dimensional laser scanning ( Jiang et al., 2021a ), light detection 
and ranging (LiDAR) ( Han et al., 2013a, 2013b ), photogrammetry 
( Attard et al., 2018 ) are widely used for tunnel construction monitoring. 
However, as the tunnel construction information is diversified, variable 
and complicated, a single index is not enough for decision makers to use 
as the basis of judgment ( Ding and Zhou, 2013; Liu et al., 2018; Fan and 
Zhou, 2021 ). 
Meanwhile, the rapid development of information technology based 
on the internet allows for various possibilities in different aspects of

in civil engineering due to its smarter and forward idea in the connection 
of physical and virtual part ( Zhang et al., 2021a; Li et al., 2018 ). As an 
important part of realization of IoT, wireless sensor networks (WSNs) 
are employed in underground engineering to enhance safety and pro -

Based on this, through combination of information technologies and 
tunnel construction monitoring means, the intelligent early warning

projects. Applications including excavations adjacent to the existing 
metro tunnels ( Tian et al., 2021 ), prevention of risk of water and mud

The above applications promoted the progress of tunnel construction 
safety early warning system, but there are still some limitations. First, 
realization of multi-source information collection and transmission 
during tunnel construction is still a problem, especially under the harsh 
working environment and large buried depth ( Zhou et al., 2015, 2020b; 
Salzano et al., 2009; Liu et al., 2020a; Ding et al., 2014; Zhang et al., 
2021b; Pal and Hsieh, 2021 ). Second, most of studies are only focused on 
one aspect of the construction safety risks, and lack of combination with 
emergency plans and measures ( Ding et al., 2013 ). Third, the entire 
tunnel construction is a dynamic, complex and long process, safety ac -
cidents happen randomly, but the current researches on dynamic virtual 
simulation of tunnel construction process are relatively deficient ( Pal 
and Hsieh, 2021; Golovina et al., 2021 ). In addition, the term of “ Digi -
tal-twin ” (DT) has emerged in civil engineering ( Jiang et al., 2021b ). In 
DT, smart construction can be promoted and brought into the virtual 
world, and management can be conducted virtually at a low cost ( Tuegel 
et al., 2011; Pan and Zhang, 2021; Boje et al., 2020; Schleich et al., 2017; 
Tao et al., 2019 ). However, the application of DT in the field of con -
struction safety monitoring and management is still in its infancy ( Jiang 
et al., 2021b; Boje et al., 2020 ), especially for tunnel construction safety 
early warning. 
To fill this gap, the specific objectives of this research are as follows: 
(i) To propose a timely and effective feedback scheme for multi- 
dimensional dynamic construction information of tunnels under com -
plex geological conditions; (ii) To solve the problem of risk monitoring 
and early warning lag in the harsh working environment of tunnel 
construction site; (iii) To explore the application effect of the safety 
management platform which combines the DT concept with the early 
warning system in tunnel construction. 
Therefore, a DT-based multi-information intelligent early warning 
and safety management platform is proposed for tunnel construction to 
reduce the construction risks and avoid accident during tunnel con -
struction in this study. The main contributions of this work are as fol -
lows: (i) Based on the combination of base stations with different 
functions, a multi-source dynamic information collection and trans -
mission line for tunnel construction is constructed; (ii) The integration 
and bidirectional interoperation on a real-time basis between physical 
construction and digital construction in tunnel scenario is realized; (iii) 
A platform with four level early warning and emergency response plan, 
and remote interaction services for tunnel construction safety manage -
ment in site is developed. The platform combines system, personnel, 
environment, and emergency plan to meet the dynamic design re -
quirements of tunnel construction and ensure the safety of underground 
engineering construction. The DT-based platform was validated and 
verified in the project of Dongtianshan Tunnel in China. A collapse ac -
cident of tunnel excavation face was successfully predicted by the 
platform. 
The rest of the paper is structured as follows. Related works on tunnel 
construction uncertainties, early warning and digital twin ’ s potential 
use in the tunnel construction safety management are provided in Sec -
tion 2 . The architecture of a DT based multi-information intelligent early 
warning and safety management platform is described in Section 3 . A 
case study in a practical tunnel construction project is performed in 
Section 4 . Discussions are presented in Section 5 . Conclusions and future 
works are outlined in Section 6 . 
2. Related works 
2.1. Tunnel construction uncertainties 
The expansion of underground space accelerated the development in 
tunnel engineering, particularly in China. Nevertheless, due to complex 
geographical environments, varied geological conditions, and incom -
plete safety management system during tunnel construction, which 
brings great uncertainty to the safety of tunnel construction ( Sousa and 
Einstein, 2021; Ding et al., 2012; Guo and Zhang, 2021 ), frequent tunnel 
construction accidents can be caused ( Zhou et al., 2022 ). To reduce and 
avoid tunnel construction safety accident, sufficient attention should be 
paid to the potential uncertainty in tunnel construction. The imprecision 
and randomness of tunnel construction is mainly featured in following 
aspects. 
(i) In the aspect of human behavior and machinery, construction 
worker ’ s physical quality and technical level can directly affect the 
construction safety ( Ou et al., 2021; Zhou and Ding, 2017 ). Accident

records show that most accidents in civil engineering are caused by 
human faults. Human errors have repeated patterns and usually include 
lax management, poor technology, and improper handling of hazards. 
As for mechanical tunnel excavation, Liu et al. (2018) found that 30% of 
accidents are caused by human errors, while only 10% of the accidents 
could be completely attributed solely to natural causes, and 60% of 
accidents are resulted from the combination of human mistakes and 
natural disaster. 
(ii) In the aspect of project management, it is reported that man -
agement and control errors are the causes of the accident (Zhou et al., 
2015, 2020b; Ding et al., 2014), which including: inexperienced and 
improper management, inadequate emergency response measures, poor 
supervision of construction work, wrong construction method, errors in 
the collection and processing of monitoring data. Effective safety man -
agement can prevent the occurrence of accidents (Zhou et al., 2015). 
The International Tunneling Association (ITA) has published Guidelines 
for Tunneling Risk Management in 2004 (Ding et al., 2012). Meanwhile, 
a variety of evaluation methods have been proposed by many scholars 
(Ou et al., 2021; Zhou et al., 2020b), such as the fault tree method, 
artificial neural network methods, the method of fuzzy comprehensive 
evaluation, Bayesian network, and the multi-source information fusion, 
etc. 
(iii) In the aspect of construction environment, the construction 
environment inside the tunnel is complex and harsh. The dim light and 
narrow space are not conducive to the workers and construction ma -
chinery activities (Zheng et al., 2012; Zhou and Ding, 2017; Wang et al., 
2021a). The construction site ventilation is not smooth, the accumula -
tion of harmful gases and dust is constantly threated the health of 
workers, and even cause adverse physical reactions. Noise from drilling 
holes and construction vehicles can lead to poor communication be -
tween workers in site, and sustained high intensity noise can also cause 
fatigue, resulting in low efficiency and high error rate. In addition, Sousa 
and Einstein (2021) proposed that unexpected ground conditions are 
often the main causes of accidents in tunnel construction, and classified 
the accident into rockfall, collapse, daylight collapse, flooding, spalling, 
excessive deformation, and collapse in specific locations. 
Despite great efforts have made to avoid construction accidents, 
effective safety management remains a challenging task because of the 
significant uncertainties during the construction. For instance, Bjelland 
and Aven (2013) introduced the treatment of uncertainty in risk as -
sessments in the Rogfast road tunnel project, and concluded that a much 
broader risk assessments of the uncertainties in the construction process 
is required. Zhu et al. (2022) statistically analyzed major 48 cases of 
tunnel construction accidents in China from 2010 to 2020, and pointed 
out that more than 60% of accidents are directly related to insufficient 
prospecting, poor monitoring, and lack of rescue planning. Therefore, it 
is very important to control multi-dynamic construction information 
comprehensively and timely, and formulate scientific construction early 
warning and response scheme for tunnel construction safety. 
2.2. Early warning and its applications 
Early warning system (EWS) is a warning system to mitigate the 
risks, which is composed of information communication systems and 
sensors, event detection and decision subsystems (Waidyanatha, 2010). 
In 1868, EWS was first used for earthquake disaster prevention in San 
Francisco (Nakamura et al., 2011). With the development of society, 
EWS has been gradually applied in the fields of military defense, eco -
nomic, natural disasters and diseases, climate. Multiple sources of in -
formation are the foundation of building EWS. Lan et al. (2017) 
proposed that “multisource data are collected from multiple sources to 
meet one common surveillance purpose” in the summarization of 
development of early warning models. Yager (2016) claims out that “the 
early warning process is essentially a multi-source information fusion 
process, which conducts information collection, processing, screening, 
extraction and synthesis on the available data and then the judgment 
and decision could be made from the fused information”. Wang et al. 
(2021b) established a multi-factor comprehensive early warning iden -
tification model for real-time monitoring and early warning of mine 
water inrush. 
Nowadays, numerous scholars have contributed in-depth studies on 
the EWS in the tunnelling engineering field. The traditional tunnel 
monitoring mainly relies on manual, and the prediction and early 
warning effect of tunnel accidents is relatively poor (Liu et al., 2020a). 
For the monitoring and early warning of the complex underground 
working area, on the one hand, intelligent monitoring system is estab -
lished through sensors and wireless transmission. For instance, Wei 
Tian, et al (Tian et al., 2021). utilized a backpropagation neural network 
to predict the real-time deformation of the tunnel based on monitoring 
data from the adjacent construction site. Wang et al. (2021a) studied an 
improved method for the accurate measurement of sensors and wireless 
transmission bands suitable for complex tunnel environments, and 
proposed a real-time monitoring system for water inrush in tunnels. Liu 
et al. (2020a) designed an innovative intelligent tunnel monitoring 
system based on micro-service architecture and applied in the Anping 
tunnel. Zhang et al. (2021b) reviewed early warning methods about 
rockbursts in tunnel engineering based on microseismic monitoring. 
On the other hand, real-time interaction and safety management are 
realized by wireless communication technology. Ding et al. (2013) 
presented a real-time safety early warning system to prevent accidents 
and improve safety management in underground construction, based on 
the IoT technology. Koch et al. (2017) presented a tunnel information 
modelling framework to support management. Xie et al. (2018) pro -
posed a platform with timely auto-sending of monitoring data for set -
tlement control during shield tunnelling construction. Jo and Khan 
(2017) introduced a real-time monitoring, event-reporting and 
early-warning platform based on the IoT for underground coal mines. 
However, most of those studies are only focused on the recognition of 
potential specific risk, or identification of one specific aspect of the 
tunnel construction potential safety hazards, lack of and combination 
with emergency plans and measures (Ding et al., 2013). Considering 
entire tunnel construction project as a complex and dynamic process, 
information of tunnel construction is hard to completely controlled due 
to the signal shielding inside tunnel especially for large buried depth 
tunnel. How to construct information network and realize the trans -
mission and integration of multi-information in tunnel construction is 
still a problem that needs to be solved at present. 
2.3. Application of DT in tunnel construction 
Nowadays, many scholars have defined and discussed the concept of 
DT (Tuegel et al., 2011; Pan and Zhang, 2021; Boje et al., 2020; Schleich 
et al., 2017; Hou et al., 2020). Akanmu et al. (2014) pointed out that the 
nature of DT is to connect a physical artefact to its digital counterpart. 
Tao et al. (2019) proposed the idea that “DT must consist of five parts: 
physical part, virtual part, connections, data, and services”. Specifically, 
the physical part is the foundation of the virtual part, and mirrored by 
the virtual part; data and information could be transferred by the 
connection part; simulation, decision making, monitoring and control of 
the physical object are provided by the services part of DT; data drives 
the services part to enhance the entire system. Jiang et al. (2021b) 
summarized the DT definition and its constituents in civil engineering. 
At present, with the development of IoT, the concept of DT is applied 
in construction, operation and maintenance of the project. At the project 
construction stage, through application of DT in construction sites, 
construction information including process, machinery and materials, 
workers, quality and safety can be controlled and managed in real-time 
(Boje et al., 2020; Hou et al., 2020). For example, Pan and Zhang (2021) 
proposed an integrated digital twin framework for project management. 
Lei Hou, et al (Hou et al., 2020). summarized that although DT tech -
nology has been proven to be effective in improving construction worker 
safety, the construction industry still needs to take full advantage of

3.2. Physical layer 
3.2.1. Multi-information of tunnel construction 
Tunnel construction is a long and complicated process. Multi- 
information of tunnel construction should be controlled by manager in 
time to avoid safety accidents, as shown in Fig. 3 . 
3.2.2. Real-time data acquisition 
To effectively obtain construction site information, real-time data 
acquisition is shown in Fig. 4 . Several base stations with different 
functions are connected with the gateway to control and manage ter -
minal equipment. Signal base station is responsible for commends 
sending, warning light control, voice commands and information cue. 
Node base station is used for positioning the workers and mechanical 
devices. Transmission and controlling base station carries out the uplink 
and downlink control of data and instructions. Collection base station is 
responsible for the collection and transmission of multi-information 
during tunnel construction. Various sensors, including safety sensors, 
environmental sensors, construction sensors, auxiliary sensors, are 
connected with collection base stations, so that real-time information 
about voice, video, image and data of the construction site could be 
gathered. Positioning and warning card is carried by workers at the 
construction site, which consists of induction, positioning and text 
message module. Personnel information could be identified and sent 
back to the service center as the person enters the base station detection 
range with the card, and distribution map and lists could be generated 
by the software, so that workers and machines position information 
could be directly seen by manager. 
Layout of hardware platform in the construction site is shown in 
Fig. 5 . The sensors and cameras are installed on the tunnel construction 
site, the laser instruments are arranged on the cross sections, the 
personnel cards are worn by the constructors, and all the devices are 
connected by wireless technology. The collected information is sent to 
the wireless base station with GPRS network through the wireless 
transmission link, and then the signal is transmitted to the monitoring 
center. A GPRS wireless transmission base station is erected at the tunnel 
portal, so that all the monitoring information can be sent to the moni -
toring center. Appropriate sensors are selected and installed according 
to the specific conditions of tunnel construction. All the sensors are 
uniformly controlled by the background server. Continuous power is 
supplied and sufficient battery capacity is provided to ensure 24 h 
normal operation of the system. Therefore, the management personnel 
can know about the tunnel safety and construction conditions anywhere 
through the Internet.

3.3. Digital layer 
3.3.1. Tunnel construction digital twins 
Tunnel construction virtual model is constructed based on internet 
network equipment and real-time information collection. In order to 
make construction workers and managers operate conveniently and 
easily, Browser/Server (B/S) mode is selected (Ding et al., 2013). Pro -
cess of DT model is constructed upon the real-time data on the con -
struction site, as shown in Fig. 7a. Based on the characteristics of tunnel 
structure, tunnel engineering design scheme and structural parameters, 
tunnel simulation model, surrounding rock and environment model 
were established by Open Graphics Library (Open GL) technology, and 
tunnel investigation and design information, excavation, and lining 
construction process information were input and recorded. Digital layer 
of the platform is shown in Fig. 7b. Data visualization and imple -
mentation of alerts are important functions of the platform. Different 
with previous studies (Fan and Zhou, 2021; Tian et al., 2021; Ding et al., 
2013; Liu et al., 2020a; Koch et al., 2017; Xie et al., 2018; Jo and Khan, 
2017; Ren et al., 2021), the realization of intelligent early warning is a 
comprehensive process. By integrating multiple monitoring data, 
real-time updated advanced geological prediction information, con -
struction progress and other information, the platform can not only 
realize the dynamic interaction between managers and workers, but also 
provide the basis for users to make comprehensive decisions. 
In the scene prediction before the construction, as shown in Fig. 7b. 
At first, according to the feedback and update results of tunnel advanced 
geological prediction that collected in the platform, and combined with 
the current construction conditions of the tunnel, the layout position of 
monitoring sensors is determined. Then, the values of monitoring points 
are assigned in advance in light of the relevant specifications, structural 
numerical analysis results and expert experience. When the monitoring 
value exceeds the value assigned by the platform, early warning is 
started automatically, the corresponding emergency plan is triggered, 
and relevant manager and workers in site is informed.

Moreover, the data of the monitoring points could be viewed by 
opening the data display window at the mileage position of the moni -

the corresponding response plan and related disposal measures could be 
released manually or automatically by computer. In addition, the in -
dicators, whistle, personnel card prompt and SMS prompt of the base 
station at the construction site are associated with the four-level early 
warning system to provide the early warning and responses inside and 
outside the tunnel. 
3.4. Service layer 
3.4.1. Four-level automatic early warning service 
To facilitate the users to judge the safety level of tunnel construction

level. Based on the real-time monitoring value that transmitted from the 
construction site, the cumulative value and the change rate of moni -
toring point are both selected as control indexes. The warning level is 
determined by the color of those double control index. A single moni -
toring point may contain multiple aspects of tunnel construction

between the two inclined shafts is 4.2 km. 
The project site is located in a high-altitude area with complicated 
topographic, geological, hydrological and meteorological conditions, 
and high seismic intensity, where winters are cold, windy and snowy.

4.3. Application effects 
4.3.1. Harmful gas monitoring near the working face 
Due to the long tunneling distance and the long fault crossing dis -
tance, it is very easy to produce harmful gas near the working face of the 
tunnel. Therefore, in the construction stage, the harmful gas in the 
tunnel environment was monitored in real time to judge the hazard of 
harmful gas to the constructors. The automatic monitoring data of car -
bon monoxide concentration and methane concentration on the left line 
of the tunnel, as shown in Fig. 14 , indicated that the concentration of 
harmful gas increased during the construction stage, and sometimes 
even reached the blue and yellow warning value.

4.3.4. Safety risk early warning and management during construction 
During the tunnel construction in the active fault F2, the platform 
launched a red early warning once. The tunnel face was then located at 
K0 + 668, as shown in Fig. 18 . The middle and lower part of the tunnel 
face was mainly composed of the strongly-weathered tuffaceous sand -
stone, where the surrounding rock was very broken, the joint fissures 
were well developed, and the rock mass was mostly fracture. The fissure 
water ran out of the tunnel face in strands. The surrounding rock was not 
stable with weak bonding force between strata. The upper part of the 
tunnel face was mainly composed of the fully-weathered tuffaceous 
siltstone in mud shape, with water seepage on the surface and extremely 
unstable surrounding rock. 
Due to high tunnel construction risks, the geological prediction and 
monitoring of surrounding rock deformation was strengthened in the 
construction site. The construction safety emergency and response plan 
were preset in the platform before the construction. Process of the 
response was shown in Fig. 19 . At first, according to the information on 
advanced geological prediction, which includes the geological logging 
of the tunnel face, the tunnel seismic prediction and the exploration data 
of advanced horizontal drilling, in the 30 m section from K0 + 668 to 
K0 + 698, the surrounding rock was seriously broken, rich in water, and 
likely to collapse and have water inrush, so that the great attention 
should be paid. Then, based on real-time information of surrounding 
rock deformation, the platform gave out an early warning and prediction 
in time for the possible collapse during excavation. Meanwhile, an 
emergency plan was automatically launched by the platform to evacuate 
the workers on site in case the collapse and water inrush occurs. Finally, 
multiple messages receiving functions were provided through SMS, 
mobile client and web pages to build the connection between workers 
and managers, so as to realize the early detection and early treatment of 
safety abnormalities and alarms, prevent disasters, and reduce 
accidents. 
The tunnel face collapse was recorded by the remote video camera, 
lasting about only 1 – 2 min, as shown in Fig. 20 . However, due to the 
timely warning and the appropriate measures taken, no casualty was 
caused in the collapse. Subsequently, with reference to the geological 
conditions in construction at the fault, the contractor developed the 
relevant construction measures to prevent collapse, such as reserving the 
core soil on the tunnel face, lengthening the advanced small pipe 
appropriately and strengthening the grouting. In addition, the lining 
structure was reinforced to control the deformation strictly. Because of 
the real-time monitoring, feedback and early warning of the system 
during tunnel construction, no accident occurred during the whole 
process of tunnel construction. 
According to the application effect of the platform in the collapse 
accident safety management during construction, the main contribution 
of the platform includes: (i) an orderly and timely communication line 
inside and outside the deep buried depth tunnel is established; (ii) the 
construction scene of digital twin tunnel is constructed, and early 
warning and emergency plan services are provided. The application of 
the platform gives a useful reference for the construction and safety 
management of tunnel engineering. 
5. Discussions 
Construction safety early warning is a necessary task for the entire 
tunnel construction management. Compared with traditional means and 
previous studies, the merits of the platform in this paper are summarized 
as follows: (i) Through the integration of different functional base sta -
tions and multi-source data, the effective transmission and collection of 
tunnel dynamic construction data are realized. (ii) Compared with 
previous studies in the construction safety early warning ( Fan and Zhou, 
2021; Tian et al., 2021; Ding et al., 2013; Liu et al., 2020a; Koch et al., 
2017; Xie et al., 2018; Jo and Khan, 2017; Ren et al., 2021 ), the platform 
in this paper provides more services and functions, such as real-time 
image acquisition of tunnel construction face, long-term monitoring of 
entire construction process, early warning and safety management, etc. 
(iii) The construction of the platform is a beneficial attempt based on the 
DT concept. A 3D virtual tunnel model is established by integrating 
tunnel survey, design data and construction dynamic data. The virtual 
tunnel roaming function is developed to realize the virtual dynamic 
tunnel browsing and monitoring, as well as the real-time visualization 
display inside the virtual tunnel. (iv) The platform automatic warning 
classification method is developed, and the alarm threshold and grade 
can be preset by client. When the warning is issued, the location, level 
and other detailed information of the warning are dynamically dis -
played in the platform, and transmitted to the construction site to start 
the corresponding emergency response plan. 
However, there are two limitations in the proposed platform. First, 
the details displayed in the construction process of virtual simulation 
part of the platform need to be strengthened. Detailed display and 
monitoring about worker ’ s behavior and health ( Zhou and Ding, 2017 ), 
and preventing collisions ( Baek and Choi, 2018 ) in the construction site 
are useful to be added in the future. Second, how to analyze potential 
safety risk via various information and prevent accident before the 
construction is also important ( Lan et al., 2017; Wang et al., 2021b ). A 
large numbers of safety information were involved during tunnel con -
struction, effective safety risk analysis based on the engineering geology 
and environment conditions of project before construction should be 
added to the platform. 
6. Conclusions and future work 
Towards high safety risk during tunnel construction, a DT based 
multi-information intelligent early warning and safety management 
platform is proposed in this study. A detailed framework of the DT 
containing physical, virtual, and connection, data, service parts are 
developed and introduced. The conclusions are as follows: 
In the physical layer of the platform, a tunnel construction infor -
mation collection and transmission line based on WSNs is established. 
Several base stations with different functions, including instruction 
sending, warning, positioning the workers and mechanical devices, in -
formation transmission, connected with the gateway to control and 
manage terminal equipment. Various sensors, including safety sensors, 
environmental sensors, construction sensors, auxiliary sensors, inte -
grated and linked with the base stations of the platform. Dynamic con -
struction information about voice, video, image and data of the 
construction site can be fluently transferred inside and outside the 
tunnel. Meanwhile, multi-source information of construction site can be 
automatically sent and received by the platform, including temperature, 
humidity, dust concentration near the excavation face, structural and 
surrounding rock deformation, updated advanced geological prediction.

machines positioning; (iv) safety risk early warning and management 
during construction. Finally, a collapse accident of tunnel excavation
