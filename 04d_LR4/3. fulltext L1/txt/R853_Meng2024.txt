Abstract 
To improve the accuracy of rope force prediction and the safety of the construction process. In this paper, based on digital 
twin technology in the design process, digital twins of physical entities in virtual space are constructed using digital to 
achieve digital control and optimization of physical entities. The structural response information collected by sensors is 
brought into the dynamic load identification method by calculating the solution method of the dynamic load of the digital 
twin technology. The damping effect is considered in the load vector. The displacement is eliminated by constructing an 
average loss function to disperse the motion time to solve for the dynamic load imposed on the structure and obtain the 
load-bearing state of the structure f or dome cable force prediction. To verify the feasibility of the model, the results of 
the cable force prediction analysis show that the overall displacement of the mesh shell displacement nodes at the bishop 
number 200-400 has a more uniform step distribution, and the cable force of the internal and external radial cables has a 
small change in the predicted structural cable force at the position of 40° angle with the long axis. Thus, it can be seen 
that the cable force prediction of a circular chord-supported dome with digital twin technology can break the tradition and 
further improve structural safety and implementability. 
Keywords: Digital twin technique; Mean loss function; Load vector; Damping effect.

1 Introduction 
The string-supported dome structure is a new type of efficient large-span building structure, which is 
usually a new hybrid space structure system combining the upper single -layer mesh shell and the 
lower cable rod system [1 -3]. The structure has reasonable force and cleverly combines the 
advantages of both single -layer mesh shell and cable dome, which is a high -performance structure 
with reasonable force science and is widely used in large public buildings such as stadiums an d 
convention centers [4 -7]. Giv en that such structures are characterized by dense crowds and large 
flows of people, attention needs to be paid to their structural safety, so the study of the effects of 
explosions, impacts and other dynamic effects of chord -supported domes cannot be miss ing [8-10]. 
The string-supported dome structure, in which the tension cables are subjected to huge tensile forces, 
is the core component of the structure. In terrorist attacks, construction defects and other unexpected 
circumstances will occur the phenomenon of broken cables; the sudden breakage of the tension cable 
will produce a certain dynamic impact effect on the structure, causing the vibration of the nearby rod 
position [11-13]. The internal force changes and the sudden impact effect will make the internal force 
of the rod oscillate up and down substantially, and its magnitude can far exceed the original level, so 
the study of the dynamic impact effect of the broken cable is very important [14-18]. 
Due to the continuous development and improvement of digital twin technology, the emergence and 
application of various large -scale and comprehensive finite element design and analysis software 
have effectively solved the problems of prestressed steel structures in design and analysis, 
construction simulation, engineering applications and other key technologies, which have led to the 
faster development of large-span prestressed steel structure systems. For example, intelligent energy 
management operation through digitalization is realized in the literature [19]. These problems are 
overcome by changing the microgrid configuration and operation algorithms in virtual space in 
various ways and performing real -time tests using digital twin technology. Propose an operational 
scheduling model for energy storage systems to be applied in the virtual space when building 
microgrids using digital twin technology. The optimal charging and discharging schedules are 
established and implemented using a decision tree and nonlinear autoregressive neural network model 
instead of existing optimization techniques. The literature [20] proposes that processing quality is the 
basis for ensuring product quality, reflecting the development needs and application values for 
achieving smart manufacturing. For the problems of low efficiency, poor timeliness and unpredictable 
quality control in tracing quality problems during machining, digital twin technology can provide a 
new intelligent solution. Firstly, a Bayesian network model for factor analysis affecting processing 
quality is introduced to determine the correlation and influence the weight of each factor with 
processing quality. Secondly, it integrates multi-source heterogeneous data to track processing quality 
and establishes a multi-level scalable information model and correlation mechanism. 
A novel stochastic computational framework based on fractional Meyer wavelet artificial neural 
networks for nonlinear Singer fractional differential equations was designed in the literature [21]. The 
modeling advantages of wavelet artificial neural network s are used to convert differential systems 
into difference equations and to implement an approximation theory in the sense of mean square error 
to develop merit functions for nonlinear Singer fractional differential equations. The literature [22] 
shows tha t digital twin technology has been increasingly used to optimize the performance of 
industrial systems and is listed as one of the top ten most promising technology trends for the next 
decade. In it, a holistic digital twin architecture is proposed where t he network is integrated along 
with other industrial components of the system. A digital network twin allowed closed-loop network 
management throughout the network lifecycle. Connections between each digital twin and its physical 
counterpart in the industr ial system can be established. The feasibility of the proposed architecture 
was verified by selecting the most suitable communication mechanism to meet the real -time 
requirements of a flexible production system. Each model in the above literature fails to analyze its

Based on this, this paper applies the digital twin technique to the string-supported dome, firstly using

digital twin technique. The system equations of motion are discretized by time, and the overall

the digital twin techniqu e, the comparative analysis of the mesh displacement, the X - and Y -

results of the model, which provides data support for the prediction of the cable forc e of the chord-
supported dome.

4.1 Comparative prediction of mesh shell displacement 
Digital twin technology combines structural engineering to summarize a more intuitive structural 
twin architecture. First, a channel is constructed that enables real -time data transfer with physical 
entities. Secondly, a highly simulated digital twin modeling of the problem to be solved is performed 
based on real -time data. Since the absolute value of the mesh shell displacement is small, it is not 
normalized. The analysis calculates the data results of mesh shell displacement, support reaction force 
and tensile force caused by the difference in the members’ specification, the prestress’s size, the 
number of lower supports and the form of the structure. Different and different change laws, and after 
normalization, the differences within the resultant data ca n be eliminated, and the influence of the 
subtle differences of the basic parameters on the research results can be reduced, as shown in Figure

Figure 3. Comparative analysis of mesh shell displacement 
The comparative analysis of the mesh shell displacement is shown in Fig. 3. When the number of 
bishops is 0, the displacement of the elliptical string -supported dome is -0.01, which is at the edge 
position, and the mesh shell node displacement of the circular string-supported dome is fixed. As the 
number of bishops moves from 200 to 400, the overall displacement of the casing nodes of the circular 
chord-supported dome shows a more uniform stepped distribution, indicating that the displacement

one, but the degree of unevenness is larger, which indicates that its displacement distribution is more 
uneven. It shows that the numerical model based on the chord -supported dome is more obvious in 
predicting the cable force, which is a superior method and reduces the limitations and inapplicability.

5 Conclusion 
This paper combines the digital twin technique with the chord-supported dome to solve for dynamic 
loads to obtain the structural load carrying the state. The response information is collected using the 
virtual physical and digital twin, and the average loss function is constructed to predict the cable force 
of the chord -supported dome. In the prediction process, a numerical model of the chord -supported 
dome based on the digital twin technique is designed and analyzed to verify the model’s practicality. 
The following conclusions are obtained: 
1) When the mesh shell displacement elephant number movement is not obvious to be an uneven 
step change, the smaller displacement value and larger displacement value in the circular 
chord-supported dome occupy a larger proportion. The middle value is less, indicating that its 
displacement distribution is more uneven than that of the circular chord -supported dome, 
indicating that the prediction of the cable force is more obvious in the numerical model based 
on the chord -supported dome. The method is superior to reducing the limitation and 
inapplicability. 
2) When the maximum values of the X -direction and Y-direction are 51823N and 38609N, the 
reaction force is symmetrically distributed along the biaxial axis, and the change along the 
circumferential constraint is more drastic. It indicates that the influence o f cable force 
prediction in the numerical model based on a chord -supported dome is smaller, and the loss 
of prestress in the vertical reaction force of the support constraint is smaller. 
3) In the internal and external radial cable force at the S axis (long axis), E3 axis (40° pinch axis) 
and E1 axis (short axis), the structural cable force prediction changes less, indicating that the 
structural cable force prediction under different combinat ions in the numerical model based 
on the chord-supported dome is not very variable, so the cable force prediction of the chord -
supported dome in this paper has better accuracy.
