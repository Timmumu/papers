Abstract Even at this point in the era of digital transformation, we are still facing 
many problems in the safety sector that cannot prevent the occurrence or spread of 
human casualties. When you are in an unexpected emergency, it is often difficult to 
respond only with human physical ability. Human casualties continue to occur at 
construction sites, manufacturing plants, and multi-use facilities used by many people 
in everyday life. If you encounter a situation where normal judgment is impossible in 
the event of an emergency at a life site where there are still many safety blind spots, 
it is difficult to cope with the existing manual guidance method. New variable guidance 
technology, which combines artificial intelligence and digital twin, can make it possible 
to prevent casualties by processing large amounts of data needed to derive appropriate 
countermeasures in real time beyond identifying what safety accidents occurred in 
unexpected crisis situations. When a simple control method that divides and monitors 
several CCTVs is digitally converted and combined with artificial intelligence and 3D 
digital twin control technology, intelligence augmentation (IA) effect can be achieved 
that strengthens the safety decision-making ability required in real time. With the 
enforcement of the Serious Disaster Enterprise Punishment Act, the importance of 
distributing a smart location guidance system that urgently solves the decision-making 
delay that occurs in safety accidents at various industrial sites and strengthens the real-
time decision-making ability of field workers and managers is highlighted. The smart 
location guidance system that combines artificial intelligence and digital twin consists 
of AIoT HW equipment, wireless communication NW equipment, and intelligent SW 
platform. The intelligent SW platform consists of Builder that supports digital twin 
modeling, Watch that meets real-time control based on synchronization between real 
objects and digital twin models, and Simulator that supports the development and 
verification of various safety management scenarios using intelligent agents. The smart 
location guidance system provides on-site monitoring using IoT equipment, CCTV-
linked intelligent image analysis, intelligent operating procedures that support 
workflow modeling to immediately reflect the needs of the site, situational location 
guidance, and digital twin virtual fencing access control technology. This paper 
examines the limitations of traditional fixed passive guidance methods, analyzes global 
technology development trends to overcome them, identifies the digital transformation 
properties required to switch to intelligent variable smart location guidance methods, 
explains the characteristics and components of AI-based public facility smart fire safety 
integrated system (ISFS).

2. Limitations of Existing Fire Safety Systems
Over the past few decades, emergency guidance 
systems have relied on collective evacuation methods by 
fixed passive guidance lights and alarm facilities (alarm, 
emergency broadcasting, etc.). In other words, the existing 
method relies on a uniform method, not various methods 
for responding to disaster accidents such as fire or gas 
leakage, and it is impossible to inform appropriate 
evacuation and response methods for each location of the 
victim, resulting in constant casualties. As shown in 
Figure 1, the existing guide lights serve as a guide to 
evacuation in the event of a fire by instructing the 
location and direction of the evacuation area, but they 
have a decisive limitation in not knowing whether the 
evacuation route and exit are safe in the event of an actual 
situation. In addition, if you rely only on safety manage-
ment personnel due to the large size and complexity of 
buildings, blind spots will occur and the time to respond 
to disasters will inevitably increase structurally.
Existing fixed evacuation guidance methods are 
suspicious even if actual fire warnings occur due to 
frequent false alarms, and as a result, it is difficult for 
panicked people to make normal judgments, resulting in 
casualties or expansion. As shown in Figure 2, three main

3. Overseas technology development trends
In Europe, the passive fixed evacuation induction 
method has a recognition rate of only 38% in the event of 
disasters and terrorism, so it is difficult to increase the 
evacuation rate within the golden time.1 In Europe, 
where major disasters have continued due to large fires 
and terrorism, R&D investment is being supported to 
urgently convert the existing passive safety management 
method to a new active location guidance method.
3.1. Project GETAWAY
The EU invested about 3.11 million euros for three 
years from November 2011 to October 2014 to carry out 
research and development to innovate location guidance 
and evacuation guidance methods in case of a disaster in 
underground-linked complex buildings (see Fig. 3). Tests 
on conventional fixed passive evacuation guidance methods 
in London Underground and Barcelona Metro, Spain, 
showed that only 38% of people were evacuated to suitable 
places. Existing passive evacuation guidance lights have 
limitations in that it is difficult to evacuate within golden 
time due to low recognition rates, so to overcome this, 
they attempted to improve the recognition rate and 
evacuation decision-making time for evacuation targets.
1
However, the GETAWAY project team faced the fatal 
disadvantage of taking too long to calculate the evacuation 
route because it calculated the evacuation route using a 
simulation program for designing fire routes and exits in 
the event of a disaster. If more than 6 to 8 minutes are 
spent calculating routes, this is 1/3 of the average golden 
time in multi-use facilities, so if real-time is not secured 
within seconds, it is difficult to use in actual disaster 
situations.
3.2. eV ACUATE
The eV ACUATE is a large project promoted by the 
European Commission with an investment of more than 
8.58 million euros over four years from April 2013 to 
May 2017 to prepare an active variable guidance system 
for high-rise and regional infrastructures with large 
populations (see Fig. 4). Efforts were made to detect 
large-scale personnel flows using CCTV and mobile 
devices, and to simultaneously provide places of danger 
to the general public in the event of a disaster or terrorist 
accident and the direction of evacuation through active 
guidance devices and mobile apps.2

3.3. HEX Dynamic Evacuation
HEX Saving is a Taiwan-based company with excellent 
technology to manufacture its own fire fighting system. 
In conjunction with existing fire fighting systems, 
techniques have been developed to provide active dynamic 
location guidance by eliminating paths where flame or 
hazardous smoke is detected from available copper wires 
(Figure 5).
The HEX Dynamic Evacuation system provided 
dynamic location guidance by excluding areas where 
danger was detected while omitting the real-time route 
search process, so it showed technical limitations that 
only simple variable guidance was possible because fast 
route or entry blocking guidance was not possible.
The path from various locations inside the building to 
multiple exits is a three-dimensional path that combines 
horizontal and vertical movements, including emergency 
stairs. In addition, when the concept of time expansion is 
added to the disaster situation as it changes from time to 
time, the network graph model turns into a vast problem 
with more than hundreds of millions of possible 
combinations. Real-time variable guidance requires the 
development of a high-level algorithm that performs vast 
amounts of calculations in seconds to calculate safe and 
fast paths that are still available in an emergency in a 
large three-dimensional graph network. In particular, 
considering the growing size of the building, the size of 
the graph network connecting the available evacuation 
routes also increases rapidly, making it difficult to solve 
the problem due to an exponential increase in calculation 
time with the Dijkstra technique.
4. Integrated Smart Fire Safety (ISFS) system
4.1. Dynamic positioning problem properties
General navigation path calculation is a problem of 
obtaining a single path from a single starting point to a 
single destination. A path using a vehicle is a horizontal 
movement single path that exists on a plane. In contrast, 
the problem of calculating travel routes from various 
floors to multiple exits inside the building in an emergency 
increases complexity in three dimensions, and if the 
concept of time expansion is added to reflect changing 
disaster situations, the combination of possible travel 
routes rapidly expands. Figure 6 shows the com-plexity 
of the multi-path calculation problem of three-dimensional 
escape from human life in buildings with the concept of 
space and time expansion compared to the problem of 
calculating the shortest path of vehicle navigation. Although 
it is a simple network graph model that expresses the 
place where the guide device is installed and connects 
four nodes and nodes with a link, it is easy to see that the 
size of the problem increases rapidly even if the unit time 
increases from T0 to T4. In this type of network graph 
model, the problem of calculating an effective path is 
called the capacity constraint routing problem (CCRP), 
and it is well known that even if the size of the problem 
grows a little (i.e., even if the building grows a little), it 
takes a very long time to calculate the backtracking. In 
particular, in order to secure real-time calculation of life 
escape routes required in di saster situations, intelligent 
search techniques using AI engines different from the 
previous one should be applied. 
The current fire law does not systematically stipulate 
variable guidance devices. This is a facility that must be 
additionally installed by the user's choice, and economic 
feasibility is very important. For this reason, it is difficult 
to use expensive large computers, including parallel 
computing, to complete variable path calculation in 
seconds, and an extremely efficient AI engine-based path 
search algorithm is required to calculate results in seconds 
only by using low-end PC server-level computers.

4.3. ARTERA AI Engine
As shown in Figure 9, the Altera engine was developed 
to apply a safe and fast three-dimensional three-dimensional 
path search technique while expanding the graph according 
to the passage of time and the movement of the real 
person to reflect the changing disaster situation every 
second. Considering the path calculation complexity of the 
time-expanded network graph model, the Time-dependent 
Quickest Path algorithm was developed and mounted to 
compensate for the limitations of soaring calculation time 
when the model size increases.
In the case of commercial optimization calculation 
solvers such as IBM CPLEX, even in a simple path 
network model, the calculation time increases rapidly 
when the number of nodes increases, and the speed 
decreases significantly when calculating three-dimensional 
paths from large locations to multiple exits. In the case of 
the CCRP algorithm, which is a representative of the path 
calculation algorithm in the existing time extension 
graph, problems such as speed reduction and the use of 
fixed value travel time still occur when the size of the 
path network increases by relying on Dijkstra's algorithm. 
The Altera engine can reflect exceptions according to 
copper wire modeling in the network creation stage, and 
the shortest path is calculated by applying AI path search 
techniques rather than Dijkstra's algorithm in the path

4.5. Digital twin technology elements
As shown in Figure 12, the smart fire safety integrated 
system reflects the evacuation line as a node and arc 
graph along with the internal structure on the three-
dimensional spatial model of the building and automati-
cally converts it into a path calculation repair problem 
based on a time-expanded network graph without 
complex mathematical modeling. The movement path in 
the building is represented by a single graph model in 
which nodes (location) and links (paths) are connected. It 
supports the application of the real-time high-speed route 
calculation algorithm Altera engine by reflecting easy 
graphic-based movements without difficult mathematical 
modeling. A tool was developed to place a guide device 
(node) in a 3D model and to generate a movement path 
(arc) between evacuation guide devices. This generates 
both node and arc connection information and attribute 
information as well as a simple graph modeling function.
When a 3D digital twin model is created, the smart fire 
safety integrated system can easily apply safety scenarios 
by synchronizing real-time with event data for various 
situations in the building, as well as performing comparative

4.6. Application Effect of Integrated Smart Fire Safety 
System 
The smart fire safety integrated system studied through 
the high-level technology development process is equipped 
with an Altera AI engine, a variable guidance device 
using LPW A wireless communication, and a 3D digital 
twin control platform. The following is a summary of the 
key performance demonstrated by accredited test results.
① If there is a movement path between multiple 
stations installed in a certain space and the above 
stations, the movement path is defined as a graph 
including nodes and edges, respectively, and the 
evacuation guidance of objects is defined as a 
problem of finding the shortest path through the 
graph.
② In an emergency, evacuation guidance is defined as 
a problem of moving as many objects from the 
corresponding edge to a specific node (e.g., exit 
node) and the available people for each movement 
path for a unit time.
③ By defining a time-reflecting graph so that appro - 
priate active evacuation guidance can be carried out 
over time, and by creating and guiding evacuation 
routes reflecting the current situation over time, 
efficient evacuation guidance can be carried out.
It was reviewed through authorized tests that the 
alternative escape guidance system used instead of the 
lighting device to the emergency escape route in the test 
space implementing complex facilities, public spaces, and 
pedestrian corridors of public facilities met the functional 
and performance standards.
① Using the system, it was confirmed that the escape 
route can be easily identified when general emergency 
lighting does not perform efficient operation due to 
smoke.
② Using the system, it was confirmed that when one 
exit was not used, the way to the other exit could be 
easily found.
③ Using the system, it was confirmed that when 
moving randomly (up or down) a designated layer, 
it could be moved to a predetermined layer.
In particular, after placing 1,000 people inside the 8th 
floor of L Department Store (10 floors above ground, 1 
basement floor), a multi-use facility located in Seoul, two 
evacuation guidance scenarios (

a fire in the lower left stairwell, upper left stairwell, and

conducted three times, and the total evacuation completion 
time was measured. As a result of a performance 
experiment, as shown in Table 1, the evacuation reduction 
effect of more than 30% was confirmed when the smart 
location guidance system was applied compared to the

5. Conclusios
The smart fire safety integrated system developed 
through this study can search for available routes in about 
1 second in a 10-story small and medium-sized building 
that can accommodate more than 1,000 people using an 
Altera engine, and secured communication safety through 
ultra-low power long-distance wireless repeaters. In 
addition, it does not perform difficult mathematical modeling 
through 3D digital twin technology, but incorporates a 
real-time high-speed path calculation algorithm through 
easy graphic-based copper wire reflection. As a result of 
verifying the performance of the smart location guidance 
system, it was confirmed that the existing passive analog 
evacuation guidance system could be converted to an 
active digital method to actively evacuate through real-
time variable location guidance reflecting risk factors. In 
addition, it is thought that it can be used as a system that 
can greatly contribute to the protection of people's lives 
by maximizing survival rates and minimizing casualties
