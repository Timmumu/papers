ABSTRACT
In the process of large-span steel structure construction, because its structure has not yet formed a complete 
system, so its mechanical properties and safety status are not stable, and it is necessary to control the safety of the 
structure, and the current traditional structural health monitoring (SHM) has the problems of high cost, easy to 
be interfered, and unpredictable, etc. The digital twin, as a popular technology in the engineering industry, has 
the characteristics of multidimensionality, high integrative, intelligent and real-time, so it is very necessary to 
introduce digital twin technology in the construction process. In this paper, a digital twin-driven intelligent 
safety control method for large-span steel structures is proposed. The digital twin model is constructed by col -
lecting actual engineering data and integrating finite element analysis data. Based on the random forest algo -
rithm, The influencing factors of the overall jacking construction process of the large-span steel mesh frame are 
analyzed to obtain the importance ranking of the features affecting the process. The results show that the most 
influential features for the overall jacking construction are the height difference of the jacking frame and the 
jacking speed. Therefore, the above two should be paid special attention to in the construction safety control.
1. Introductions
Space steel mesh frame structure is one of the earliest and most 
widely used structural forms of various space structures, which is 
commonly used in the roofing system of some large-scale infrastructures 
and public buildings, and is often seen in some airports [1,2] , high-speed 
rail stations [3] , gymnasiums [4] , and exhibition centers and other 
architectural facilities. The mesh frame structure itself has excellent 
structural properties such as good integrity, high spatial stiffness, strong 
stability, light weight and beautiful shape [5] . However, since the full 
stress design method is generally used for calculation and analysis in the 
design stage of the net frame structure, this also leads to the fact that the 
net frame structure is very sensitive to the initial defects of the rods and 
the adjustment of the use of the load and other factors, and in the process 
of construction or operation and maintenance of the construction load 
[6] , the use of the function of the changes in the use of the renovation of 
the event, resulting in the load borne is different from the design stage, 
so that the key rods and the key node The load carrying capacity is 
exceeded, leading to local instability or overall collapse of the structure, 
which ultimately causes major casualties and economic losses, there -
fore, the control of structural safety is particularly important.
Currently, general construction projects collect the safety data of the 
structure through contact sensors, total stations [7] , etc., and compare 
the data with the limit values corresponding to the code, so as to derive 
the current safety status of the structure, i.e., the structural health 
detection (SHM) in the traditional sense. However, this method has 
certain drawbacks: (1) structural health detection requires the installa -
tion and maintenance of a large number of sensors, but due to the eco -
nomic impact, they are often installed at critical locations only based on 
engineering experience, and both periodic inspection and data analysis 
require a large amount of manpower and material input, making the cost 
of the traditional SHM method relatively high [8,9] ; (2) the contact 
sensors usually need to be manually mounted directly on the structure. 
Some of the sensors have inconvenient installation problems [10] , 
which may lead to inaccurate data collection due to irregular installa -
tion factors, and they may also be dropped outdoors due to wind, rain, 
snow, and human influence; (3) The traditional SHM method mainly 
focuses on the current state of the structure [11] , which makes it difficult 
to accurately predict the future performance of the structure and the 
possible problems that may occur. However, Wang et al. have proposed 
some effective methods for engineering nonprobabilistic transient reli -
ability in the case of insufficient data [12 – 15] .

Digital twin technology is to make full use of physical models, sensor 
updates, historical operational data, etc., integrating multi-disciplinary, 
multi-physical quantity, multi-scale, multi-probability simulation pro -
cesses, and completing the mapping in the virtual space, so as to reflect 
the corresponding entity’s full life cycle process. Pioneered in the 
aerospace field and applied, in recent years, digital twins have also been 
introduced in the manufacturing industry [16,17] , construction industry 
[18,19] to carry out digital intelligence upgrading, especially in the 
introduction of digital twins in civil engineering projects, the digital 
twins are used in the whole life cycle of the project, such as design [20] , 
construction [21,22] , operation and maintenance [23,24] , demolition 
remodeling [25] , and other phases. Especially in the construction phase 
of civil engineering projects, digital twin technology has been more 
widely used.
In the context of bridges and highways, Mafipour [26] proposed a 
reverse engineering method for parametric modeling of bridges. for 
generating parametric models of bridges from segmented point clouds. 
Following the concept of reverse engineering for parametric modeling, a 
parametric prototype model (PPM) is proposed as a tool for extracting 
parameter values from the point cloud. Local and global optimization 
problems were defined to tune the PPM and assemble it into an inte -
grated model. Jiang et al. [27] proposed a digital twin based road 
widening system approach for checking the clearance of underpasses in 
highway widening projects using online map data, creating digital twins 
of underpasses based on the digital twins of existing arterials and a BIM 
model of the newly widened road to aid in clearance inspection and 
redesign.
In terms of tunneling, Li et al. [28] conducted a comprehensive 
analysis from the perspectives of real-time or instantaneous data 
acquisition, data interpretation, and data aggregation in a complete and 
seamless “data acquisition-interpretation-aggregation” workflow, which 
is linked to dynamic tunneling to support optimal design, and proposed 
a conceptual framework for instantaneous tunneling support. Design. 
Subsequently, it integrates digital twins (DT) into the implementation of 
JIT design for rock tunnels, proposes a 
scenario-entity-relationship-event-control modeling approach as a novel 
DT application model for cross-domain sharing and reuse, and integrates 
multiple advanced data acquisition, interpretation, and aggregation 
methods using DT [29] .
In terms of building structure, Liu et al. [30] proposed an intelligent 
construction method based on digital twin, which integrates IoT, BIM 
and finite element model to build a framework of intelligent construc -
tion method based on digital twin, gives real-time feedback and regu -
lation to the whole process of construction, and improves the 
information utilisation rate of construction operation.Wang et al., based 
on the concept of digital twin, proposed a method to consider the 
spatio-temporal variation of the Based on the concept of digital twin, 
Wang et al. proposed a safety control method for cable network structure 
considering spatial and temporal changes. This method can monitor and 
control the construction process of cable network in real time on site, 
and it is convenient to compare and analyse the mechanical and 
geometrical information on the construction site with the real-time 
finite element simulation results [31] .
In order to solve the technical problems of structural health moni -
toring and digital twin, this study combines the digital twin (DT) tech -
nique with the stochastic Morien algorithm. It makes up for the 
traditional SHM method that relies heavily on manual operation and 
judgement, which leads to errors and subjectivity, and solves the diffi -
cult real-time problem, and also makes the digital twin technology 
complete the mirror mapping of the structure with the help of sensors.
Based on this, this paper proposes a digital twin framework for safety 
control of spatial structures based on random forests, and verifies the 
effectiveness of the proposed method by establishing a model of random 
forests, using actual construction data for training and feature correla -
tion analysis. This study aims to achieve the purpose of risk control 
based on digital twin and machine learning technology, with the help of 
Random Forest algorithm to process the data collected during the con -
struction of the overall jacking of the steel mesh frame and to improve 
the next construction step. The method has the following advantages: 
(1) low cost - through the reasonable arrangement of sensors, it can 
achieve the monitoring of the key position of the structure and establish 
the digital twin model through the data; (2) real-time - combined with 
the historical data, it can be realised in DT space health monitoring, 
performance prediction and maintenance guidance; (3) high accuracy - 
able to dynamically show the mapping of the physical structure in DT 
space through the sensors; (4) expandability - able to be carried out in 
engineering projects using the integral jacking method and the integral 
jacking method for use.
The rest of the paper is organised as follows: Section 2 describes the

Section 3 describes the process and methodology of feature analysis

2. Intelligent safety control method for large-span steel 
structures driven by digital twins
Digital twin technology refers to the simulation, analysis and opti -
misation of the whole life cycle of physical entities through digital 
means in order to achieve two-way mapping and interaction between 
physical entities and digital models. In the field of large-span steel 
structures, digital twin technology can realise real-time monitoring, data 
analysis, fault diagnosis and predictive maintenance of large-span steel 
structures, and provide powerful support for the safe and intelligent 
control of large-span steel structures [32] .
In this paper, the DT framework for large-span steel structures is 
built, as shown in Fig. 1 , which takes the space steel mesh frame 
structure as the physical entity for DT mapping, corresponding to the 
construction data of the project on the left side of the figure, including 
static data and dynamic data. Static data refers to the geometrical in -
formation of the relevant components of the structure, such as rod 
length, inner and outer diameters and monitoring equipment models, 
which are basically unchanged and inherent; dynamic data refers to the 
data that will change as the construction advances, such as ambient 
wind speed, temperature, and the data of rod stresses and displacements. 
It also contains a document database with architectural drawings, 
specifications and geographic information data, such as the top and 
bottom chord layout of the grid, structural construction staircase sample 
drawings and the underground pipe network of the terminal building. 
The right part is the digital twin model, which is a virtual object mapped 
by the physical space, containing geometric and finite element models 
(geometric, physical, behavioural, and rule-based), predictive control 
algorithms, and the physical building, which integrates all kinds of static 
and dynamic data.
2.1. Geometric models
To realise the perfect mapping of virtual space to physical space, it is 
necessary to construct a geometric model of the long-span steel structure 
first. The model should be able to comprehensively reflect the geometric 
characteristics and material properties of the long-span steel structure.
It mainly includes the construction form, span, height, material, 
connection form of nodes, etc. The material characteristics mainly 
include material type, yield strength, elastic modulus, etc., and it can 
achieve real-time synchronisation with the physical entity. In the con -
struction process, the technical means of 3D scanning and laser mea -
surement are used to obtain the accurate geometric information of the 
large-span steel structure. In this paper, three-dimensional modelling

2.2. Physical models
A physical model is a digital representation of a real-world physical 
entity or system that simulates the structure, properties, behaviour of 
physical entities and their interactions with the environment. The 
physical model is one of the core components of digital twin technology, 
which provides the basis for real-time synchronisation and interaction 
between the virtual space and the physical space.
In this paper, numerical simulation modelling has been carried out 
using finite element software, in which all components of the steel roof 
cover are meshed using beam cells. The model was designed completely 
based on the actual situation and boundary conditions. According to the 
characteristics of welded ball nodes and bolted ball nodes, a form of 
multiplying the self-weight coefficient of the steel roof cover by an 
expansion factor of 1.3 is used for application. The key to simulate the 
mechanical behaviour of the jacked structure is the actual estimation of 
the partial coefficient of static load, and a value of 1.3 was adopted for 
the finite element model. Fig. 3 shows the finite element model of the

2.3. Behavioral models
At the behavioural level, digital twins are able to simulate the entire 
process of project construction, equipment operation or user behaviour. 
By building an accurate behavioural model, the mechanical response of 
a structure under different scenarios can be predicted and simulated, 
potential factors that may lead to risks can be identified, preventive 
measures can be taken in advance and solutions can be optimised.
In this paper, based on the geometric and physical models, the 
construction conditions consistent with the jacking scheme of the proj -
ect are established in the behavioural layer, and the corresponding 
conditions are analysed and computed by the finite element model, 
which results in the loads that the project will be subjected to and the 
safety performance and structural state of the project under the con -
struction conditions, and is used to predict the maintenance and safety 
control of the structure.
2.4. Rule-based model
In the whole digital twin modelling process, the rule model plays a 
crucial role. The rule model firstly defines and quantitatively limits the 
mechanical performance parameters of each type of equipment and size 
of components during the construction process according to the na -
tional, industrial or corporate internal standards. These parameters are 
the basis for assessing the safety performance of the structure, and any 
simulation results outside the limits will be considered unacceptable, 
thus ensuring the rigour of the analysis.
The rule-based model should also support the dynamic adjustment of 
mechanical performance parameters as construction progresses and 
external environmental conditions change. For example, considering the 
influence of temperature, humidity and other environmental factors on 
the material properties, the relevant parameters are automatically 
adjusted through the preset algorithms to make the simulation closer to 
the actual situation. Combined with the data monitored by the sensors in 
real time, the rule model is able to analyse the operating status of the 
equipment, predict the potential risk of failure, and send out warning 
signals in advance. This helps to take timely measures to avoid the 
adverse effects of equipment failure on construction progress and 
quality.
3. Data analysis and troubleshooting
Through the data actually collected in the project, fusing the twin 
data simulated by the high-fidelity digital twin model, the correlation 
analysis of the relevant parameters is carried out by taking the relevant 
contents of jacking height as the characteristic variables. This project is 
based on the random forest approach to carry out feature correlation 
analysis of the specific height of the jacking frame, the maximum jacking 
height, the average jacking height, the maximum height difference of 
the jacking, and the jacking speed, which provides certain basis for the 
safety control of the structure and the construction plan.
3.1. Random forest
The Random Forest (RF) algorithm is an integrated learning method 
that belongs to the category of supervised learning algorithms and 
consists of classifiers or regressors constructed from multiple decision 
trees. It was proposed by Leo Breiman [33] in 2001 and aims to improve 
the predictive accuracy and robustness of models by constructing mul -
tiple decision trees.
The Random Forest algorithm has demonstrated significant benefits 
and advantages in the field of machine learning and data analysis, and it 
stands out in many applications, mainly including: (1) By integrating 
multiple decision trees, Random Forest effectively improves the accu -
racy and stability of prediction, and this integration mechanism can 
significantly reduce the bias and variance of a single model, so as to 
obtain more reliable prediction results. (2) It performs well in overfitting 
resistance, thanks to its Bagging-based integration strategy and random 
feature selection mechanism, which helps to maintain the generalisation 
ability of the model in complex datasets. (3) Performs well in dealing 
with large-scale datasets, and its parallelised processing gives the algo -
rithm a significant advantage in terms of efficiency and scalability.
In random forests, each decision tree is constructed by recursively 
partitioning the dataset into purer subsets. This partitioning is done 
based on a certain feature, with the aim of making the partitioned subset 
as low as possible in terms of impurity in the target variable (e.g., Gini 
impurity or information gain in classification problems, variance in 
regression problems). Therefore, each feature brings a certain amount of 
impurity reduction when splitting the nodes of the decision tree. The 
basic idea of average impurity reduction can be expressed by Eq.1 :

3.2. Information collection
In the construction process using the overall jacking method, the 
structure has not yet formed a reliable system, therefore, after consulting 
the corresponding steel structure construction specification and ana -
lysing the relevant factors affecting the safety of the structure, the stress 
is selected as the index for determining the performance of the structure, 
and the main influencing factors are the temperature effect, the wind 
load and the height of the jacking frame.
The overall jacking time is relatively short, in the process of the 
overall jacking temperature will not occur during this time, will not have 
a greater impact on the overall rigidity and stability of the structure; for 
the wind load, the jacking frame structure erection is completed, the top 
of the cable wind is also installed, as shown in Fig. 4 forming a more 
complete structural system, then start jacking, gravity load for the 
structure of the weight and the weight of the object to be lifted. In the 
construction specification, the structure should be able to resist when 
the wind is not more than grade 8, so it is not considered in this paper.
As the technology at the present stage cannot achieve a fully 
synchronised overall jacking, nor can it control the loading of cylinders 
and the height of a single jacking when jacking, the height of the jacking 
frame will have a slight error, which also leads to the part of the 
structure that does not match with the design deflection, and thus leads 
to changes in the structural stresses.
In summary, this paper chooses the height of the jacking frame as the 
main control content, i.e., the specific height of the jacking frame, the 
average height, the difference between the maximum height and the 
minimum height, and the jacking speed in the jacking process are the 
main influencing factors of the stress.
The specific height of the jacking frame refers to the height value of 
each jacking frame involved in jacking, and the overall jacking method 
is mainly to adjust the height of each jacking frame to realise the overall 
jacking, so the control of the specific height of the jacking frame is in fact 
the most important content to control the safety of the overall jacking 
method. In the specific construction process, the specific stiffness of the 
jacking frame is often not an intuitive factor to react to the structural 
safety performance, therefore, after analysing the specific height of the 
jacking frame, the average height of the jacking frames, the height dif -
ference between the highest jacking frame and the lowest jacking frame, 
and the speed of the jacking frame are more intuitive factors to react to 
the structural stresses.
The average height reflects the approximate height to which the roof 
was lifted by the jacking frames, i.e., the average position reached by all 
the jacking frames in the process of jacking, which is an important basis 
for evaluating the effect of jacking and determining the final position of 
the structure.
The height difference between the highest jacking frame and the 
lowest jacking frame reflects the height difference between different 
jacking frames. The size of the height difference has a direct impact on 
the stability and safety of the structure, so it needs to be closely moni -
tored and adjusted during the jacking process.
The jacking speed reflects the height of each jacking step, which is an 
important index for assessing the jacking efficiency and controlling the 
stability of the jacking process. Too fast a jacking speed may lead to 
uneven stresses and reduced stability of the structure; while too slow a 
jacking speed may prolong the construction period and increase the 
construction cost. Therefore, it is necessary to control the jacking speed 
reasonably according to the actual situation during the jacking process.
Based on the importance of the jacked structure and the uncertainty 
of the overall jacking operation, during the jacking process, it is neces -
sary to reflect the safety performance of the structure and the safety 
status of the structure through continuous monitoring indicators, mainly 
deflection, stress, strain, cracks and other mechanical property in -
dicators. Since the project is a new construction stage and mainly a steel 
structure net frame, the crack factor of the rods can be disregarded, 
while the displacement and deflection of the adjacent points are reacted 
by the height of the jacking frame, which is a factor affecting the stress, 
and is no longer considered as a separate factor. Therefore, during the 
overall jacking process, the strain of the key bars is monitored and the 
bar stresses are calculated to assess the reliability of the structure and 
the safety performance of the construction.
The sensor network can monitor the large-span steel structure in real 
time and collect various data including stress, strain and temperature. 
The collected data can also be pre-processed, including filtering, 
denoising, calibration and other operations to improve the accuracy and 
reliability of the data. In this project, stresses are measured and moni -
tored by deploying vibrating string stress sensors on the critical bars of 
the steel mesh frame to react to the safety performance of the structure, 
as shown in Fig. 5 .
Stress data were collected and stored automatically by a computer- 
controlled data acquisition system. Careful consideration was given to 
the selection of monitoring points, and based on the analysis of the 
simultaneous jacking simulation, vibrating string stress gauges were 
mounted on the surfaces of key bars, making it relatively easy to install 
monitoring points as well, ultimately in order to collect sufficient stress 
values representative of the overall steel roof with a suitable number of 
sensors.

3.3.2. Outlier processing
Outliers, as extreme observations in the data, may have a significant 
impact on the results of the analyses. Therefore, various statistical 
methods (Z-score test, IQR method) were used to identify outliers in the 
dataset during the data cleaning process. For the detected outliers, 
appropriate treatment strategies were adopted based on the specifics of 
the data and analysis needs. Obvious errors or outliers that could not be 
reasonably explained without relevant data before and after were 
deleted; outliers that were caused by similar data before and after, by 
measurement errors or data entry errors were corrected by linear 
interpolation through the proximity of the data; and outliers that devi -
ated from the subject distribution but had potential analytical value 
were retained and analysed as special cases for subsequent analyses.

3.3.4. Characteristic Importance Analysis
In this paper, the following steps are taken to carry out the analysis of 
feature correlation and feature importance based on random forest:
Constructing Decision Trees: Firstly, each tree in a random forest is 
constructed independently. When constructing each tree, the random 
forest algorithm randomly selects a subset of features, and searches for 
the optimal split points on these features to construct the nodes of the 
tree.
Node Gain: Whenever a feature is used to split a node, the algorithm 
calculates the gain generated by the split, i.e., the amount of impurity 
reduction before and after the split using the feature. Impurity is usually 
measured by Mean Squared Error (MSE).
Feature Contribution Accumulation:For each tree, the algorithm 
keeps track of the contribution of each feature in the split nodes and 
accumulates these contributions to get the total contribution of the 
feature.
Average Feature Importance: Since the random forest contains 
multiple trees, the contribution of each feature is averaged across all the 
trees to get the average importance score of the feature.
Feature Importance Ranking: Finally, all features are ranked in order 
of importance to get the highest to lowest ranked feature importance 
and to get the most important features that affect the prediction results.
3.3.5. Characteristic correlation
Fig. 6 shows the correlation matrix between the input feature vari -
ables considered in this study, and it can be observed that there is a 
strong correlation between each specific jacking height (R = 1),which is 
mainly attributed to the fact that the jacking heights are actually

and the specific heights of the individual jacking frames during the

of that jacking is not reached occasionally, so the maximum height 
difference and the jacking speed are also affected by each jacking height. 
Occasionally reached the highest height of the jacking, so the maximum 
jacking height and the average jacking height is a uniform effect, and the 
maximum height difference and jacking speed is also affected by the 
individual jacking height.
3.3.6. Characteristic importance
Determining the order of importance of each feature is critical to the 
impact on the structural safety performance of the overall jacking, and 
the results of the feature importance analysis can provide a basis for the 
safety control of the overall jacking phase. The Random Forest algorithm 
is used to assess the feature importance by disrupting the order of the 
features and calculating the degree of attenuation of the comparative 
model performance. The trained Random Forest model is analysed 
several times according to the sensors at different locations to obtain the 
importance ranking of the features, as shown in Fig. 7 .
From Fig. 7 , it can also be concluded that the maximum height dif -
ference of jacking and the speed of jacking are in the first two places in 
the ranking of each graph, and the difference in values with other fea -
tures is large, which can be concluded that the maximum height dif -
ference of jacking and the speed of jacking are the most important 
feature variables affecting the safety performance (stress) of the struc -
ture, which is also in line with the previous research on what should be 
paid attention to during the construction process, and also in line with 
the construction specification mentioned in the content to be controlled. 
Therefore, in projects where the overall jacking method is used, extra 
attention should be paid to the performance of both, and the difference 
between the maximum and minimum heights of the jacking frame 
should be strictly controlled, and the jacking should be carried out 
slowly and at an even speed.
In addition, compared with the maximum difference in height and 
speed of jacking, the maximum jacking height and average height have 
less influence on the structural safety compared with the maximum 
difference in height and speed of jacking, which also proves that as long 
as the difference in height of the jacking frame and the speed of jacking 
can be controlled, the safety performance and structural condition can 
be guaranteed.
4. Security control decision-making objectives
4.1. Minimum stress change target
In engineering projects, construction safety has always been a 
problem that has aroused widespread concern, and so far there is still no 
widely recognised structural safety risk indicator system, but some ex -
perts and scholars have also put forward safety risk indicators that they

each jacking frame is kept within the error range during the jacking

4.3. Overall jacking speed target
Under the premise of ensuring structural safety and construction 
efficiency, through reasonable control of the jacking speed and the de -
gree of coordination between the jacking frames, the steel structure rises 
smoothly and slowly during the jacking process, minimising the vibra -
tion and dynamic response of the structure during the jacking process, 
preventing excessive structural acceleration due to the overall jacking of 
the jacking frames too fast, or structural instability due to a single overly 
large jacking height, and controlling the overall jacking speed. The 
purpose is to make the stress distribution of the structure more uniform 
during the jacking process, avoid structural damage caused by local 
stress concentration, and ensure the smoothness and controllability of 
the jacking process.
4.4. Control system
The overall jacking method in this paper adopts multiple hydraulic 
jacking frames to provide jacking force in groups, and according to the 
stress collected by the sensors and the height linkage of the jacking 
frames, the steel mesh frames are assembled and shaped in-situ, and 
then jacked up to the unloading height by the jacking system, so as to 
realise the overall jacking of the large-span steel mesh frames structure.
The overall synchronous jacking system consists of jacking system 
(jacking frame group), power system (hydraulic pump station), moni -
toring system (sensors) and control system (computer) composed of four 
parts, the overall jacking synchronous control flowchart as shown in 
Fig. 8 .
The jacking frame group is the jacking component of the system, 
which can be configured in different quantities and arranged in the 
corresponding positions according to the load of the steel mesh roof 
required by the project, and the counterforce at each jacking point can 
be accurately obtained through the finite element analysis of the digital 
twin physical model, which is used in the hydraulic jacking jacks in this 
project.
The hydraulic pump station provides power for the whole jacking 
system, and the jacking and descending of the jacking frame through the 
extension cylinder action or contraction cylinder action is divided into 
total control and sub-control, which carries out the overall synchronous 
jacking control and the sub-control of each jacking frame locally, which 
can effectively improve the synchronous adjustment performance of the 
whole jacking system.
The monitoring system obtains mechanical information (stress, 
displacement, etc.) by deploying sensors around each jacking frame, 
obtains displacement information through the computer control system, 
and provides real-time feedback of mechanical and displacement data to 
the digital twin model. The digital twin model regulates the next action 
according to the current structural air attitude information.
The main control computer sets the stress limit value according to 
the result of finite element construction process analysis. When the 
stress near the jacking point is too large or the height difference between 
individual jacking frame and other jacking frames is too large, it will 
automatically alarm and send out the shutdown command, and adjust 
the height of individual jacking frames so that the whole structure is at 
the same height again.
5. Conclusions and outlook
In this paper, the digital twin-driven safety control method is applied 
to safety monitoring and intelligent control by taking the construction of 
the overall jacking of the large-span steel roof cover in the process of the 
reconstruction and expansion of an airport as an example. By con -
structing a digital twin model, the stress, strain and other parameters 
monitored in real time are analysed and fault diagnosis is performed.

After practical application verification, the method can effectively 
improve the safety and reliability of the large-span steel structure, 
reduce the maintenance cost and extend the service life. The specific 
conclusions are as follows:
(1) The digital twin-driven safety and intelligent control method for 
large-span steel structures is an effective solution that can achieve real- 
time monitoring, data analysis, fault diagnosis and predictive mainte -
nance of large-span steel structures. With the continuous development 
and improvement of digital twin technology, its application in the field 
of large-span steel structures will become more and more extensive. In 
the future, the in-depth integration of digital twin technology with 
advanced information technologies such as Internet of Things, cloud 
computing and big data can be further explored to provide more 
comprehensive, efficient and intelligent support for the safe and intel -
ligent control of the construction process of large-span steel structures.
(2) Through the data collected in the actual engineering scenarios 
and data analysis based on the random forest algorithm, the height 
difference of jacking frame and jacking speed are derived from the 
characteristic correlation analysis as the biggest factors affecting the 
mechanical performance of the structure, which is consistent with the 
content of the specification, proving the feasibility of the method and 
verifying its applicability in the construction safety. Therefore, through 
real-time monitoring and predictive control, the constructor can observe 
more intuitively the dynamic change of the overall jacking behaviour of 
the steel mesh frame during the construction stage, which significantly 
strengthens the structural safety in the construction process.
(3) In order to ensure the analysis accuracy of the model, the data 
collected during the construction process is used. The efficiency of data 
transmission also needs to be improved. Future research could focus on 
improving the quality and integration of the model data, such as opti -
mising predictive control algorithms, using high-precision sensors and 
data high-rate transmission techniques to obtain more comprehensive 
construction data. In addition, the method can be extended to different 
types of structures and other construction scenarios using monolithic 
jacking or monolithic jacking, such as bridges with high-rise building 
platforms.
