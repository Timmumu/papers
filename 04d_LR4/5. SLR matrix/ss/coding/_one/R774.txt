F:Optimization method of cable structure demolition driven by digital twin evolution model Guoliang Shi a , b , Zhansheng Liu a , b , * , Dechun Lu a , b , Qingwen Zhang a , b a Faculty of Architecture, Civil and Transportation Engineering, Beijing University of Technology, Beijing 100124, China b The Key Laboratory of Urban Security and Disaster Engineering of the Ministry of Education, Beijing University of Technology, Beijing 100124, China ARTICLE INFO Keywords: Cable structure Digital twin Structure demolition Optimization of scheme Experimental verification ABSTRACT The large-span cable structure has the problems of complex component connection, cumbersome demolition process and difficult process coordination, which lead to high construction safety risk and low efficiency. Moreover, the optimization of the construction process scheme is difficult, and the convergence and visualization of the finite element analysis are insufficient, which hinders the construction efficiency. This study proposes a cable structure demolition optimization method driven by the digital twin (DT) evolution model. From the perspective of virtual-real mapping, the DT evolution process of cable structure demolition is clarified. The virtualization method of physical demolition process is formed by integrating visual modeling, simulation modeling and data modeling. Taking full account of the temporal and spatial evolution of the demolition process, a DT evolution model of the demolition process is established. Starting from the information flow, component flow, control flow and their relationship, the problems existing in the process of demolition execution are analyzed. Driven by the twin evolution process, a data mapping method for the demolition process is proposed. The safety risk is characterized by the mechanical properties (MPs) of the structure, and the expression language of the data association relationship is established. The particle swarm optimization back propagation neural network (PSO-BPNN) is improved, and an intelligent analysis method of MPs in the whole process of demolition is proposed. The best construction scheme is obtained by integrating the changes in MPs between the various demolition steps. The information of the demolition evolution process is divided into functional modules, and a DT platform is developed to efficiently guide the actual construction. Taking the demolition process of a cable truss experimental model as an example, the effectiveness of the proposed method is verified. The research results show that the DT evolution model can accurately map the actual demolition process. The deep learning method analyzes the mechanical properties of the structure and obtains the best construction scheme quickly and accurately. The development and application of the twin platform improves the degree of visualization and realizes the efficient guidance of the virtual model to the actual construction. 1. Introduction 1.1. Background The construction period of the cable structure is short and the shape plasticity is strong. The cable member only bears the axial tension, the material strength utilization rate is high, and the structural force is reasonable. Cable structure is widely used in large-span engineering [1] . The cable structure is mostly used in public buildings such as stadiums and exhibition centers. Taking the cable component as the key stress component, the structural systems such as cable truss [2] , cable dome [3] and chord-supported structure [4] are formed. Due to the change of building performance and structural safety performance, the cable structure often needs to be reformed and dismantled. The cable structure has many components and complex forces, which leads to complicated construction process, difficult coordination and high safety risk [5,6] . Therefore, it is of great significance to realize the efficient sched- uling of resources in the demolition process, the optimization of the demolition scheme and the accurate analysis of the safety state. In the process of structural construction, the cable force is an important basis for measuring the safety state [8]. It is necessary to clarify the development of each cable force to ensure that the cable force can develop smoothly and evenly during the demolition process [9]. In the process of demolition, according to the development of cable force, the best demolition scheme needs to be obtained, and the efficient guidance of the construction process are the focus of this study. In this process, it is necessary to clarify the batching, grading and cable relax- ation degree at all levels of the demolition process [10]. Many scholars have proposed new methods and techniques in structural optimization. Saleh et al. [11] proposed an optimization method for the mechanical properties of shear members based on topology optimization. Through numerical analysis, it was found that the optimized connection showed superior performance, and showed higher shear strength and energy dissipation capacity under monotonic and cyclic loading. Ghabussi et al. [12] used steel curved dampers to optimize the seismic performance of symmetrical and single-slope portal frames of variable-slope roofs. Based on the finite element (FE) analysis method, the influence of the curve damper on the energy dissipation, frame strength and stiffness of the portal frame is evaluated. Ghabussi et al. [13,14] used the FE analysis method to evaluate the seismic performance of the cold-formed hot-rolled steel wall system with curved steel dampers. Considering the strain energy, geometric comprehensive index and ultimate bearing capacity, Cao et al. [15] optimized the design of free-form cable-- supported reticulated shell. The FE analysis verifies that the designed structure has better mechanical properties and mesh quality. The above optimization methods provide reliable technical support for the opti- mization of mechanical properties of steel structures and composite structures. Moreover, most of the above optimization methods use the FE numerical simulation method, which also provides a reference for the optimization of the structural construction scheme. In the analysis of construction scheme, the traditional analysis method is to set the boundary conditions and working conditions in the FE model [16], and then obtain the change trend of cable force in the process of structural demolition. There are many uncertain factors such as temperature and wind load in the process of structural demolition. The traditional anal- ysis method cannot accurately obtain the best demolition scheme. In addition, the FE analysis method usually obtains the mechanical response of the structure according to the specific construction in- structions to judge the rationality of the scheme. This method is discrete and cannot realize the continuous dynamic analysis of the construction process. Therefore, the optimal selection of construction scheme cannot be realized in the process of finite element calculation. FE analysis fo- cuses on the mechanical information of the structure. However, in the actual construction process, how to realize the efficient allocation of resources in the construction process is the key to guiding the con- struction. Therefore, the construction process should be further visual- ized on the basis of FE calculation. The tooling configuration, action position and specific process parameters of each construction step are clarified. The process operation in the process of structural demolition often relies on empirical knowledge and subjective judgment, or needs to manually check the existing construction standards or specifications. Therefore, the current technical methods lead to the low level of con- struction management, which seriously affects the efficiency and accu- racy of demolition construction. Under the background of the rapid development of the new gener- ation of information technology, intelligent construction is reshaping the mode of MPs analysis and construction scheme control [17]. Through the cross-integration of new generation information technolo- gies such as digital twin (DT), artificial intelligence, building informa- tion modeling (BIM), and Internet of Things, solving various problems in engineering construction has become the focus of realizing the intelli- gence of the construction industry [18–20]. DT is a technology that makes full use of models and their data to achieve real-time synchronization, virtual-real mapping, and digital- analog linkage [21]. As an artificial intelligence method that can learn the deep features of data, deep learning can directly extract high-level features from the original data for perceptual decision-making and efficiently process state data [22].
