## Abstract
^sec-abstract

> Pending verification against fulltext. Cited in LR_cc.md Sec 3.4 for the integration of real-time locating systems (RTLS — UWB, RFID, BLE) into digital twin architectures, providing the IoT sensing layer needed for worker and equipment position tracking at construction sites.

## Core Claims
- ① Worker/asset location is fundamental to construction-safety DT.
- ② RTLS technologies (UWB, RFID, BLE) can provide positioning data.
- ③ DT provides spatial-semantic context for locating data and downstream safety reasoning.

## Limitations Noted in LR_cc.md
- Positioning accuracy under construction-grade occlusion and multi-path interference remains unvalidated.
- Remains within Level-2 maturity (detect & visualize; no autonomous rule revision).
