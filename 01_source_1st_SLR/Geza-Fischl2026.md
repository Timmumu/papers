## Abstract ^sec-abstract

; Purpose ; The implementation of the European Union's Level(s) framework at Level 3 – focused on in-use performance – is hindered by fragmented tools and a lack of integrated digital workflows. To bridge this gap, this study proposes and develops a prototype Collaborative Digital Platform (CDP) designed specifically to address Level 3 requirements by integrating Building Information Modeling (BIM), Internet of Things (IoT), Digital Twin and Artificial Intelligence (AI) components. ; ; ; Design/methodology/approach ; Employing a Design Science Research methodology, the study conceptualizes, architects and demonstrates a functional CDP prototype. The platform is built using a modular, openBIM-based architecture to enable the merging of static design data with dynamic operational data streams. ; ; ; Findings ; The developed prototype successfully demonstrates the technical feasibility of integrating BIM, simulated IoT sensor data and a rule-based AI interface within a unified dashboard. It provides a proof-of-concept workflow for visualizing key Level 3 indicators – specifically operational energy use, carbon emissions and indoor environmental quality – in near real time. The findings highlight the platform's potential to support data-informed monitoring and enhance transparency for sustainability reporting. ; ; ; Practical implications ; The study provides a replicable architectural template and a functional prototype, demonstrated via a tutorial residential building case study. This foundational work offers a concrete starting point for developers and researchers aiming to build digital tools for Level(s) compliance and establishes a basis for future pilot deployments, empirical validation and the development of automated compliance-checking features. ; ; ; Originality/value ; The primary contribution of this research is a novel, problem-specific digital architecture designed explicitly to operationalize the reporting requirements of the EU Level(s) framework at Level 3. While the constituent technologies are established, their purposeful synthesis into a cohesive platform blueprint addresses a distinct gap in translating policy-led sustainability assessment into practical, integrated digital workflows. ;

## Introduction & Background ^sec-intro

Implementing the EU Level(s) framework at Level 3 (in-use performance) is hindered by fragmented tools and a lack of integrated digital workflows. The study proposes a prototype Collaborative Digital Platform (CDP) integrating BIM, IoT, Digital Twin, and AI to address Level 3 requirements.

## Method ^sec-method

Using Design Science Research, the study conceptualises, architects, and demonstrates a functional CDP prototype built on a modular, openBIM-based architecture that merges static design data with dynamic operational data streams.

## Key Findings ^sec-results

The prototype demonstrates technical feasibility of integrating BIM, simulated IoT sensor data, and a rule-based AI interface in a unified dashboard, visualising key Level 3 indicators (operational energy, carbon emissions, indoor environmental quality) in near real time to support data-informed monitoring and sustainability reporting.

## Discussion & Implications ^sec-discussion

Relevant to the *BIM+IoT+DT+AI integration* and building-performance-reporting strands, with an openBIM, standards-aligned (EU Level(s)) architecture template. A proof-of-concept rather than a validated deployment.
