# Vo, T.D.K et al. (2026) — Camera-relocalization-driven monocular 3D safety monitoring system for crane operations

- **ID:** R239 | **Source:** Scopus | **DOI:** 10.1016/j.autcon.2026.107057
- **Journal:** Automation in Construction
- **Authors:** Vo T.D.K.; Jeon Y.; Pham T.-D.; Aung P.P.W.; Ko D.; Park S.

## Abstract summary
Construction remains one of the most hazardous industries, with tower crane operations posing significant risks under limited visibility and dynamic site conditions. This paper proposes a calibration-free vision-based framework for safety-oriented 3D localization using crane-mounted monocular cameras. The D2S-MDE framework recovers metric-scale 3D worker positions from a single RGB image by enforcing geometric consistency between camera relocalization and monocular depth estimation. Sparse metric depth anchors from scene coordinate regression are used to align dense monocular depth, enabling robust metric depth reconstruction under dynamic viewpoints. The estimated 3D positions are integrated into a BIM-aligned digital twin in NVIDIA Omniverse for real-time safety monitoring and risk assessment.
