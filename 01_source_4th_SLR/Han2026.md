**Key evidence (AG):** Full-cycle municipal pipeline DT platform (Shanghai Oriental Hub): BIM0–BIM6 full-phase connection + lightweight BIM-GIS, 28 IoT sensing nodes (water pressure, gas leakage, surface temp, <1 s delay) with 5-month pilot; addresses planning collision risks, construction rework, and delayed leakage detection; AI risk model predicted a valve aging risk 7 days in advance (verified on-site); 36.4% O&M cost reduction. One-way IoT→platform monitoring/decision support, no actuation and no self-improvement loop.

*Full-text source: 04c-port*

**AH — strength & gap filled:** Fills the gap between macro city-level twin frameworks and single-phase pipeline platforms with a full-cycle BIM0 to BIM6 plus lightweight BIM-GIS architecture for the Shanghai Oriental Hub; a 5-month pilot deployed 28 IoT nodes (pressure, gas leakage, surface temperature) with under 1 s delay, raising planning collision detection from 68 to 97 percent and predicting one valve aging risk 7 days in advance.

**AI — deficiencies & future work:** The pilot covers only water supply and gas pipelines, lacking power and communication monitoring modules, and its data is not yet connected to Pudong Airport systems for cross-node emergency linkage; multi-type pipeline adaptability and cross-departmental data interface standards are stated as future work.
