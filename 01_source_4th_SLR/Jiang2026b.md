## Abstract summary
Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) can accelerate Building Information Modeling (BIM) and digital-twin workflows, but probabilistic outputs and manipulated inputs create data-integrity risks that conventional construction-software liability rules do not fully explain. This study develops a reproducible doctrinal and comparative-law method that maps the GenAI value chain onto the Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (AEC) lifecycle. Its principal advantage over single-actor product or professional-negligence analyses is a qualitative proportional-liability assessment framework that evaluates technical control, foreseeability, verification capacity, causal contribution, and evidence preservation across developers, deployers, and professional users.
