## Abstract summary
With the acceleration of global informatization, digital twin (DT) technology is gradually infiltrating into all fields of urban infrastructure construction. Technologies based on CAD (Computer aided design) and VR (Virtual reality) provide strong support for DT cities, and provide new ideas for realizing sustainable development and intelligent governance of cities. Based on CAD and VR, this article discusses the implementation method and framework of infrastructure construction in DT cities. A 3D city modeling algorithm based on CAD and VR is proposed. Through the DT modeling and real-time monitoring of urban infrastructure, intelligent management and efficient operation of urban infrastructure can be realized, and the efficiency and safety of urban operation can be improved.
