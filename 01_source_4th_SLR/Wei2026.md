## Abstract summary
Efficient, timely and accurate calibration of digital twins is critical for their application in real-time decision-making and condition monitoring. Digital twins, as dynamic virtual representations of connected physical systems, require continuous recalibration to adapt to evolving system states, environmental changes, and degradation over time. Without timely and effective recalibration, discrepancies between the digital and physical counterparts accumulate, potentially leading to compromised safety and operational inefficiencies. This study addresses these challenges by introducing the Online Adaptive Reinforcement Learning and Approximate Bayesian Computation (OARL-ABC) framework, a novel and model-agnostic calibration algorithm.
