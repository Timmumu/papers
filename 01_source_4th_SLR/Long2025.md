## Abstract summary
This study proposes an AI-powered digital twin (DT) platform designed to support real-time traffic risk prediction, decision-making, and sustainable mobility in smart cities. The system integrates multi-source data—including static infrastructure maps, historical traffic records, telematics data, and camera feeds—into a unified cyber–physical platform. AI models are employed for data fusion, anomaly detection, and predictive analytics. In particular, the platform incorporates telematics–video fusion for enhanced trajectory accuracy and LiDAR–camera fusion for high-definition work-zone mapping. These capabilities support dynamic safety heatmaps, congestion forecasts, and scenario-based decision support.
