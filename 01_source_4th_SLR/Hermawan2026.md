## Abstract summary
Software quality assurance is pivotal in safety-critical domains such as railway systems, where failures could have catastrophic consequences. In this context, the train control and management system, which enables communication and control across multiple subsystems (such as doors and information panels) within a modern train, and its software must undergo rigorous validation. Alstom Rail Sweden AB employs a digital twin infrastructure to simulate and validate train control and management system software. While this setup improves system-level testing, root-cause analysis of test failures remains a manual, time-consuming bottleneck. In this study, we explore the potential of large language models to automate root cause analysis by interpreting test execution logs generated during digital twin-based testing.
