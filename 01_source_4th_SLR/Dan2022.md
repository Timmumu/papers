## Abstract summary
Bridges play an important role in transportation infrastructure systems. Intelligent and digital management of bridges group is an essential part of the future intelligent transportation infrastructure system. This paper proposes a digital twin system for bridges group in the regional transportation infrastructure network, which is interconnected by measured traffic loads. In physical space, a full-bridge traffic load monitoring system based on information fusion of weigh-in-motion (WIM) and multi-source heterogeneous machine vision is set up on the target bridge to measure traffic loads, also lightweight sensors are employed on the bridges group for structural response information. **Key evidence (AG):** Digital twin system for a regional bridges group (Shanghai, Tongji U): physical space - WIM + multi-source machine vision traffic-load monitoring on target bridge + lightweight sensors on bridge group; digital space - mechanical analysis models per bridge linked by measured traffic loads for working-condition perception and safety warning across the regional network; preliminarily implemented in a Shanghai bridges group. One-way monitoring to analysis to warning for operators; no actuation and no learning loop.

*Full-text source: 04c-port*

**AH — strength & gap filled:** Fills the group-level gap in bridge digital twins: for a regional bridge network around Shanghai, weigh-in-motion and multi-source machine vision fusion measure full-bridge traffic loads on a target bridge, lightweight sensors capture responses on group bridges, and measured loads drive mechanical models in digital space, enabling working-condition perception and safety warning across the whole network, demonstrated with Monte Carlo and Intelligent Driver Model simulations on the Yongjiang cable-stayed bridge.

**AI — deficiencies & future work:** One-way implementation: digital models are loaded with measured traffic loads but no feedback or control channel back to physical bridges exists, and deriving bridge health status from simulation results to form more interaction tasks between the two spaces is left as future exploration.
