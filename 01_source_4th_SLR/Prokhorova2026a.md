## Abstract summary
This study investigates the process of continuous monitoring and control of the technological process parameters at a nuclear power plant power unit using a digital twin model implemented on the basis of a system-cluster approach. The task addressed relates to the lack of a comprehensive, integrated system for continuous monitoring of the technical condition of the power unit, capable of collecting, processing, and analyzing information from sensors and diagnostic subsystems in real time. It is proposed to model the state of the technological equipment at a power unit using a digital twin and a system-cluster approach. Within the framework of the study, a system-cluster architecture of a digital twin was designed, which reflects the interaction among the physical, analytical, and control levels of the power unit at a nuclear power plant.
