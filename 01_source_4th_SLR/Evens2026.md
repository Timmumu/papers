## Abstract summary
Heat pumps are a key technology for decarbonising the building sector and for supporting the electricity grid through the provision of energy flexibility services. Regarding the latter aspect, accurate heat pump control is essential. However, most available models operate at time steps above fifteen minutes and do not reach the energy flexibility targets set by the energy management system. Achieving high control accuracy is challenging due to internal control strategies set by manufacturers to ensure safety and reliability, which can generally not be bypassed and significantly affect operation. Developing a digital twin, capable of incorporating these internal strategies is therefore crucial.
