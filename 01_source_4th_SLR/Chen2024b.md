## Abstract summary
The increasing importance of mineral resources in contemporary society is becoming more prominent, playing an indispensable and crucial role in the global economy. These resources not only provide essential raw materials for the global economic system but also play an irreplaceable role in supporting the development of modern industry, technology, and infrastructure. With the rapid development of intelligent technologies such as Industry 5.0 and advanced Large Language Models (LLMs), the mining industry is facing unprecedented opportunities and challenges. The development of smart mines has become a crucial direction for industry progress. This article aims to explore the strategic requirements for the development of smart mines by combining advanced products or technologies such as Chat-GPT (one of the successful applications of LLMs), digital twins, and scenario engineering.
