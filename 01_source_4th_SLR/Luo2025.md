## Abstract summary
Urban Digital Twins (UDTs) offer a promising avenue for advancing sustainable urban development by mirroring physical environments and complex urban dynamics. Such technology enables urban planners to predict and analyze the impacts of various urban scenarios, addressing a global priority for sustainable urban environments. However, their potential in public engagement for environmental perception remains unfulfilled, with existing research lacking the capability to analyze urbanscapes' visual features and predict public perceptions based on photo-realistic renderings. To fill the gap, our study developed and implemented a UDT platform designed for the dual purposes of objective feature evaluation and subjective visual perception, alongside the prediction of perceptions in simulated scenarios.
