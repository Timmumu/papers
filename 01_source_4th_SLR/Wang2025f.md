## Abstract summary
Ship damage control (DC) is pivotal to platform survivability in the face of battle damage and severe accidents. The DC context features multi-hazard coupling among flooding, fire, and smoke, as well as fast system dynamics and intensive human–machine collaboration, demanding real-time predictive simulation and decision support. Conventional DC simulations fall short in multiphysics fidelity, predictive speed, and integration with onboard sensing and control. A digital twin (DT) framework for predictive shipboard DC is introduced with an explicit capability envelope, observability, and latency requirements, and a cyber-physical mapping to ship systems.
