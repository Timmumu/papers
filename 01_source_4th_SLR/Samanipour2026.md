## Abstract summary
The automotive industry is undergoing a significant transformation toward software-defined vehicles (SDVs), in which software systems govern the driving experience, enabling vehicles to evolve beyond traditional mechanical functions. The adaptive reconfigurable SDV (ARSDV), introduced in this paper, is an SDV that autonomously adjusts or replaces its software modules based on current environmental, vehicular, traffic, and infrastructural conditions. The goal is to improve overall driving utility, measured in terms of safety, energy consumption, resource demand, and travel time.
