# Tan, J.-K et al. (2026) — Shear capacity design method for vertical joints in wind turbine concrete towers under combined bending-shear loads

## Abstract summary
Wind turbine concrete towers are frequently subjected to complex stress states involving compression, bending, shear, and torsion during normal operation. To investigate the failure mechanisms and root causes of vertical joint degradation under bending-shear loads, this study performs a comprehensive failure analysis through combined experimental and computational approaches. Four vertical joint specimens were designed for push tests, followed by a systematic analysis of their failure modes and load-displacement curves. The results reveal that interface debonding between the epoxy and concrete, together with shear failure within the concrete layers, constitutes the primary failure mechanism at the joint interface.
