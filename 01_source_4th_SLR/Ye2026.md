## Abstract summary
Fatigue cracks are prone to initiate in rib-to-deck (RTD) weld joints of orthotropic steel decks (OSD) under traffic loading, posing a serious threat to functionality and safety of bridge structures. To achieve accurate fatigue crack life prediction of RTD weld joints of OSD, a digital twin-based multiscale fatigue crack life prediction framework from microcrack initiation and propagation to macrocrack propagation is proposed. Eccentric tensile fatigue tests as a physical entity are first conducted to obtain test data on fatigue crack propagation. A multiscale crack propagation model coupling crystal plasticity finite element model with fracture mechanics is then established as a virtual entity to describe microcrack initiation and propagation as well as macrocrack propagation.
