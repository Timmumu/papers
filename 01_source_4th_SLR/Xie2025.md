## Abstract summary
Complex dynamics inherent of building fire poses big challenges to firefighting and rescue, especially with limited access to critical fire-hazard information. This work proposes the novel AIoT-integrated Digital Twin for the full-scale multi-floor building to manage the dynamics fire information. This system allows for super real-time mapping of actual building fires into accurate and concise digital fire scene at the cloud platform. By developing the ADLSTM-Fire model, we effectively transform discrete sensor-array data into high-dimensional spatiotemporal temperature fields in real-time, and furthermore, forecast future fire development and hazardous regions 60 s in advance. **Key evidence (AG):** AIoT-powered building digital twin for smart firefighting and super real-time fire forecast (PolyU): AIoT-integrated DT maps building fires to digital fire scene; ADLSTM-Fire model transforms discrete sensor-array data into high-dimensional spatiotemporal temperature fields; forecasts fire development and hazardous regions 60s in advance; validated via full-scale building fire experiment; 93% reconstruction accuracy, 92% forecast accuracy. One-way monitoring→forecast→firefighting support; no actuation and no learning loop (offline-trained).

*Full-text source: 04c-port*

**AH — strength & gap filled:** Bridges the gap between sparse on-site sensor readings and comprehensive understanding of building fire dynamics: an AIoT-integrated digital twin with LoRa wireless thermometry whose ADLSTM-Fire model reconstructs the full spatiotemporal temperature field from discrete sensor-array data (R2 93%) and forecasts fire development 60 s ahead (R2 92%), validated against CFD benchmarks and full-scale building fire experiments.

**AI — deficiencies & future work:** The model is offline-trained on numerical simulation data and the advance forecast is less precise than reconstruction, with up to 18% error in one experimental scenario; future work refines ADLSTM-Fire for unfamiliar buildings, adds building layout as an input parameter, enriches datasets with structural and wind factors, optimizes sensor layouts, and tests more building types.
