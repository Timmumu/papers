## Abstract summary
Shield attitude control is vital for tunnel safety, yet existing single-scene models lack generalization and real-time adaptability across diverse geologies. Herein, a digital twin (DT)-driven intelligent control framework integrating the improved black kite algorithm (IBKA), natural gradient boosting (NGBoost), and NSGA-III is proposed to achieve automated, optimal attitude management. Using a Wuhan metro underpass as a case study, the framework’s performance was validated across typical geological conditions.
