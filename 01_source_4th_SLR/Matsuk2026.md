## Abstract summary
The object of research is the process of assessing the level of safety of complex technical systems of critical infrastructure under conditions of uncertainty. The problem of the limitations and asymmetry of risk assessment methods was investigated. Risk assessment processes were studied based on IAEA data, using a combination of theoretical and computational modeling methods. The theoretical basis was based on factor risk analysis. Dynamic and temporal dependencies were taken into account using a synthesized modular scalable dynamic Bayesian network (MSDBN), which integrated local components and their interaction into hierarchical models. Probabilistic assessments were performed using Monte Carlo simulation, as well as structural and hybrid learning algorithms for Bayesian networks.
