## Abstract summary
Power asset management is a critical public safety service to ensure electrical networks' reliability, efficiency, and resilience. Traditional approaches, relying on periodic inspections and reactive main-tenance, often leave the grid vulnerable to failures, costly outages, and operational inefficiencies. Adopting digital twin (DT) technology has introduced a paradigm shift by enabling real-time monitoring, predictive maintenance, and network optimization through virtual replicas of physical assets. However, the effectiveness of DTs is highly dependent on the timely availability of high-quality data, which remains a major challenge. In parallel, recent generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) advancements have demonstrated significant potential in data generation, predictive modeling, and decision support.
