# Meng, F et al. (2024) — Research on the prediction method of string-supported dome cable force based on digital twin technology

- **Journal:** Applied Mathematics and Nonlinear Sciences
- **Authors:** Meng F.; Yu S.; Xue J.

## Abstract summary
To improve the accuracy of rope force prediction and the safety of the construction process. In this paper, based on digital twin technology in the design process, digital twins of physical entities in virtual space are constructed using digital to achieve digital control and optimization of physical entities. The structural response information collected by sensors is brought into the dynamic load identification method by calculating the solution method of the dynamic load of the digital twin technology. The damping effect is considered in the load vector. The displacement is eliminated by constructing an average loss function to disperse the motion time to solve for the dynamic load imposed on the structure and obtain the load-bearing state of the structure for dome cable force prediction.
