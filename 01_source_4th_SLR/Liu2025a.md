## Abstract summary
Cracks in railway tunnel linings pose significant safety risks and demand timely, condition-based maintenance. While LiDAR point clouds offer high-resolution structural data, crack detection remains challenging due to severe class imbalance, and interference from surface contamination, construction joints, and other non-crack features. This study presents PointCrackNet, a novel deep learning framework for accurate crack segmentation in LiDAR-acquired railway tunnel point clouds. PointCrackNet integrates Dynamic Graph Convolution, Graph Attention, and Spectral Convolution with a specialized crack-enhancement attention module, capturing fine-scale crack details while preserving global structural context.
