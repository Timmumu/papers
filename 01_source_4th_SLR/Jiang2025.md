## Abstract summary
Structural safety assessment is an important means to ensure the safe operation and maintenance of civil engineering structures, However, conventional planned maintenance strategies suffer from a low degree of informatization and rely on experience for decision-making. Thus, this paper presents a digital twin-driven structural safety assessment method applicable to civil engineering structures during the operation and maintenance phase. Firstly, according to the structural service scenarios, the digital twin framework with 5 levels of structural safety assessment is proposed, and the functions of each part are elaborated in details. Secondly, three key technologies for the construction of digital twin models and multimodal twin data, a data interaction network and a structural safety assessment method are summarized.
