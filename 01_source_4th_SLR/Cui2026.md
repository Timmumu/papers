## Abstract summary
The use of artificial intelligence (AI) in high-rise building rectification is emerging as an essential way to address structural issues. This study explores how AI technologies are used in lifting, grouting, and reinforcement to enhance safety, efficiency, and sustainability in structural realignment practices. The PRISMA-ScR framework was utilized for conducting a scoping study published over the last seven years on AI-driven advancements in grouting, lifting reinforcement, and structural health monitoring (SHM) technologies. Furthermore, thematic analysis was employed to determine the recent developments, barriers encountered along the way, and future directions using data from major academic databases.
