## Abstract summary
The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) is revolutionising transportation by connecting vehicles, infrastructure and devices, enabling more intelligent and safer mobility. One key challenge is ensuring efficient and secure communication among vehicles with varying capabilities, including different sizes, speeds and sensor configurations. This research introduces a Federated Learning-Driven Deep Learning (FLDL) approach to intelligent collision avoidance, designed to address the heterogeneity of vehicles in the IoV ecosystem. The system integrates real-time data from vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) and vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) communications, while considering factors like vehicle type, road conditions, driver behaviour and Digital Twins.
