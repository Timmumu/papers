## Abstract summary
Artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly being explored as a decision-support layer in process safety across chemical, energy, and manufacturing industries. Conventional safety approaches, often based on deterministic analyses, retrospective incident reviews, and static risk metrics, can be limited in highly interconnected and dynamically changing industrial systems. This review provides a structured qualitative synthesis of literature on AI applications relevant to process safety in high-risk industrial environments, with emphasis on machine learning (ML), deep learning (DL), surrogate modeling, and safety-oriented digital twins for hazard identification, dynamic risk assessment, fault diagnosis, predictive maintenance, emergency response, and safety management.
