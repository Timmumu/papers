## Abstract summary
In order to improve the safety and reliability of nuclear reactors, and to enhance the digitalization and intelligence level of operation and maintenance management, a digital twin technology architecture for nuclear reactor intelligent operation and maintenance was proposed based on a conceptual analysis of nuclear reactor digital twin technology. Based on the technical implementation framework elements and their mutual relationships, the research on digital twins in nuclear reactor operation management scenarios can establish a process, and from the perspective of technical implementation, based on the number of twin intelligence operations, applications are analyzed.
