## Abstract summary
Urban transport systems are increasingly susceptible to cascading failures, necessitating advanced methods for risk identification and mitigation. This paper presents a multi-modal risk embedding framework integrating ℓ2,1-regularized feature selection, spatiotemporal transformer encoding, and spatially-constrained spectral clustering for assessing vulnerability and structuring resilient communities within the transport networks of Singapore and Beijing. By fusing data from urban sensor networks - including traffic flow, structural health, and environmental metrics - into a unified 460-dimensional representation, the framework identifies and classifies cross-modal vulnerability patterns across structural, operational, and environmental dimensions.
