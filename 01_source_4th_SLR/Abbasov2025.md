## Abstract summary
The lifetime and effectiveness of transport system components are greatly impacted by thermomechanical stress, which results from the interaction of mechanical stresses and temperature variations. Overstress increases maintenance expenses and safety hazards by causing material fatigue, distortion, and breakdowns. In order to improve stress prediction and mitigation in moving transport components, this study presents an AI-driven digital twin platform. By using finite difference-based thermal stress simulations, Bayesian inference for real-time stress refinement, and Monte Carlo simulations for probabilistic stress distribution, the suggested model reduces stress uncertainty by 30% and increases component lifespan by 25% through optimized predictive maintenance.
