## Abstract summary
The Longdong region of China is endowed with abundant resources such as coal, oil, and natural gas. Longdong Comprehensive Energy and Chemical Industry Base is in the stage of high-quality development, which plays an important role in the realization of the strategic goal of strong industry in Gansu. Based on data retrieved from the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (Gansu), the Gansu Provincial Tax Service of the State Taxation Administration, and relevant local government departments, we compiled enterprise information for Qingyang and Pingliang cities as of March 2025.
