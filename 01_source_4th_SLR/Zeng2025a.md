## Abstract summary
Managing high-density pedestrian flows in urban public spaces via Information Technologies (IT) is crucial for safety and efficiency. Despite advancements in sensing, AI-driven prediction, and control, a critical gap persists: lacking the systematic integration needed for robust automated crowd management systems, an issue intensified by AI/IoT growth. To address this challenge, a comprehensive review of the literature from 2014 to 2024 has been conducted, analyzing and synthesizing IT-driven decision support approaches for automated crowd management.
