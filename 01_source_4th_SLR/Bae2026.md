## Abstract summary
Background: Hospital IT outages severely disrupt clinical workflows and use of electronic medical records, threatening patient safety and operational continuity. Traditional disaster response training faces limitations, including high resource requirements, restricted repeatability, and inability to be conducted without interrupting 24/7 hospital operations. Digital twin technology enables realistic, repeatable simulation training in virtual environments, avoiding operational disruption. Objective: This study developed and implemented a digital twin–based virtual hospital platform for level 1 IT outage disaster response training and evaluated its feasibility through quantitative performance metrics and participant survey feedback.
