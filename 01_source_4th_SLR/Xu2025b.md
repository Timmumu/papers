# Xu, L et al. (2025) — Automation in manufacturing and assembly of industrialised construction

- **ID:** R920 | **Source:** Web of Science | **DOI:** 10.1016/j.autcon.2024.105945
- **Journal:** AUTOMATION IN CONSTRUCTION
- **Authors:** Xu, L; Zou, Y; Lu, YQ; Chang-Richards, A

## Abstract summary
The integration of automation technologies has improved the efficiency of industrialised construction (IC), yet a deeper understanding of their effects on the manufacturing and assembly stages remains necessary. This paper provides a systematic review of how various automation technologies influence these key stages in IC, analysing 53 articles. It explores the deployment of 22 technologies, including the Internet of Things (IoT), deep learning, digital twins, and robotics, and identifies seven benefits for IC: (1) interoperability, (2) scheduling optimisation, (3) production traceability, (4) production safety, (5) manufacturability, (6) quality assurance, and (7) constructability.
