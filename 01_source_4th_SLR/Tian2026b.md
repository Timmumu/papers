## Abstract summary
Digital twin technology has emerged as a promising paradigm for advancing instrumentation and measurement in dam safety monitoring. However, existing models often face significant cross-domain discrepancies between numerical simulations (source domain) and in-situ measurement data (target domain), which constrain prediction accuracy and generalization under varying operating conditions. To address these challenges, this study proposes a novel cross-domain adaptive digital twin framework that integrates graph attention networks, gating mechanisms, and feature alignment strategies to enhance measurement consistency and predictive reliability. Multi-source numerical simulation domains are systematically constructed to enrich input variability and improve measurement-domain coverage.
