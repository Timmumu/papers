# Su, D et al. (2025) — Real-time attitude control of large rectangular pipe-jacking machines using a mechanics-informed digital twin

- **ID:** R464 | **Source:** Scopus | **DOI:** 10.1016/j.autcon.2025.106373
- **Journal:** Automation in Construction
- **Authors:** Su D.; Chen J.; Chen P.; Wang L.; Zhou J.; Chen X.

## Abstract summary
Traditional experience-based methods for controlling the attitude of pipe-jacking machines frequently result in notable deviations from the planned trajectory, posing risks such as excessive jacking forces and structural damage to segments. Achieving precise control of machine attitudes is vital for large-section pipe-jacking projects. This paper presents a mechanics-informed digital twin framework designed to reduce trajectory deviations by leveraging intelligent control systems. A dynamic control platform processes diverse real-time data from construction operations, issues immediate risk alerts and suggest corrective forces derived from a theoretical load model.
