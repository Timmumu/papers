## Abstract summary
Thermal buckling of railway tracks is a critical safety concern during hot weather and is expected to become more severe under a warming climate. Reliable rail-temperature forecasting is therefore essential for proactive railway operation. However, existing forecasting approaches are often limited by short prediction horizons, insufficient accuracy, or dependence on inputs unavailable for real-time operation. To address these limitations, this study proposes a foundation-model-based rail-temperature forecasting framework using TimeGPT-1, a pretrained time-series foundation model adapted through lightweight fine-tuning. The model uses routinely available meteorological variables, including air temperature, solar radiation, sunshine duration, relative humidity, wind speed, and rainfall, to predict rail temperature up to six hours ahead.
