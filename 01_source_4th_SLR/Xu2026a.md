## Abstract summary
Interlayer bond strength is critical for ensuring the safety and durability of 3D-printed concrete (3DPC) structures. However, there remains a lack of real-time prediction methods addressing interlayer performance under the combined effects of interval time and environmental factors during the in situ printing process. To address this issue, this study conducted experiments considering various printing interval times and environmental conditions, incorporating monitoring of dielectric constant and water evaporation, alongside interlayer splitting tensile tests.
