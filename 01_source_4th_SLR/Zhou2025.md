## Abstract summary
In the realm of marine resource exploration and exploitation, umbilical cables are indispensable as the exclusive pathway for the transmission of energy and signals towards underwater systems. The safe operation of these cables is paramount to the safety and reliability of the entire explorat ion or exploitation equipment. The predominant safe risks of umbilical cables stem from suboptimal configuration, which makes the real-time configuration inversion of the cables very necessary. Despite the importance, due to limitations in model accuracy and computational complexity, traditional mechanical analysis methods are unable to provide reliable real-time simulations of umbilical cables' configuration.
