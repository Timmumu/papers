## Abstract summary
To address the limited perceptual range of physical sensors in the digital twins of engineering structures, a smart virtual sensing technology based on an Al-enhanced reduced-order model (AI-ROM) is proposed. This technology integrates multi-source physical sensor data to predict the global response of complex structures in real time. Methodologically, the structural. response is represented as a linear combination of bases using reduced-order modeling theory. This formulation transforms virtual sensing into the optimization of the combination coefficients constrained by the measured response data. Accordingly, a deep-learning loss function capable of handling multi-source physical virtual sensing was constructed.
