## Abstract summary
Driven by Industry 4.0, the construction of reservoir dams is entering a new phase characterized by both opportunities and challenges. The deep integration of technologies such as artificial intelligence, big data, and digital twins aims to enhance the safety, quality, efficiency, and sustainability of dam projects. This paper utilizes VOSviewer analysis software to conduct a bibliometric analysis of intelligent construction technologies within the field of reservoir dams. The research findings reveal current trends in this field, providing an in-depth examination of global publication patterns, national contributions, and keyword co-occurrence.
