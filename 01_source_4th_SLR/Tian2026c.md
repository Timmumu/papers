## Abstract summary
Advancing the intelligent development of mines is a crucial pathway to enhance the safety, efficiency, and green development of the coal industry. As a new-generation information technology, digital twin technology provides important support for the intelligent transformation in the field of fully-mechanized coal mining equipment. Therefore, a review study on the digital twin technology of fully-mechanized coal mining equipment was carried out. First, the fundamental concepts and core principles of digital twins are elucidated. Building upon this foundation, the review focuses on the current application landscape within the mechanical engineering sector.
