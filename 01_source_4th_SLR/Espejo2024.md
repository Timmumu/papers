# Espejo, F et al. (2024) — Low-cost “buffer” structural measure for flooding risk reduction in irrigation reservoirs

## Abstract summary
Irrigation reservoirs (IRs) largely located off-stream are the great forgotten hydraulic infrastructures in comparison with the deeply studied dams. This research is mainly aimed at reducing the risk associated with IR-Breach floods in irrigation ponds through a novel and low-cost structural measure, named “buffer”, which is in the risk element. This guarantees a maximum flood lamination at a breaching event as well as the minimization hydrograph peak. Therefore, a reduction of the potential hazard associated to these undesired events is produced. Methodology starts with the identification of flood hazard generating causes; then, a detailed/comprehensive numerical hydraulic simulation framework is performed through synthetic models of IRs with an analysis based on breaching events and slope “buffer” thresholds.
