## Abstract summary
The construction of modern intelligent transportation infrastructure has brought many inconveniences to transportation due to its large number of vehicles, high traffic density. This article applies digital twins to intelligent transportation devices in the internet of vehicles, and studies and analyses the composition of intelligent transportation IoT systems and the application of digital twins in intelligent transportation. First, the intelligent transportation equipment of different vehicles was tested, and then the digital twin intelligent transportation equipment was used to configure resources. The results showed that the average satisfaction score of the intelligent transportation equipment improved by digital twin technology was 7.73 points, while the average satisfaction score of traditional intelligent transportation equipment was 8.26 points, an increase of about 6.9%.
