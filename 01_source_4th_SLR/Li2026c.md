## Abstract summary
The exploitation of low-altitude airspace in urban areas has given rise to the concept of low-altitude economy (LAE). However, urban aviation may face safety challenges due to the complex environment caused by dense buildings. Therefore, a customized infrastructure that provides ubiquitous and reliable communication in the three-dimensional (3D) urban region is essential for efficient aircraft orchestration. This necessitates global and accurate radio environment information acquisition. As one of the promising technologies for the sixth generation (6G) system, channel knowledge map (CKM) can provide a priori location-specific channel knowledge for further communication performance improvement.
