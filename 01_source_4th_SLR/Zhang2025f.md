## Abstract summary
Cable-supported bridges are critical civil infrastructure. With increasing traffic volume and extreme weather conditions, these bridges face threats to their functional and structural safety during their service life, necessitating timely health monitoring and condition assessment. In recent years, digital twin (DT) has achieved significant progress in bridge monitoring and assessment, effectively addressing the shortcomings of traditional methods. However, current bridge DTs, especially those for cable-supported bridges, still face a series of issues, such as ambiguous concepts and lack of unified standards.
