# Shao, C et al. (2026) — Lightweight graph retrieval-augmented generation agent for safety monitoring digital twins in small- and medium-scale hydropower projects

- **Authors:** Shao C.; Qin X.; Li X.; Yang M.; Yuan D.; Zheng S.

## Abstract summary
Intelligent agent assistants are becoming essential for efficient interaction in safety monitoring digital twin (DT) systems. However, their deployment in small- and medium-scale hydropower projects remains limited by complex modules, inefficient interaction, and constrained computing resources. To bridge this gap, this paper proposes a lightweight agent assistant for DT systems that integrates graph retrieval-augmented generation (GraphRAG)-enabled task-oriented context construction with Gemma-based large language model (LLM) response generation. Graph-constrained automatic speech recognition (ASR) correction improves intent recognition and hands-free operation. Additionally, a conflict resolution and dynamic update strategy supports lightweight knowledge graph (KG) maintenance.
