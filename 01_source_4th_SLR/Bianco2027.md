## Abstract summary
In the context of climate change, robust operational strategies are required to mitigate water supply depletion and ensure the reliable operation of decentralized water infrastructure systems. Rainwater Harvesting, Treatment and Reuse Systems (RHTRSs) are a feasible option, especially for non-drinking purposes, but their effectiveness depends on maintaining service continuity and compliance with water quality standards under uncertain environmental and operating conditions. In this study, a reliability-oriented Digital Twin (DT) was developed and implemented to simulate the RHTRS of a commercial building, combining the Building Information Modeling (BIM)-based geometry, hydrological study, physical-chemical treatment, Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average (ARIMA) modeling, and an immersive Mixed Reality (MR) interface.
