# Fikri, A.A et al. (2025) — Sensor Fusion of Laser and Inertial Units with Kalman-KMeans-Fuzzy Framework for Real-Time Railway Geometry Monitoring

## Abstract summary
Maintaining railway track geometry integrity is essential to ensuring transportation safety and predictive maintenance. Conventional manual inspection methods are limited by low sampling frequency, subjective interpretation, and delayed anomaly detection. This study introduces a real-time, embedded monitoring system using VL53L0X infrared laser sensors and an MPU6050 IMU to measure gauge, cross-level height, and inclination. Sensors are mounted on a lightweight aluminum trolley and sampled every 0.5 seconds using an Arduino-based platform. A Kalman Filter reduces measurement noise, with tuned covariance matrices based on field calibration. Filtered outputs are clustered via K-Means (K = 2), validated by the Elbow Method and Silhouette Score (>0.6).
