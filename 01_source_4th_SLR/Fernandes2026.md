## Abstract summary
Recent projections by the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change indicate that global warming will turn permanent and further intensify the severity and frequency of extreme weather events (heat waves, rain, and intense droughts), with coastal regions being the most vulnerable to extreme events. Therefore, the risk of natural disasters and the associated regional impacts on water, food, energy, social, and health security represents one of the world’s greatest challenges of this century. However, conventional methodologies for monitoring these regions during extreme events are usually not available to managers and decision-makers with the necessary urgency.
