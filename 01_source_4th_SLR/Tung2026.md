## Abstract summary
Water systems increasingly face hydro-climatic non-stationarity, extreme events, and water-quality deterioration that reduce the effectiveness of static infrastructure-based management. This systematic-integrative review examines how digital technologies can support climate-resilient water management by converting real-time information into operational decisions and adaptive control. Searches of Web of Science and Scopus identified 400 records; 120 full-text articles were assessed and 76 studies were included.
