## Abstract summary
Lithium-ion batteries have been rapidly developed as clean energy sources in many industrial fields, such as new energy vehicles and energy storage. The core issues hindering their further promotion and application are reliability and safety. A digital twin model that maps onto the physical entity of the battery with high simulation accuracy helps to monitor internal states and improve battery safety. This work focuses on developing a digital twin model via a mechanism-data-driven parameter updating algorithm to increase the simulation accuracy of the internal and external characteristics of the full-time domain battery under complex working conditions. An electrochemical model is first developed with the consideration of how electrode particle size impacts battery characteristics.
