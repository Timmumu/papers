# Huang, L et al. (2025) — Real-time simulation for deep-sea mining system with sea trial validation

- **ID:** R248 | **Source:** Scopus | **DOI:** 10.1016/j.oceaneng.2025.122751
- **Journal:** Ocean Engineering
- **Authors:** Huang L.; Wang S.; Liu W.; Sun Y.; Li Y.; Hong X.; Xu M.; Jiang L.; Xu F.; Li Y.; Wu H.; Zhan C.

## Abstract summary
This paper proposed a real-time simulation method for the deep-sea mining system to predict dynamic responses and the spatial configuration of the umbilical cable. The information of the marine environment and system motion were monitored by different sensors, such as USBLs (Ultra short baselines), ADCP, wave radar, tension sensor. The monitoring data were used to generate the real-time simulation model by a self-developed MATLAB-OrcaFlex interaction program. It achieved the shift from local monitoring data to global response prediction. The proposed method was verified during a 3300 m deep-sea mining sea trial. Compared with the top tension obtained by the tension sensor on the winch, the predicted result agreed well.
