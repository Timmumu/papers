## Abstract summary
In this mini-review, the authors propose an extended HACCP-based preventive control framework for novel foods, including plant-based and cultured meat, to address emerging safety requirements that surpass traditional process-centered controls. Regulatory approaches among different jurisdictions were found to be characterized by substantial heterogeneity, which complicates industry compliance. Furthermore, managing non-process hazards, such as allergen cross-contact in complex supply chains or atypical risks in bioreactors (e.g., unforeseen toxicity), poses challenges regarding the capacity of conventional manual monitoring.
