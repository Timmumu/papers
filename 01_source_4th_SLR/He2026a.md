# He, Y et al. (2026) — Deep learning-based intelligent path planning system design for subsea pipeline laying

- **ID:** R427 | **Source:** Scopus | **DOI:** 10.1016/j.jpse.2025.100394
- **Journal:** Journal of Pipeline Science and Engineering
- **Authors:** He Y.; Zhang L.; Wang B.; Huang J.; Jiang B.; Chen M.; Li X.

## Abstract summary
Subsea pipeline route planning is one of the core challenges in marine engineering. Traditional methods, which rely on manual expertise and static algorithms, struggle to adapt to complex and dynamic seabed terrains, leading to prolonged decision-making cycles and high construction costs. This paper proposes an intelligent path planning system based on deep learning and multi-objective optimization. By leveraging transfer learning to extract common features from historical seabed terrain data and integrating a modified sparrow search algorithm (MSSA) with gradient constraints, the system achieves dynamic multi-objective optimization balancing route length, installation cost, and safety. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed system significantly outperforms conventional methods in planning efficiency, path quality, and cost control under complex seabed conditions.
