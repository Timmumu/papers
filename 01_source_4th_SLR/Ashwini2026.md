## Abstract summary
The rapid evolution of electric vehicles, smart grids, and energy storage infrastructures has exposed critical limitations in conventional Battery Management Systems (BMS), particularly in adaptive intelligence, privacy preservation, degradation awareness, and real-time decision autonomy. This paper proposes a novel Federated Physics-Informed Digital Twin Reinforcement Learning Battery Management System (FPD-RL BMS), an autonomous multi-layered framework that integrates Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), federated edge learning, deep reinforcement learning, and synchronized digital twin technology into a unified intelligent battery ecosystem.
