## Abstract summary
Objective: This work proposes a unified framework for causal digital twins, integrating Structural Causal Models (SCMs), the Potential Outcomes Framework, and reinforcement learning. The objective is to enable individualized counterfactual reasoning and dynamic treatment policy optimization, moving beyond prediction toward actionable, ethical, and adaptive decision support. Materials and methods: This work formalizes causal digital twins by embedding causal inference principles into digital architectures, modeling both observed and counterfactual outcomes for each patient. Individualized Treatment Effects (ITE) are estimated through causal modeling, and sequential decision-making is optimized using reinforcement learning techniques.
