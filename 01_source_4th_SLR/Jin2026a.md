## Abstract summary
The use of geological models for mine production scheduling, planning, and design is a common aspect of current digital mine construction. Establishing a mapping relationship from digital geological resources to mining process simulation and then to risk early warning, enabling real-time interaction between digital models and physical mines, is an essential component of mining digital twins and an important direction for future development. This study is based on a non-ferrous metal mine and involves the development of data interaction functionality between 3Dmine (enterprise edition) and 3DEC7.0 software. This enables data mapping between geological models and numerical models, as well as real-time 3D visualization of risk points in the geological model.
