## Abstract summary
Dynamic prediction of in-service physical fields (e.g. stress, strain, and temperature fields) constitutes a cornerstone technology for digital governance of mechanical equipment. The stochasticity and time-varying characteristics of external excitation loads (e.g. thermal, vibrational, and impact loads) introduce significant complexity in physical field prediction. Online monitoring of physical fields at the assembly interfaces of mechanical systems is critical for ensuring structural safety, extending service life, and optimizing design. This study proposes TransPhyX (Transformer-Based Physical Field Prediction with XGBoost Precoder), a hybrid data-driven framework designed to overcome these challenges.
