## Abstract summary
The increasing complexity of current system realities (e.g., pandemics, healthcare, energy transition, process industry 4.0 etc.) would require the evaluation of the actual way systems are modified, often referred with "work as done", rather than "work as imagined". The safety of a complex system is one of the emergent properties depending upon the interactions between the system's components and subsystems. This paper is focused on the analysis of the nature of the interactions within a complex system when it is subjected to cumulative stresses, crises and accidents. The objective is to identify, test and validate integrated emergency management procedures in the event of accidents, crises or major incidents occurring during the loading and unloading of goods and hazardous substances.
