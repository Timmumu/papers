## Abstract summary
Translucent polymer-concrete claddings are emerging as multifunctional building skins that merge structural performance, daylighting, and human–architecture interaction. However, the lack of integrated design frameworks coupling durability, real-time aesthetics, and long-term service-life prognosis limits their adoption. Here, we present a glass-fiber-reinforced polymer (GFRP) mesh-enhanced translucent polymer-concrete (GFRP-TRPC) panel that simultaneously serves as load-bearing façade, AI-driven light canvas, and self-sensing structural element. Epoxy-vinyl ester polymer concrete with 30 vol % recycled glass aggregate and embedded PMMA optical fibers (ϕ 1 mm) was fabricated by a one-step vacuum-assisted resin infusion process, yielding 30-mm-thin elements with 65 MPa compressive strength and 22% optical transmittance.
