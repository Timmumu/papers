## Abstract summary
Traffic simulation technologies are now an essential tool in traffic safety research, as they enable complex real-world scenarios to be reproduced in a safe and controllable virtual environment. With the growing attention to mixed traffic and human-vehicle interaction, higher requirements have been placed on real-time interaction and behavioral fidelity across diverse road users. However, most existing platforms still center on a single road-user type, while other participants are often represented as scripted agents, which makes it difficult to capture genuine human reactions and interaction feedback. To address these limitations, we propose a high-fidelity, multi-road-user traffic behavior simulation system, the Multi-Road-User Interaction Simulation Platform (MRUISP).
