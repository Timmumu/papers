## Abstract summary
Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies are rapidly transforming the Architecture, Engineering and Construction (AEC) industry. In this Special Issue, cutting-edge research results in the application of AI in AEC are collected around eight themes under the topic of "AI for the Built World". The eight key themes are: Digital Twins & AI, AIBased Decision Support, Natural Language Processing (NLP) & Remote Perception, Physics-Informed AI, Building Energy & Environment, Infrastructure Planning & Maintenance, Semantic Model Generation, and AI in Geotechnical Engineering. Across these themes, the research highlights significant advancements. NLP and Remote Perception research introduces models for AI-assisted planning, regulatory document digitalization, and advanced object detection in point clouds for applications such as Building Information Modelling (BIM) and quality assessment.
