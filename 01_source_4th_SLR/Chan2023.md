## Abstract summary
The Hilti Jaibot, a state-of-the-art construction site drilling robot, has demonstrated remarkable productivity gains while also underscoring the need for improved safety and monitoring capabilities. This study aims to address this need by harnessing Internet of Things (IoT) technologies and predictive maintenance methodologies. The proposed enhancements encompass a comprehensive sensor and camera integration to monitor the robot's environment, coupled with the development of a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) predictive maintenance algorithm to preemptively identify operational issues. These improvements enable the Jaibot to autonomously detect and mitigate risks, such as obstacles and human activity, while providing real-time safety alerts to operators.
