## Abstract summary
This study presents a comprehensive experimental and AI-based investigation into the three-point bending behavior of aramid and glass fiber-reinforced hybrid composites, considering different EKabor-II nanoparticle reinforcement levels (0 wt%, 0.5 wt%, and 1 wt%). Artificial Neural Networks (ANN), Classification Learner, and Regression Learner algorithms are comparatively applied for the first time to predict the mechanical responses of composite panels with millimeter-level precision. The ANN model, trained using Levenberg–Marquardt with 5 neurons and a learning rate of 0.013, achieved a validation MSE of 0.678 MPa and R2 of 0.9997. Meanwhile, the Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) method produced outstanding results (RMSE = 0.089 MPa, R2 = 0.9999).
