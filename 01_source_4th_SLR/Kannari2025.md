## Abstract summary
As part of the broader digitalization of society, artificial intelligence is one promising way to improve and enhance energy use in buildings. Traditionally, the building service systems have been managed with manually tuned rule-based controllers. While they are cost-efficient and simple, they might suffer from suboptimality and lack of adaptivity to the changes in the surrounding environment. Reinforcement learning (RL) is a machine learning type well-suited for control and optimization tasks. In RL, optimal control is approached through trial and error, with the goal of maximizing cumulative rewards. In simulation studies, it has shown great potential for the control and optimization of building applications.
