## Abstract summary
System-level thermo-mechanical states are essential for safety assessment and operational control of high-temperature energy equipment. However, field measurements are extremely sparse and confined to accessible components, while numerous time-dependent thermo-mechanical boundary conditions are unavailable. Inferring full-field states in real-time from highly local observations without explicitly identifying high-dimensional boundary conditions therefore remains a major challenge for digital-twin modeling. To address these challenges, this paper proposes a sparse-observation- and sparse-physics-informed system-level digital twin (SOPI-SDT), for real-time inverse inference of high-dimensional thermo-mechanical states.
