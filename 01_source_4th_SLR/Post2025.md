## Abstract summary
Edge computing and digital twin technology integration enhance hazard detection, improve safety system resilience and address cybersecurity challenges as safety infrastructure becomes more interconnected. There was a time when process safety relied almost entirely on the sharp eyes and quick reactions of seasoned workers on the factory floor. But technology is changing that equation.
