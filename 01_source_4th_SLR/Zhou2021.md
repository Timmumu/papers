## Abstract summary
In the process of building construction, traditional architectural design and construction methods take a long time. The built buildings perform poorly in terms of energy usage and energy conservation. The study expects to explore the potential safety hazards of prefabricated buildings during the construction process. On this basis, a modelling study of the construction process is carried out. The study uses Digital Twins (DTs) technology and prefabricated Building Information Modelling (BIM) to conduct in-depth modelling research on the building construction process. The prefabricated building construction system oriented to DTs technology can well solve the problems of structural damage and deformation in the production, transportation, and assembly process of building components. **Key evidence (AG):** Modelling study of prefabricated building construction process using digital twins + prefabricated BIM (Chongqing Jiaotong U): DT/prefab construction system with measuring sensors for real-time monitoring and prediction of component damage/deformation (production, transport, assembly); assembly BIM transferred to network cloud for BIM display; risk-indicator data analysed with factor analysis (Cronbach alpha 0.8-0.9, KMO, variance 65.02% cumulative) for potential safety hazards during construction. One-way monitoring/prediction→designer guidance; no actuation and no self-improvement loop (data collection is narrow — acknowledged limitation).

*Full-text source: 04c-port*

**AH — strength & gap filled:** Combines DT technology with prefabricated BIM to model prefabricated building construction, so measuring sensors monitor displacement and rotation torque during component production, transport, and assembly, and risk indicators are tracked in real time. Validation uses factor analysis on risk cases (130 valid cases, 100 percent effective, extraction values 0.75 to 0.86), filling the gap of few DT studies on prefabricated construction in smart cities.

**AI — deficiencies & future work:** The index selection in the construction process is not comprehensive enough and the data collection area is narrow, which the authors state affects universality; the work remains a modelling study without a demonstrated live sensor-to-twin data link. Future work will select multilevel data, supplement research data, and reduce the deviation of research results.
