## Abstract summary
Recently, higher education has undergone significant changes due to the use of digital technologies and innovations. The use of innovative platforms, hybrid models and online services cannot overcome the requirements of today. To solve these problems, the article proposes a hypothesis regarding the use of a digital twin of a teacher. The use of modeling, design and optimization of the pedagogical educational process based on a cyber-physical system built on mathematical modeling and synergy is suggested.
