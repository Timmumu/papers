## Abstract summary
Safety-critical energy infrastructures require high-fidelity digital twins (DTs) for reliable dynamic risk assessment. However, conventional state estimation faces trade-offs between computational efficiency and uncertainty calibration accuracy under degraded sensor data. To address this challenge, we propose a spatially selective Bayesian inference framework for real-time uncertainty quantification (UQ) in inverse heat transfer problems (IHTPs). By dynamically decoupling the physical domain into localized region-of-interest (ROI) subgraphs via graph neural networks (GNNs), the framework eliminates redundant far-field computations and isolates measurement anomalies.
