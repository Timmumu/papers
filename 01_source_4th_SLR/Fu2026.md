## Abstract summary
The stable output of China's open-pit coal mines is crucial to ensuring the secure and stable supply of national energy. Since the 14th Five-Year Plan period, China's open-pit coal mines have witnessed continuous development, contributing to national energy supply security, scientific and technological progress, work safety, and ecological and environmental protection. It systematically summarizes the major achievements of China's open-pit coal mines since the 14th Five-Year Plan in terms of production scale, intelligent equipment level, ecological governance effectiveness, scientific and technological innovation, standards and specifications, construction of key laboratories, scientific and technological research.
