## Abstract summary
High-fidelity digital twins are essential for infrastructure life cycle management, yet their development is hindered by the inherent conflict between reconstruction fidelity and data acquisition efficiency. Conventional automated flights often fail to capture complex substructures, while manual inspections are plagued by high costs and irreproducibility. To address this challenge, we propose an autonomous framework centered on a novel continuous sampling space initialization method that generates safety-constrained viewpoints tailored to bridge geometry. Within this space, we formulate a joint objective function that combines multiview stereo (MVS)-based quality (FQ) and path efficiency (FE).
