## Abstract summary
In the construction process of a prestressed steel structure, it is a point of research interest to obtain the safety state of the structure according to the design parameters and working conditions of the structure. The intelligent prediction of structural construction safety provides the basis for safety control. This study proposes an intelligent prediction method of structural construction safety based on a back propagation (BP) neural network. Firstly, the correlation mechanism of structural construction safety performance parameters is established, which involves structural design parameters and mechanical parameters. According to the basic principle of a BP neural network, the relationship between design parameters and mechanical parameters is captured. *Full-text source: 04c-port*

**AH — strength & gap filled:** Establishes a correlation mechanism between structural design parameters and mechanical parameters and fuses it with a four-level geometry-physics-behavior-rule digital twin, training a BP neural network on finite element samples to intelligently predict construction safety of a string beam. Predicted vertical displacement and lower-chord cable stress were verified against field measurements, providing a basis for safety control.

**AI — deficiencies & future work:** The BP network is trained only on finite element simulation data, only two mechanical parameters are predicted, and validation covers a single string beam of one convention center roof; no online updating from live monitoring is implemented. Future research will combine mechanical with design parameters to find key safety factors and consider more realistic conditions.
