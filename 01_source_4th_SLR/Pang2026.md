# Pang, Z et al. (2026) — A fast-response framework for real-time 3D wind field reconstruction: Supporting urban air mobility applications in complex environments

- **ID:** R284 | **Source:** Scopus | **DOI:** 10.1016/j.scs.2026.107860
- **Journal:** Sustainable Cities and Society
- **Authors:** Pang Z.; Gu J.; Xu Q.; Han S.; Luo L.; Zhang Z.; Zhu Z.; Zhang T.; Ma Y.; Shen D.

## Abstract summary
Urban Air Mobility (UAM) demands high-resolution, fast-response 3D wind diagnostics for flight safety and stability. However, current meteorological systems face severe bottlenecks: traditional numerical models are computationally too slow for timely hazard avoidance, while sparse single-point observations lack 3D spatial coverage. To address these gaps, this study proposes a real-time 3D wind field reconstruction framework designed for rapid cross-city deployment. It integrates Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) for vertical interpolation of observations, Space-Time Multiscale Analysis System (STMAS) for variational assimilation, and the QES-Winds microscale diagnostic model. Through one-way coupling between the mesoscale background field and the microscale model, the system generates a 5-m resolution 3D wind fields over a typical 4-km urban domain within 1 to 2 min.
