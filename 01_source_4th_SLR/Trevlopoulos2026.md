# Trevlopoulos, K et al. (2026) — Interpretable damage state-dependent fragility models for multi-hazard interactions based on Bayesian modeling and causal inference

- **Journal:** Structural Safety
- **Authors:** Trevlopoulos K.; Gehl P.; Negulescu C.

## Abstract summary
Considering the increasing exposure of cities to multiple hazards, disaster risk management requires trustworthy fragility models that account for cumulative damage at the building level. This study develops damage state- and hazard-dependent fragility models for a reinforced concrete building subjected to sequential earthquake and flood events. Using Bayesian inference with Hamiltonian Monte Carlo, we construct and compare statistical models based on predictive accuracy, guided by information criteria, a proposed causal directed acyclic graph, and domain knowledge. Results highlight the importance of the existing damage state in improving predictive power. Incorporating structural damage proxies, hysteretic energy, and especially period elongation, further enhances model accuracy.
