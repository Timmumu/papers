## Abstract summary
The integration of computer vision technology into construction sites poses various challenges due to the complex environment. Prior studies on computer vision related to heavy construction equipment has primarily focused on a limited range of equipment types provided in standard databases, such as the Microsoft Common Objects in Context (MS COCO) data set. The conventional approach has limitations in capturing the diverse working conditions and dynamic environments encountered in real construction sites. To overcome the challenge, this study proposes a new benchmark model for the automated detection and classification of a wide range of heavy construction equipment (i.e., nine representative types) commonly used in construction sites by using a deep convolution neural network.
