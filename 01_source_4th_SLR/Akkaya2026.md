## Abstract summary
Given the increasing energy demands associated with the use of advanced medical technologies in healthcare services, it has become imperative to implement energy optimisation measures in line with global trends. New technologies such as digital twins, system information modelling, the Internet of Things, sensing technologies, and data analytics have become indispensable elements in creating more efficient systems. This study presents analyses using a digital twin-based approach to ensure energy efficiency in medical gas systems through predictive demand control in hospital intensive care units. Within the scope of the study, a digital twin was developed by simulating operational dynamics, taking into account the physical system model, operational dynamics modelling, and control-optimisation modelling.
