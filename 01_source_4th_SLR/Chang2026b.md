## Abstract summary
This study evaluates the effectiveness of the digital twin-construction safety training framework (DT-CSTF) using a sample of 100 construction workers. The workers underwent safety training in a virtual reality training environment (VRTE), integrated with real-time data from a digital twin (DT) model, building information modelling (BIM), and safety protocols. Key performance metrics, including hazard detection rate (HDR), compliance rate (CR) and worker stress level (WSL), were analysed to measure the system's effectiveness. Results showed that the DT-CSTF significantly enhanced worker safety awareness, with HDR and CR metrics consistently exceeding 95%, even in complex, high-stress scenarios.
