## Abstract summary
Smart city implementation increasingly relies on sensing and analytics; however, a persistent operational gap remains between anomaly detection and safe, timely, and accountable intervention in civil infrastructure systems. This paper proposes an Agentic AI-supported Digital Twin framework for smart city civil infrastructure management, where monitoring and action are linked and auditability is maintained. The Digital Twin continuously updates asset and network models of bridges, roads, and water infrastructure using multi-stream telemetry, incorporating state estimation, predictive maintenance, and what-if simulation services. At the orchestration layer, an agent-based Perception-Conceptualization-Action workflow implemented with LangChain and LangGraph enables cross-domain reasoning and coordinated mitigation planning through controlled API calls to municipal data. **Key evidence (AG):** Agentic AI-enhanced DT framework for smart-city civil infrastructure (Islamic U Madinah, PLOS ONE 2026): DT continuously updates asset/network models of bridges, roads and water infrastructure from multi-stream telemetry with state estimation, predictive maintenance and what-if simulation; LangChain/LangGraph Perception-Conceptualization-Action multi-agent workflow performs cross-domain reasoning and coordinated mitigation planning through controlled API calls; permissioned blockchain cryptographically binds observations, approvals and executed interventions; evaluated with 18,000 incident simulations (5 architectures x 3 complexity levels x 30 runs): mean detection latency 3,197 s vs 39,374 s, mitigation success 66.2% vs 45.5%, blockchain-anchored justifications 71.8%, operator workload -91.7%. Synthetic evaluation only (authors state no real-world operational validation); agentic recommendations with human operators; no learning loop.

**AH — strength & gap filled:** Fills the anomaly-detection-to-action gap in smart-city infrastructure management: agent-based Perception-Conceptualization-Action orchestration plus a permissioned blockchain links monitoring, reasoning and executed interventions with auditability, cutting detection latency from 39,374 s to 3,197 s and raising mitigation success to 66.2% in 18,000 simulations.

**AI — deficiencies & future work:** Evaluation is fully synthetic (18,000 simulations; authors state it does not constitute real-world operational validation); mitigation actions, latency and blockchain overhead on municipal infrastructure remain unverified, and LLM-agent behaviours are not stress-tested against adversarial or data-quality issues.
