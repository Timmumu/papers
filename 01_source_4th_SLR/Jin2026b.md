# Jin, Y et al. (2026) — Unveiling the future: A bibliometric review of emerging trends and hotspots in sustainable modern industrial chains

- **Authors:** Jin Y.; Liu J.

## Abstract summary
A modern industrial system is the material and technical foundation of a modern nation and sustainability is an important requirement for the construction of modern industrial chains. However, there remains a gap in research on the sustainability of modern industrial chains, not to mention visual research. Based on scientometric methods, a systematic analysis was conducted on 1049 literatures related to sustainable modern industrial chains (SMIc) during the period 2016–2025. Software such as VOSviewer 1.6.20 and CiteSpace 6.3.3 were used to perform visual analysis on the references and keywords of the collected literature, in order to explore the research hotspots and emerging trends of SMIc. The results showed that "Sustainability" is the most prominent keyword and the largest research hotspot of SMIc.
