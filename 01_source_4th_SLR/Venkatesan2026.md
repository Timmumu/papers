## Abstract summary
Cognitive Radio Networks (CRNs) can address spectrum scarcity, but real-world implementation still faces challenges such as severe dynamic traffic, incomplete and unreliable spectrum sensing, stringent QoS/URLLC demands, overhead from multi-agent collaboration, limited fairness among diverse users, energy constraints at the Internet of Things (IoT) edges, and jamming and adversarial vulnerability, especially in dense 5G-Advanced/6 G and in RIS-assisted environments. The existing issues are addressed by introducing the Reinforcement Learning-related Dynamic Spectrum Allocation (RL-DSA) framework, which is used to control, predict, and ensure the safety of CRN management. Initially, the hierarchical multi-agent RL controller receives context from the transfer-related occupancy forecaster with graph networks.
