## Abstract summary
This study presents an innovative, intelligent obstacle avoidance module intended to significantly enhance the collision prevention capabilities of the robotic arm mechanism onboard a high-speed rail tunnel lining inspection train. The proposed module employs a fusion of ORB-SLAM3 and Normal Distribution Transform (NDT) point cloud registration techniques to achieve real-time point cloud densification, ensuring reliable detection of small-volume targets. By leveraging spatial filtering, cluster computation, and feature extraction, precise obstacle localization information is further obtained. A fusion of multi-modal data is achieved by jointly calibrating 3D LiDAR and camera images.
