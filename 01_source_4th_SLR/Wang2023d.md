## Abstract summary
The safety and quality of cable structure construction necessitate a comprehensive analysis approach. However, conventional methods suffer from difficulties in the temporal and spatial integration of construction information and low efficiency in construction analysis. This study proposes a multi-dimensional digital twin model for cable structure construction to optimize conventional calculation methods. Firstly, this study proposes a continuous dynamic analysis method for cable structures based on the digital twin, which reveals the mechanism behind the continuous dynamic analysis of cable structures. Furthermore, a multidimensional digital twin model is established, and the model is continuously corrected using real-time data collected by sensors.
