# Liu, W et al. (2026) — Digital twin-enabled safety management: A bibliometric and knowledge mapping analysis with core challenge insights

- **Journal:** Journal of Loss Prevention in the Process Industries
- **Authors:** Liu W.; Tong B.; Liu H.; Xu Y.; Fang L.; Zhou H.; Wu Y.; Kong J.

## Abstract summary
Digital twin technology is increasingly used to support proactive safety management, but existing studies span diverse domains and provide limited direct evidence for process-industry loss prevention. This study adopts a two-level bibliometric design. A broad corpus of 1526 records on digital twin-enabled safety applications was retrieved from the Web of Science Core Collection using bibliometric methods. A process-safety-specific corpus of 198 records was then constructed to support a focused analysis of process safety and loss prevention. The broad corpus shows that digital twin safety research is distributed across construction safety, structural health monitoring, robotics, vehicle systems, battery systems, smart manufacturing, and real-time monitoring.
