## Abstract summary
Modern cyber-physical systems (CPS) are interdependent, mechanical and IT components that support operations in most of society's critical infrastructures. Time and again history has proven that CPS are vulnerable to numerous types of threats, ranging from safety accidents to cybersecurity malicious attacks. Current research focuses on analyzing the various input vectors in CPS, whether mechanical or IT, to detect and patch flaws and vulnerabilities to mitigate potential impact in operation. Still, there is little work that can inherently analyze the software-based implementation of modern CPS with complex behavior and failure modes.
