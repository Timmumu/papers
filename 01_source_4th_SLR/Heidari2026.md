## Abstract summary
Current construction safety protocols often stagnate by treating human error as a stochastic variable rather than a predictable, mechanistically driven parameter. Addressing this critical gap, this study introduces the Neuro-Cognitive Safety Framework (NCSF), a novel interdisciplinary paradigm that bridges environmental multi-physics stressors with worker neurobiology. Anchored by a Traceability Dictionary of over 150 empirically validated concepts and a hierarchical Level of Detail (LOD) architecture, the NCSF operationalizes Cognitive Load Theory. The framework’s core mechanistic concepts were empirically validated using an experimental Electroencephalography (EEG) and n-back dataset (N = 16).
