## Abstract summary
Effective maintenance of transportation infrastructure relies on the accurate and timely assessment of pavement conditions. Traditional manual inspections, however, are labor-intensive, subjective, and hazardous. While deep learning has advanced automated distress detection, a critical gap remains in translating computer vision outputs into standardized engineering metrics suitable for Digital Twins. This study proposes an automated engineering integration framework for pavement distress assessment and quantification. The methodology integrates YOLOv12, a state-of-the-art object detection model, with DeepLabV3+ for high-fidelity semantic segmentation. The system was trained and validated on a comprehensive multi-source dataset comprising public benchmarks (Crack500) and crowdsourced field data.
