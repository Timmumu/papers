# Rocca, I et al. (2026) — Digital heritage preservation through drone surveying: Vulnerability analysis of Venetian church façades

- **ID:** R128 | **Source:** Scopus | **DOI:** 10.1016/j.daach.2026.e00537
- **Journal:** Digital Applications in Archaeology and Cultural Heritage
- **Authors:** Rocca I.; D'Acunto G.; Forti I.; Saetta A.

## Abstract summary
Preservation of Venice's unique cultural identity requires innovative approaches to safeguard its fragile architectural heritage. This study explores the potential of drone-based surveying and Historical Digital Twin methodologies to assess the vulnerability of city's façade systems. Focusing on emblematic case studies, high-resolution drone imagery was employed to generate detailed 3D digital models of the façades and their statuary elements. Models served as base for structural vulnerability analyses, enabling precise monitoring, maintenance planning, and safety evaluations. The research demonstrates how the integration of advanced digital surveying techniques with structural engineering expertise can enhance understanding and management of risks affecting Venice's cultural heritage.
