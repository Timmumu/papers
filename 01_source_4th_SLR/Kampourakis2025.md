## Abstract summary
The escalating digital interconnectivity of Critical Infrastructure (CI) necessitates robust cybersecurity solutions, particularly for effective incident detection and response. This systematic literature review explores the promising role of Digital Twins (DTs) in enhancing these critical functions across diverse CI sectors, including smart cities, healthcare, and energy. Covering research from 2019 onwards, we conduct an in-depth analysis of 27 curated articles, examining attacks, detection methods, response techniques, and emerging trends specifically related to incident handling within this domain. Our review reveals DTs as a powerful tool for CI cybersecurity, enabling proactive threat simulation and improved incident management capabilities.
