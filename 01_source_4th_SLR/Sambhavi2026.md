## Abstract summary
Hydrogen-powered electric vehicles have become a viable option for achieving sustainable, low-carbon transportation, especially for applications that require high utilization, quick refuelling, and long driving range. With a focus on proton exchange membrane fuel cells (FCs), hybrid powertrain architectures, and their integration into contemporary vehicle systems, this paper provides a thorough, up-to-date overview of hydrogen FC electric vehicle (HFCEV) technologies. FC technologies and system configurations, hydrogen generation pathways and related costs, and the operational features of FC battery and FC supercapacitor powertrains are all thoroughly reviewed in this study.
