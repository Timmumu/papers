## Abstract summary
This study presents a Digital Twin–Mixed Reality (DT–MR) framework for the immersive and interactive supervision of automated container terminals (ACTs), addressing the fragmented data and limited situational awareness of conventional 2D monitoring systems. The framework employs a middleware-centric architecture that integrates heterogeneous subsystems—covering terminal operation, equipment control, and information management—through standardized industrial communication protocols. It ensures synchronized timestamps and delivers semantically aligned, low-latency data streams to a multi-scale Digital Twin developed in Unity.
