## Abstract summary
Poor construction worksite conditions cause many Serious Injuries and Fatalities (SIF). This paper introduces the Right-time Analysis and Mitigation (RAM) module as part of the Digital Twin for Construction Safety (DTCS) concept. RAM (a) leverages safety rule-based compliance checking of construction layout plans in Building Information Modeling (called safeBIM) and (b) records and analyzes resource trajectory data involved in potential struck-by incidents using wearable and mobile Real-Time Kinematic Global Navigation Satellite Systems (RTK-GNSS) technology. RAM further (c) provides new access to unprecedented information and gives detailed insights into human-machine interactions that (d) permit rapid implementation of change by targeted control. **Key evidence (AG):** Digital construction site layout planning and real-time trajectory analysis for proactive safety monitoring and control of struck-by hazards (DTCS Right-time Analysis and Mitigation module; DTU/TUM): RTK-GNSS + LoRa live location tracking of pedestrian workers and heavy equipment streamed to DTCS; semi-static and dynamic hazard zones (1-m boundary + operator blind-space geometry); real-time incident detection of unauthorized entry and too-close proximity; density maps / histogram heatmaps identify hazardous activities; safety-plan revision recommendations (separating worker/equipment areas, temporary pedestrian walkways); field experiments in Munich subway rail maintenance staging area showed significant reduction in spatial conflicts and improved zone connectivity, with newly detected hazards (rail traffic, delivery trucks) triggering iterative plan updates. One-way monitoring to analysis to mitigation recommendations/alerts for safety personnel; no actuation, no learning loop.

**AH — strength & gap filled:** Extends the Digital Twin for Construction Safety concept with a Right-time Analysis and Mitigation module tested at a Munich railway maintenance staging area: safeBIM rule checking plus RTK-GNSS over LoRa tracking at 1.0 cm median and 2.3 cm 95th percentile precision, detecting 99 unauthorized entries, mapping telehandler blind spots (right side obstructed to 1.9 m) and computing safety-weighted walkway routes that informed a revised safeBIM plan.

**AI — deficiencies & future work:** Experiments lasted a limited period in a controlled staging area, RTK-GNSS suffers signal loss and interference, and operator awareness of workers could not be recorded, so head-pose or eye-tracking data is proposed; future work includes longer data collection, other equipment types, drone-based condition capture, big data analytics and standards for safe distances.
