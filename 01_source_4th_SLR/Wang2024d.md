# Wang, Y et al. (2024) — Digital twin approach for enhancing urban resilience: A cycle between virtual space and the real world

- **ID:** R880 | **Source:** Scopus | **DOI:** 10.1016/j.rcns.2024.06.002

- **Authors:** Wang Y.; Yue Q.; Lu X.; Gu D.; Xu Z.; Tian Y.; Zhang S.

## Abstract summary
Construction of disaster-resilient cities has attracted considerable attention. However, traditional methods of studying urban disaster resilience through experimental approaches are often constrained by various limitations, such as testing sites, costs and ethical considerations. To address these constraints, this paper proposes incorporating digital twin concepts into urban disaster resilience research. By establishing a connection between the physical realm of the city and its virtual counterpart, this approach utilizes digital simulations to overcome the limitations of experimental methods and enables dynamic deduction and control of the disaster process.
