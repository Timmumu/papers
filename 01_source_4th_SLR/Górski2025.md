## Abstract summary
The last decade has seen the dynamic development of robotic tools, particularly the unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV). Miniaturization, improvement of control tools, and specialization of these devices have made them tools that are eagerly used by various industries, including the construction industry. Initially, UAVs were used for visual inspections; today, highly specialized devices are an increasingly important element of Structural Health Monitoring (SHM), assessing the condition of structures and estimating their load-bearing capacity. This article aims to present the current state of technology in robotic solutions in SHM, with particular emphasis on those providing possibilities for contact-based diagnostics.
