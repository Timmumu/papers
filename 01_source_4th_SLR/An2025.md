# An, Y et al. (2025) — Efficient low-collision UAV-based automated structural surface inspection using geometric digital twin and voxelized obstacle information

- **ID:** R279 | **Source:** Scopus | **DOI:** 10.1016/j.autcon.2025.105972
- **Journal:** Automation in Construction
- **Authors:** An Y.; Ning J.; Hou C.; Ou J.

## Abstract summary
The application of Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) automatic flight is increasingly popular for structural surface inspection. To address the low level of automation and insufficient adaption of the flight path in response to environmental obstacles, a method of automatic planning UAV inspection mission based on the Geometric Digital Twin (GDT) model and Voxelized Obstacle Information (VOI) is proposed. First, a method for shifting the Field of View (FOV) centroids in parallel is proposed to efficiently generate inspection waypoints. Second, a waypoints adjustment method based on environmental VOI of 3D point clouds is proposed to address the safety issues. Third, a method combining Genetic Algorithm (GA) with A* based on VOI is proposed for optimizing UAV flight path to avoid real-world obstacles. **Key evidence (AG):** Automatic UAV inspection mission planning from a Geometric Digital Twin (GDT) and Voxelized Obstacle Information (VOI) (Guangxi U/DUT/Beihang, AutCon 2025): FOV-centroid parallel shifting generates structural-surface waypoints; environmental VOI from 3D point clouds adjusts waypoints for safety; GA + A* hybrid optimises collision-free flight paths; verified on an office building and a steel truss bridge with significantly improved efficiency. The twin is a static geometric reference for pre-planning; the UAV then executes the plan physically, no live twin link during flight, no learning loop.

**AH — strength & gap filled:** Fills the automation/adaptation gap in UAV structural inspection: GDT + VOI waypoint generation and GA+A* path optimisation deliver collision-free, complete and efficient flight plans verified on both an office building and a steel truss bridge.

**AI — deficiencies & future work:** The GDT is static LoD-based geometry: dynamic obstacles appearing after planning and mission-level autonomy (beyond path planning) are out of scope; two structure types only, and inspection-image quality metrics are secondary to flight-path metrics.
