## Abstract summary
As natural and anthropogenic hazards intensify, improving the performance of reinforced-concrete (RC) infrastructure within a resilience-oriented assessment framework while limiting environmental burdens has become an important challenge for sustainable construction. In this context, this study proposes an OpenBIM-based digital-twin methodology to compare two equivalent RC structural scenarios: a conventional solution and an alternative incorporating unprocessed waste sheep wool fibres into cementitious materials. Using an IFC-based model of a high-rise building, the workflow enables automated extraction of structural quantities and a consistent building-scale assessment of material use, environmental impacts, and circularity indicators.
