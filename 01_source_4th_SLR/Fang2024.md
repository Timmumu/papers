# Fang, W et al. (2024) — A status digital twin approach for physically monitoring over-and-under excavation in large tunnels

## Abstract summary
Accurate assessments of over-and-under-excavation volumes during tunnel construction are critical for stability and structural safety. Over-and-under-excavation assessment is typically time-consuming as it is undertaken manually and is prone to inaccuracies that adversely impact quality, safety, and costs. To overcome this problem, a status digital twin (to monitor physical conditions) is developed to assess over-and-under excavation during tunneling, enabling a robust and automated assessment of volumes of earth to be determined. The development of the status digital twin comprises three stages: (1) creating an as-designed building information model and as-built point cloud model; (2) producing an automated status update with newly added data; and (3) calculating the of over-and-under excavation volume by building a Triangulated Irregular Network (TIN).
