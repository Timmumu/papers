# Isolan, L et al. (2025) — Extravasation modeling from a PET/CT imaging for a possible digital twin approach

- **ID:** R422 | **Source:** Scopus | **DOI:** 10.1016/j.radphyschem.2025.113080
- **Journal:** Radiation Physics and Chemistry
- **Authors:** Isolan L.; Panico N.; Fioroni F.; Grassi E.; Iori M.; Sumini M.

## Abstract summary
Extravasation of radiopharmaceuticals during PET procedures can compromise both diagnostic accuracy and patient safety by altering tracer pharmacokinetics and delivering unintended local radiation doses. A clinically observed 18F-FDG extravasation case has been reconstructed through a superposition of co-recorded CT and PET DICOM scan images to localize and quantify the extravasated activity. Building on advances in personalized computational modeling, the Digital Twin paradigm could offer a powerful framework for patient-specific dosimetry. Anatomical segmentation has been performed using Synopsys® Simpleware™ Medical to generate an accurate 3D model of the injection site and surrounding tissues.
