## Abstract summary
The oil and gas sector is witnessing rapid technological transformation through the integration of the Internet of Things (IoT)-enabled systems that facilitate the real-time monitoring, automation, and data-driven decision-making. Digital twin (DT) serves as a key IoT application, offering dynamic virtual representations of physical assets to support the predictive analytics and operational optimization. The deployment of DT is further strengthened by emerging technologies, such as fifth-generation (5G)/sixth-generation (6G) networks, artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML), and distributed computing paradigms, including edge computing (EC), cloud computing, and fog computing (FG).
