## Abstract summary
Vehicular networks are experiencing a significant transformation driven by integrating connected vehicles and Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS). The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) is a rapidly evolving domain within ITS that connects vehicles, infrastructure, and smart devices to facilitate seamless data exchange. This data encompasses vital information, and by leveraging it, vehicles can make informed decisions and adapt to real-time situations. For instance, traditional vehicle maintenance practices often rely on reactive approaches, addressing issues after failures occur, which can lead to safety risks, costly repairs, and disruptions. Thus, there is a pressing need for proactive solutions to identify vehicle failures before they escalate.
