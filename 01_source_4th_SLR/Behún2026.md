## Abstract summary
Reliable calibration of measuring instruments is a foundational yet under-reported layer of the smart-manufacturing stack: without traceable calibrations, no downstream process can guarantee the metrological quality of the data it consumes. This research asks whether an administratively managed calibration record base is sufficient to support data-driven recalibration scheduling, and diagnoses the data-governance conditions such scheduling requires.
