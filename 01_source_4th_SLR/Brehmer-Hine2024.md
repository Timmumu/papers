## Abstract summary
‘Digitalisation’ is a current mega-trend for assisting industry decision-making. The geothermal industry is not immune to this trend, given the strong incentives to improve efficiency whilst maintaining product quality, safety and productivity. The ‘digital twin’ can be considered as one of the core concepts of digitalisation, and yet it is still at the conceptual stage for many manufacturing operations. The first step in digital twin implementation is to build digital models since they are the essential components of digital twins. However, how to select suitable models from many different types of models of different fidelity for the geothermal industry is an open question which has not yet been addressed.
