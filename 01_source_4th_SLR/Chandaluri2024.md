## Abstract summary
The industries engaged healthcare, manufacturing, and construction automated with exquisite creativity and innovation at the doorstep of Industry 4.0. In this process, digital twins are diligently used to supplement industrial processes, reduce costs, monitor assets, streamline maintenance, reduce downtime, and enable the creation of connected goods. The research is aimed at finding the digital representation of electro-mechanical railway interlocking elements are simulated and its functionality is verified accordingly. With respect to carry out this research, the Indian Railways Mangalagiri Railway Station blueprints were collected to represent the physical Model which consists of signals, points, a crank handle, an LC gate, and many more elements.
