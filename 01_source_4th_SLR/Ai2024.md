## Abstract summary
Autonomous driving will be widely deployed in the near future which can efficiently reduce the workload on human drivers. Unlike complicated urban driving conditions, fully autonomous driving is expected to be implemented in closed and relatively simple environments, e.g., mines. Autonomous driving in mines holds substantial practical value that can significantly reduce mining costs and enhance operational efficiency. To ensure vehicle safety, unmanned driving systems should be rigorously tested before deployment. With the rapid development of digital twin technology, offline testing in cyberspace has gained increasing significance. While numerous driving simulators have been recently released, none of them can support simulation tests in mines.
