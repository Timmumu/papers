## Abstract summary
Artificial intelligence (AI) has the potential to revolutionize the construction industry by providing transformative tools that increase efficiency, improve safety, and facilitate rapid and smart decision making. Despite potential benefits, AI integration into construction industry faced several challenges like, high implementation costs, lack of experts, and resistance to change. Additionally, the concern of data security against the cyberattacks, high volume of data and matching data from various sources (other AI tools) are significant challenges. Although, numerous studies have been conducted on this topic, but none have comprehensively reviewed the benefits and challenges of integrating the six technologies integrated with AI of specific benefit to the industry: the Internet of Things (IoT), blockchain, machine learning (ML), building information modeling (BIM), digital twins (DT),…
