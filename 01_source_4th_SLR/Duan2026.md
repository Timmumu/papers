## Abstract summary
As the underground pipelines deteriorate with age, their structural vulnerability increases. Cured-in-place pipe (CIPP) lining is an emerging trenchless technology used to rehabilitate deteriorated pipes. However, the buckling behavior of CIPP liner-rehabilitated pipes is insufficiently investigated. This study presents an integrated framework for real-time structural health monitoring and damage detection for underground pipelines by leveraging the knowledge of distributed fiber optic sensors (DFOS), finite element analysis (FEA), deep learning (DL) algorithms, and digital twin. The study experimentally and computationally investigates the structural behavior of a single steel substrate and a CIPP-rehabilitated steel substrate under eccentric compression conditions.
