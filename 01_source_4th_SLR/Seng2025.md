## Abstract summary
Project delays are a common challenge in the construction industry and often result in increased costs and inefficiencies. As a solution to these ongoing problems, 4D Building Information Modelling (BIM) has emerged as a powerful tool for improving project planning and project management. However, despite the growing interest in 4D BIM, there is a lack of comprehensive analysis of its development and integration with new technologies in construction. This study aims to address this gap by conducting a scientometric review of 4D BIM, focusing on growth patterns, prominent subject categories, keywords, highly cited studies, influential authors and emerging themes. A total of 558 bibliographic records from the Web of Science core collection database (2007-2024) were analysed.
