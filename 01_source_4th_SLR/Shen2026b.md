## Abstract summary
Engineering design evaluation of complex spatial products—such as vehicle interiors, building environments, and aircraft cabins—frequently demands high-fidelity digital representations that couple geometric accuracy with semantic interpretability. Yet a persistent methodological gap exists between raw 3D data acquisition and computable, performance-evaluable parametric models: conventional approaches either produce geometrically precise but semantically opaque point clouds, or rely on idealized CAD models that deviate from as-built reality. This paper proposes a general-purpose semantic-geometric digital twin framework that bridges this gap, enabling automated conversion of physical environments into structured, parameterized virtual models that directly support multi-dimensional design performance evaluation and optimization.
