## Abstract summary
In recent years, the implementation of digital twin (DT) technology has gained significant attention in various industries. However, the fire safety management (FSM) sector has been relatively slow in adopting this technology compared to other major industries. Therefore, this study aims to explore the limitations, opportunities, and challenges associated with adopting DT technology in the FSM sector and further develop a DT-based FSM framework towards smart facility management (FM). To achieve this objective, this research started by reviewing several promising DTs for FSM, including building information modeling (BIM), the Internet of Things (IoT), artificial intelligence (AI), and augmented reality (AR). **AH — strength & gap filled:** Addresses the underexplored application of DT to fire safety management: a four-layer framework (physical building, virtual building, application, and user interaction layers) combining BIM, IoT, AI, data fusion, blockchain, and AR, with virtual-layer decisions fed back to the physical building layer for automatic control and operation optimization while keeping FM professionals in the loop.

**AI — deficiencies & future work:** Conceptual only: the framework was validated through a thematic analysis of 37 studies and a survey of FM professionals (66 responses, 28 complete) rather than a live implementation; no prototype or sensor deployment is reported. Future work includes a Delphi study with a broader panel and involving firefighters as end users.
