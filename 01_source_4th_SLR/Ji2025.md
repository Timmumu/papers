## Abstract summary
Objective To address the inefficiency, safety hazards, and resource wastage in traditional medical laboratory management, this study proposes a digital laboratory information management system (LIMS) based on Total Quality Management (TQM) principles. The LIMS has been implemented at the National Center for Geriatric Medicine (Huashan) affiliated with Fudan University. Methods Centered on the principles of &quot; all-staff participation, whole-process control, and comprehensive management&quot;, a multidimensional management framework was developed to integrate personnel, equipment, reagents, and safety protocols. The system incorporated IoT, digital twin, and artificial intelligence (AI) technologies to achieve end-to-end digital control.
