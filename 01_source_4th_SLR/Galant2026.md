## Abstract summary
Building information modeling (BIM) enables the integration of multidisciplinary data into detailed three-dimensional structural models. However, accurately capturing the mechanical behavior of real-world structures requires the calibration of these virtual models with in situ measurements, particularly to reflect damage-induced changes that influence safety and structural integrity. Traditional calibration workflows rely heavily on the manual updating of structural databases and parameter assignments, which are time-consuming, prone to human error, and lack scalability. This study presents, for the first time, a robotic process automation (RPA)-based workflow for automating the integration of corrosion-related damage into structural BIM models.
