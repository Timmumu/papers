## Abstract summary
The rapid expansion of the Metaverse presents complex security challenges, particularly in verifying virtual objects and avatars within immersive environments. Conventional authentication methods, such as passwords and biometrics, often prove inadequate in these dynamic environments, especially as essential infrastructures, such as smart grids, integrate with virtual platforms. Cybersecurity threats intensify as advanced attacks introduce fraudulent data, compromising system reliability and safety. Using the Electric Network Frequency (ENF), a naturally varying signal emitted from power grids, provides an innovative environmental fingerprint to authenticate digital twins and Metaverse entities in the smart grid.
