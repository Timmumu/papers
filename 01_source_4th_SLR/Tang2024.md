## Abstract summary
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is now playing a crucialrole not only in everyday life, evidenced by the booming application of Large Language Models (LLMs) such as the Generative Pretrained Transformer (GPT), but also in its potential to transform traditional industries like civil engineering. This work examines the application of novel AI tools to enable Digital Twins (DT) for engineering structures, providing a comprehensive solution for the life-cycle management. A comprehensive state-of-the-art review is conducted to explore existing advancements in sensing, inspection, and simulation that are fundamental to the development of digital twins. Building on this knowledge, a framework is proposed to define DT for Engineering(DT4ENG) based on their emphasis and data flow, including forward DT, backward DT, and DT-informed decision making.
