## Abstract summary
Hydrogen energy is emerging as a cornerstone of the global energy transition, demanding the development of autonomous, safe, and intelligent control systems for complex infrastructure. In this context, digital twin (DT) technology offers a transformative approach to monitoring, diagnosing, and optimizing the operation of hydrogen-based and hydroelectric systems. This paper presents a comprehensive methodology for implementing DT algorithms within relay protection and automation (RPA) frameworks, with direct integration into SCADA environments. The proposed algorithms enable real-time identification and localization of equipment faults by analyzing analog and discrete signals, significantly enhancing operational reliability and decision-making speed.
