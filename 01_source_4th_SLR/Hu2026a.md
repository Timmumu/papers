## Abstract summary
Tunnel leakage threatens the durability, safety, and serviceability of underground infrastructure, while conventional inspection methods remain limited by low efficiency, subjectivity, and poor capability for hidden seepage assessment. This review aims to clarify recent progress in artificial intelligence-based tunnel leakage detection and monitoring by examining sensing technologies, intelligent interpretation methods, and multimodal fusion strategies from a measurement-oriented perspective. Electromagnetic, optical, mechanical, vibration/ acoustic, environmental, wireless, self-powered, and multi-parameter sensing approaches are compared according to their measurable quantities, applicable tasks, advantages, limitations, and uncertainty sources.
