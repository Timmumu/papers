## Abstract summary
Non-metallic pipes such as polyethylene (PE), polypropylene (PP), and glass fiber reinforced composites have been widely applied in oil and gas transportation, municipal construction, and other fields due to their light weight and corrosion resistance, and they show significant potential for hydrogen pipeline applications. Butt fusion welding and electrofusion welding are the core joining methods, and the reliability of the welded joints directly determines engineering safety, making related technical research a key focus. Based on a comprehensive review of the literature with bibliometric methods, this study systematically analyzes the research landscape and technological evolution in the field of non-metallic pipe welding, with a particular focus on simulation technologies.
