# Tian, J et al. (2026) — Digital twin-driven intelligent structural fault diagnosis and decision-making for earth-rock dams using large language models

## Abstract summary
Structural health monitoring of high earth-rock dams is essential for ensuring operational safety under complex hydro-mechanical conditions. However, existing digital twin approaches mainly focus on state prediction and lack capabilities for intelligent fault reasoning and engineering decision-making. To address this issue, this study proposes a digital twin-driven intelligent fault diagnosis framework integrated with Large Language Models (LLMs). Multi-source monitoring data, finite element simulations, and transfer learning are combined to construct multi-physics digital twins of deformation, seepage, and stress fields. A robust gradient estimation method and a real-time slope stability model are developed to identify critical failure indicators, including seepage anomalies, cracking risks, hydraulic fracturing, and slope instability.
