## Abstract summary
With the extensive use of digital technologies, the modern construction industry has made progress in improving productivity and safety. Despite this, construction productivity remains among the lowest in the industry. With the development of Industrial Revolution 4.0, the construction industry has also benefited from it, forming the idea behind Construction 4.0, which is founded on: the digitization of the construction sector and the industrialization of the construction procedure. The current use of digital twins in the construction industry is considered among the most successful and influential ways to achieve Construction 4.0, optimize construction links, and improve coordination among stakeholders.
