## Abstract summary
Fatigue cracking threatens the safety of orthotropic steel decks (OSDs) in long-span bridges, yet current manual inspection lacks predictive depth. We present a closed-loop framework integrating autonomous robotic inspection, vision-based quantification, and finite-element fracture mechanics to enable adaptive fatigue prognosis. In laboratory validation, the robotic platform achieved a mean localization accuracy of 2.7 ± 0.8 cm, meeting structural precision requirements. Field deployment on an in-service cable-stayed bridge demonstrated that automated inspection reduced average time per girder from 124.6 to 50.4 minutes—a 59.6% reduction. Identified cracks were assimilated into a digital twin for adaptive state updating.
