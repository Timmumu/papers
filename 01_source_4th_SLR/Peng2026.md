## Abstract summary
Bridge infrastructure worldwide is aging, while demands for safety, sustainability, and cost-efficiency intensify challenges in bridge management (BM). This study develops a comprehensive knowledge map of BM through scientometric analysis, clarifying its intellectual structure, technological frontiers, and emerging themes. Using CiteSpace, 1582 peer-reviewed articles (2003–2024) from the Web of Science Core Collection were analyzed, with keywords and gaps refined via a Delphi process involving five senior experts. Scientometric techniques—including co-citation, cluster mapping, citation bursts, and keyword time-zone analysis—were employed to trace field evolution. Results reveal three stages: exploratory (2003–2009), steady growth (2010–2016), and digital transformation (2017–2024).
