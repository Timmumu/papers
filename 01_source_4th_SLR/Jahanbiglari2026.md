# Jahanbiglari, A et al. (2026) — Human-AI trust for safer heavy-duty equipment management: Insights from socio-technical literature review and interviews

- **Authors:** Jahanbiglari A.; Park S.; Chiou E.; Tang P.

## Abstract summary
Heavy-duty equipment management increasingly relies on AI-enabled cyber-physical systems integrating sensors and digital twins to improve safety, efficiency, and sustainability. Despite technical advances, field deployment remains limited, suggesting adoption barriers driven more by socio-technical misalignment among intelligent systems, organizational processes, and human decision-making than by algorithmic capability. Accordingly, trust between humans and intelligent systems shapes whether automation yields operational gains. This review synthesizes 90 papers and four expert interviews across construction, logistics, and fleet management using bibliometric mapping and thematic synthesis. Although cross-domain in scope, the evidence base is construction-dominant and offers transferable insights for broader equipment management.
