## Abstract summary
Mining has been a critical driver of global industrialisation for a long time. Yet, increasing demand and challenges, such as deeper deposits, more complex ores, and stricter regulations, require advanced technologies for more efficiency, safety, and sustainability in mining operations. This paper presents a comprehensive review of recent digital technologies that are reshaping the mining value chain, from exploration to processing. Drawing on peer-reviewed research, this study classifies and analyses the applications of Industry 4.0 tools, such as IoT, artificial intelligence, big data, robotics, and digital twins across each stage of mining operations.
