## Abstract summary
The realization of a specific REMote HAndling Test and Training Facility (REMHAT) is necessary for the development and optimization of the Remote Handling (RH) system, to demonstrate the fulfilment of all its design requirements, for testing and validation of the RH maintenance procedures, for the qualification and acceptance testing of RH equipment and tooling. Furthermore, it provides training and certification to the RH maintenance operators, by making available to operators the knowledge, instructions and experiencing of the Virtual Simulator platform, the control room devices, the robots and tooling to be used for RH tasks.
