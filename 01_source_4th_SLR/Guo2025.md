## Abstract summary
This study introduces an innovative data-BIM integrated digital twin framework for the quality monitoring residential developments. The framework amalgamates residential building information modeling (RBIM), surveying sensors and Internet of Things (SSIoT) technologies to establish a multi-faceted and multi-scale monitoring system. Within this framework, RBIM serves as a residential information twin model that aggregates comprehensive life cycle data of residential projects, encompassing design, building, operation, and maintenance. The physical things are linked to cyberspace via SSIoT technology, significantly improving data exchange and processing capabilities. **AH — strength & gap filled:** Proposes a sky-space-ground digital twin framework fusing RBIM with SSIoT multi-source sensing, including real-time LiDAR, automated monitoring robots, GNSS and SBAS-InSAR settlement data, linked through GUID object mapping tables and a cloud platform with weighted-average fusion and conflict detection, achieving consistent millimeter and sub-millimeter settlement precision across monitoring points.

**AI — deficiencies & future work:** The system is limited to integrating and visually presenting multi-source data; heterogeneous format standardization between BIM and point clouds remains a major challenge, and prediction or assessment is absent. Future research will add advanced data analytics and machine learning to predict maintenance needs and potential problems from historical and real-time data.
