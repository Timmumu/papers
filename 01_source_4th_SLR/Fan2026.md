## Abstract summary
Accurate acquisition of advanced geological information and experience-based decision-making remain major challenges for intelligent coal mine excavation. This study proposes a construction method for a transparent excavation geological navigation system based on comprehensive drilling geophysical exploration. Integrating the collaborative advanced detection technology of drilling geophysical exploration, multi-source geological data fusion interpretation, and navigation route planning algorithms, the study presents an integrated “detection-guidance-excavation” architecture. The innovative breakthroughs include: A collaborative mechanism for the drilling-geophysical data chain was established.
