## Abstract summary
Slope safety management is transitioning toward the digital twin paradigm, where digital models serve as a base plate for the development of further applications. This paper presents an automated solution to creating digital models for engineered slope digital twins. Utilizing city-scale point cloud, the proposed approach creates lightweight BIM models of engineered slopes consisting of drainage channels and slope topography. The proposed approach leverages a convolutional neural network (CNN) model, DrainNet, for fine-scale drainage channel delineation in images, an automated parametric modeling pipeline for scalable BIM reconstruction, and a web-based digital twin for interactive visualization. **AH — strength & gap filled:** Fills the gap of resource-intensive modeling blocking city-scale slope digital twins: DrainNet, a CNN with directional convolutions, dilated blocks and channel attention, delineates drainage channels with 81.26 percent Dice and 0.24 connectivity error using only 0.28M parameters on the 1226-image CurvaChannel dataset, and an automated Grasshopper workflow builds slope BIM models about nine times faster than Dynamo for a web-based twin platform.

**AI — deficiencies & future work:** Only exposed drainage channels and topography are modeled, cross-sections use one uniform empirical size, the closure heuristic may wrongly connect distinct channels, and the twin is static with no live monitoring data; the authors plan real-time sensor integration, hydrodynamic simulation, data-driven susceptibility analysis and higher-LOD models toward fully realized digital twins.
