## Abstract ^sec-abstract

<div class="section abstract"><div class="htmlview paragraph">Tunnels are vital infrastructures in daily life. To utilize digital twin technology for more efficient and convenient tunnel operation and maintenance, tunnel modeling serves as its foundation. However, existing tunnel modeling methods always suffer from high computational complexity, poor generalizability, and low expressive efficiency. This article proposes a data-driven tunnel modeling approach based on the Unity3D platform. Based on the actual engineering drawings, the method obtains the tunnel parameter set through the classification and feature analysis of the tunnel structure. A process-oriented model representation, i.e. a Constructive Solid Geometry (CSG) tree is then employed, enabling the creation of portal models without dependence on specific data structures. Meanwhile, the mesh optimization idea of downward triangulation and the neighbor-edge detection mechanism are introduced to improve the expression efficiency while maintaining the integrity and correctness of the structural features.</div></div>

## Introduction & Background ^sec-intro

Tunnels are vital infrastructures. To utilise **digital twin technology for more efficient tunnel operation and maintenance**, tunnel modelling serves as its foundation, yet existing methods suffer from high computational complexity, poor generalizability, and low expressive efficiency.

## Method ^sec-method

A **data-driven tunnel modelling approach** based on Unity3D: tunnel parameter sets are obtained from engineering drawings via classification and feature analysis; a **Constructive Solid Geometry (CSG) tree** enables portal model creation without dependence on specific data structures. Mesh optimisation via downward triangulation and neighbour-edge detection improves expression efficiency while maintaining structural-feature integrity.

## Key Findings ^sec-results

The CSG-tree approach provides process-oriented, data-structure-independent tunnel modelling with improved expression efficiency and structural-feature correctness.
