## Abstract ^sec-abstract

The paper provides a comprehensive review of DT in the construction industry covering current implementations, the enabling technology stack, and future directions, published open-access in Sustainability under CC-BY. It catalogues the state of construction DT adoption, organises the enabling technology landscape, and identifies forward-looking research directions for the AEC sector. ^p1

## Introduction & Background ^sec-intro

The authors position construction as a late adopter of DT relative to manufacturing and motivate a comprehensive synthesis that consolidates fragmented implementations into a coherent picture. ^p1

The review is framed around three pillars — implementations, enabling technologies, and future directions — chosen to expose both capability and gap dimensions. ^p2

## Method ^sec-method

1. Comprehensive review protocol covering construction-DT literature. ^p1
2. Classification of implementations by use case and lifecycle phase. ^p2
3. Mapping of enabling technologies (e.g., IoT, BIM, AI, cloud) used in surveyed DT implementations. ^p3
4. Derivation of future research directions through gap analysis. ^p4

## Key Findings ^sec-results

- Construction-DT implementations are heterogeneous and unevenly distributed across lifecycle phases. ^f1
- A consolidated enabling-technology stack for construction DT is articulated. ^f2
- Future directions emphasise integration, interoperability and lifecycle continuity. ^f3
- The review provides one of the most comprehensive open-access syntheses of construction DT to date. ^f4

## Discussion & Implications ^sec-discussion

The work serves as a state-of-the-art baseline that any new construction-DT framework should benchmark against, and exposes the lifecycle-coverage imbalance that a cognitive full-lifecycle DT must correct. ^p1

The enabling-technology mapping provides a ready-made checklist for AECO DT stack design and for gap identification at the cognitive layer. ^p2

## Conclusion ^sec-conclusion

Construction DT research is maturing but lifecycle integration and cognitive capability remain underdeveloped, justifying a dedicated cognitive full-lifecycle DT agenda. ^p1
