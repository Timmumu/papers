## Abstract ^sec-abstract

; Purpose ; In Africa, technological adoption in facility management (FM) is hindered by knowledge gaps, high costs and outdated infrastructure. This study aims to examine the role of innovative technology in sustainable FM (SFM) for Smart Cities through a bibliometric analysis to identify trends, themes and gaps. ; ; ; Design/methodology/approach ; A bibliometric analysis was conducted using Scopus as the primary data source, covering publications from 2014 to 2024. Data was analysed using VOSviewer to visualise publication networks, keyword clusters and citation relationships. A systematic search and content analysis were applied to ensure relevant document inclusion and thematic exploration. ; ; ; Findings ; Research interest in this field has grown significantly since 2021, with the Internet of Things, artificial intelligence, digital twin and building information modelling emerging as dominant technologies. China leads in publications, while Spain has the highest citation impact. However, international collaboration remains weak, particularly in Africa highlighting the need for stronger interdisciplinary research networks. ; ; ; Originality/value ; This study contributes to the discourse on digital transformation in FM and smart cities by mapping technological advancements and their impact. It outlines future research and suggestions for SFM development in Africa. ;

## Introduction & Background ^sec-intro

In Africa, technological adoption in **facility management (FM)** is hindered by knowledge gaps, high costs, and outdated infrastructure. The study examines the role of innovative technology in sustainable FM (SFM) for smart cities via bibliometric analysis.

## Method ^sec-method

A bibliometric analysis using Scopus (2014–2024) analysed with VOSviewer for publication networks, keyword clusters, and citation relationships, plus systematic search and content analysis.

## Key Findings ^sec-results

Research interest grew significantly since 2021, with IoT, AI, **digital twin**, and BIM as dominant technologies. China leads in publications, Spain in citation impact; international collaboration remains weak, particularly in Africa. Future research directions for SFM in Africa are outlined.

## Discussion & Implications ^sec-discussion

Relevant to the **DT/BIM in facility management + smart cities** strand, with a distinctive African/developing-region lens. Useful for FM-DT landscape and collaboration-gap discussion.
