## Abstract ^sec-abstract
Bibliometric and narrative review of 825 DT publications addressing the O&M stage of the AEC industry (January 2016 – July 2023). The review (i) summarizes the common technologies used in each current research topic, (ii) systematizes enabling technologies from the perspective of the DT five-dimensional model, (iii) establishes a complete common technology strategy for DT application in the AEC O&M stage, and (iv) identifies technical-level research gaps and opportunities. ^p1

## Method ^sec-method
Bibliometric corpus (825 papers, 2016–2023) + structured synthesis around the DT five-dimensional model (physical, virtual, service, data, connection). No primary empirical data. ^p1

## Key Findings ^sec-results

- DT applications in the AEC O&M stage are dominated by monitoring, energy management and predictive maintenance; autonomous control remains marginal. ^f1

- Mapping enabling technologies to the five-dimensional model exposes systematic under-investment in the 'connection' (real-time, bi-directional) and 'service' layers. ^f2

- A reusable DT-for-O&M technology strategy template is proposed, intended to lower the entry barrier for new researchers. ^f3


## Discussion & Implications ^sec-discussion
One of the most current and structured AECO-O&M DT reviews; provides quantitative justification for the SLR's claim that the connection/service layers are the maturity bottleneck and that the five-dimensional model is the de-facto reference taxonomy. ^p1

## Relevance to Cognitive Full-Lifecycle DT SLR ^sec-relevance
Lifecycle stage: operation & maintenance (AEC). Used in LR_cc.md as evidence for the under-developed connection/service layers and for the dominance of monitoring vs. autonomous control in AECO O&M DTs. ^p1
