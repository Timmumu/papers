## Abstract ^sec-abstract

The PRISMA statement, published in 2009 to help systematic reviewers transparently report why a review was done, what was done, and what was found, is updated because of advances in systematic review methodology and terminology. PRISMA 2020 replaces PRISMA 2009 with new reporting guidance reflecting improvements in identifying, selecting, appraising and synthesising studies. The article presents the PRISMA 2020 27-item checklist, an expanded checklist with detailed recommendations per item, a PRISMA 2020 abstract checklist, and revised flow diagrams for original and updated reviews. ^p1

## Introduction & Background ^sec-intro

The authors argue systematic reviews underpin knowledge synthesis, future-research prioritisation, theory generation and identification of primary-research problems, which makes faithful reporting essential. ^p1

They motivate the update by methodological advances since 2009 (search methods, risk-of-bias assessment, evidence synthesis tools) that the 2009 checklist no longer fully captured. ^p2

## Method ^sec-method

1. International consensus update of the 2009 PRISMA statement by an expert author team. ^p1
2. Revision of the checklist structure and presentation to facilitate implementation. ^p2
3. Production of a 27-item checklist, an expanded checklist with per-item recommendations, an abstract-specific checklist, and revised flow diagrams for new and updated reviews. ^p3

## Key Findings ^sec-results

- A 27-item PRISMA 2020 checklist replaces the 2009 version. ^f1
- An expanded checklist provides per-item reporting recommendations. ^f2
- A PRISMA 2020 abstract checklist is provided for journal/abstract-only reporting. ^f3
- Revised flow diagrams cover original and updated systematic reviews. ^f4

## Discussion & Implications ^sec-discussion

PRISMA 2020 is the current authoritative reporting standard; using it raises the credibility and reproducibility of any systematic review, including domain-specific reviews in AECO. ^p1

For LR_cc.md, PRISMA 2020 supplies the methodological backbone (search transparency, selection flow, synthesis reporting) that the SLR follows. ^p2

## Conclusion ^sec-conclusion

PRISMA 2020 provides the updated 27-item checklist, expanded guidance, abstract checklist and flow diagrams that current systematic reviews are expected to follow. ^p1
