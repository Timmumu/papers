## Introduction & Background ^sec-intro

**Circular economy (CE)** principles can enhance sustainability and regenerative potential of built environments through **facility management (FM)**. The study explores how FM can support CE during a building's use phase, focusing on applying **digital twin technologies**.

## Method ^sec-method

A case study in Trondheim, Norway, with semi-structured interviews of project owners, property managers, building operations managers, architects, contractors, and consultants.

## Key Findings ^sec-results

Circular FM is linked to building design strategies improving adaptability, extending service life, and promoting stakeholder collaboration. A **digital twin is crucial for efficient data management** supporting both FM services and CE principles, ultimately enhancing asset value.

## Discussion & Implications ^sec-discussion

**Relevant** — CE + FM + DT multi-stakeholder case study, intersecting the DT-FM and sustainability/CE strands. Provides empirical evidence for DT-enabled circular FM.
