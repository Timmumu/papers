## Abstract ^sec-abstract

In view of the current status and main problems of the construction of municipal pipelines in Shanghai Oriental Hub, based on the digital twin technology, this paper proposes and constructs a full‐cycle digital twin platform for municipal pipelines in the digital era, and discusses the implementation path of the platform construction. On the premise of clarifying the planning and management mechanism of underground pipelines, based on the establishment of a data standard system and safety control scheme, based on building information modeling, IoT perception, artificial intelligence, and other technologies, the deep integration of municipal pipelines and digital twin technology in the whole life cycle of planning, construction, operation and maintenance (O&M) is promoted, which plays an important role in promoting the intensive and efficient construction and safe operation of municipal pipelines in the Oriental Hub. This paper preliminarily discusses the application of digital twin technology in the scenario of Shanghai Oriental Hub Municipal Pipeline Platform, which can provide a reference for 3D pipeline approval, emergency support, and urban physical examination and evaluation.

## Introduction & Background ^sec-intro

Municipal underground pipelines at the Shanghai Oriental Hub face construction and management challenges. The paper proposes a full-cycle digital-twin platform for municipal pipelines and discusses its implementation path within a clarified planning and management mechanism.

## Method ^sec-method

Building on a data-standard system and safety-control scheme, the platform deeply integrates BIM, IoT perception, and artificial intelligence to apply digital twins across the pipeline lifecycle — planning, construction, and operation & maintenance.

## Key Findings ^sec-results

The platform supports intensive, efficient construction and safe operation of municipal pipelines, with application scenarios including 3D pipeline approval, emergency support, and urban physical examination/evaluation. It offers a reference implementation for full-lifecycle infrastructure DTs.

## Discussion & Implications ^sec-discussion

A practical *full-lifecycle infrastructure DT* case integrating BIM + IoT + AI — relevant to the urban/infrastructure DT and lifecycle-integration themes. Useful as an applied exemplar (utility networks) complementing building-focused cases.
