## Abstract ^sec-abstract

The fourth industrial revolution has promised enhanced flexibility, improved quality, and increased productivity in various sectors. In response to this revolution, industries have experienced notable transformations. Project management, a crucial element in the success of construction projects, has also progressed to meet the demands of this new era, commonly referred to as modern project management. This study contributes to the field by identifying obstacles encountered when implementing modern project management in construction and evaluating the impact of building information modeling (BIM) and digital twin technologies. Our findings highlight that digital twin technology can effectively address data and technical challenges compared to BIM. Nevertheless, organizational and cultural barriers remain, highlighting the necessity for additional strategies to be employed.
