## Abstract ^sec-abstract

> It is crucial to assess the structural condition of reinforced concrete (RC) structures to ensure their functionality and safety; however, accurate condition assessment remains challenging owing to uncertainties in geometric modelling, finite element analysis and assessment criteria. This study proposes a digital twin-driven multi-metrics method for condition assessment of eccentrically loaded RC columns with cracks. Three RC columns were tested, with one column used for development and two for validation specimens. The geometric digital twin (gDT) of the RC column was constructed using point cloud data for geometry and rebar scanning for reinforcement layout, thereby reducing geometric uncertainties caused by manufacturers. Building on the gDT, a nonlinear finite element model was constructed as the virtual entity and calibrated to experimental data using an optimisation algorithm, thereby producing a mechanical digital twin (mDT) that mitigates analysis-related inconsistencies. The established digital twin was subsequently used to predict the responses of two validation specimens, achieving prediction errors within 5% for ultimate load-bearing capacity. Finally, a multi-metrics condition assessment framework based on DT was proposed, enabling assessment at each loading level for all RC columns. The results indicate that the DT-driven approach substantially enhances the accuracy of structural condition assessment of the RC columns with cracks.
