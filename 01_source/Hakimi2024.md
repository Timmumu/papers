## Abstract ^sec-abstract
Bibliometric analysis of 248 DT-for-Facility-Management (FM) articles harvested from Scopus and Web of Science. VOSviewer is used to visualize keyword co-occurrence, citation and co-authorship networks. The review maps current focal areas (BIM-based FM, AI predictive maintenance, real-time CPS data integration, lifecycle asset management) and identifies under-developed areas such as AI-based real-time asset PHM, virtual intelligent infrastructure monitoring, deep-learning-aided continuous improvement, semantic interoperability and autonomous control feedback. ^p1

## Method ^sec-method
Quantitative bibliometric review (PRISMA-style screening of 248 papers; VOSviewer co-occurrence and citation network analysis). No primary empirical data — synthesis is corpus-driven. ^p1

## Key Findings ^sec-results

- DT-for-FM literature concentrates on four clusters: BIM-based FM, AI predictive maintenance, real-time CPS data integration, and lifecycle asset management. ^f1

- Autonomous control feedback and semantic data interoperability across the facility lifecycle are the most under-researched DT-for-FM gaps. ^f2

- The DT-for-FM field is geographically concentrated (China, USA, UK) and dominated by a small number of high-output authors and venues. ^f3


## Discussion & Implications ^sec-discussion
Provides quantitative evidence that the operations/FM phase is the centre of gravity of AECO DT research while simultaneously confirming that closed-loop cognitive capability (autonomous control, semantic federation) remains the weakest cluster. This is a load-bearing citation for the SLR's argument that DT maturity in operations is asymmetrically high in observation and low in actuation. ^p1
