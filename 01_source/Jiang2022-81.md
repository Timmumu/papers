## Abstract ^sec-abstract
Introduces a Digital Twin-enabled smart Modular Integrated Construction system (DT-SMiCS) for on-site assembly. On-site resources are converted into Smart MiC Objects (SMiCOs) instrumented with UWB and RFID for real-time nD data (identity, location, cost, progress). Digital twins of SMiCOs are instantiated from unified object-oriented templates and exchanged with cloud services for high-fidelity virtual monitoring and remote control. A testbed robotic demonstration validates the system. ^p1

## Method ^sec-method
System architecture + working prototype with robotic demonstration on a MiC on-site assembly testbed. Combines UWB + RFID instrumentation, object-oriented DT instantiation and a smart mobile gateway for bi-directional data flow. ^p1

## Key Findings ^sec-results

- Object-oriented templating of on-site resources (SMiCOs) is what enables scalable instantiation of construction DTs at item level. ^f1

- Bi-directional cloud services support both monitoring and remote control — moving beyond pure observation toward Level-2 actuation. ^f2

- Robotic execution becomes feasible only when the DT can deliver positionally consistent state to the robot in near real time. ^f3


## Discussion & Implications ^sec-discussion
One of the few construction DT studies that demonstrates a working robotic actuation loop, placing it at the upper boundary of current AECO DT maturity. Highly relevant to the SLR's cognitive/autonomous category. ^p1
