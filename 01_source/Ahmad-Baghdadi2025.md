## Abstract ^sec-abstract

This paper explores the transformative impact of digital twin (DT) technology on the construction industry, providing an in-depth analysis of current trends, challenges, and future trajectories. The study consolidates key insights from scholarly literature, industry reports, and case studies to highlight the multifaceted benefits of DT adoption in construction, including project optimization, resource management, and sustainability. By identifying emerging technologies and trends that are shaping the development of digital twins, this review offers a forward-looking perspective on the evolving role of DT in the construction sector. It also critically examines the limitations and challenges hindering broader adoption, such as data interoperability, lack of standardization, and cultural resistance. This paper not only offers a comprehensive overview of the state-of-the-art in digital twin applications but also identifies key areas for future research and innovation, providing a foundation for the effective implementation of tailored DT solutions in construction projects.
