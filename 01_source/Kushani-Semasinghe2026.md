## Introduction & Background ^sec-intro

Buildings must balance indoor environmental quality (IEQ) — thermal comfort, air quality — against energy efficiency. The review examines how data-driven technologies (AI, ML, digital twins) are applied to improve IEQ while reducing energy use, highlighting the need for intelligent solutions that reconcile occupant comfort and environmental impact.

## Method ^sec-method

A PRISMA-based systematic review identified 152 studies integrating AI, machine learning, and digital twins for IEQ monitoring, prediction, and control.

## Key Findings ^sec-results

Research concentrates on monitoring and predicting IEQ (especially thermal comfort and air quality) and on energy efficiency, using regression and classification models for continuous and categorical IEQ variables. Several studies integrate AI with BIM and IoT to form digital-twin frameworks for real-time performance assessment and adaptive control, though adoption is constrained by data quality, interoperability, and scalability.
