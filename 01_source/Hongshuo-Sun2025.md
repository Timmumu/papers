## Abstract ^sec-abstract

> Bridge performance prediction helps to develop preventive maintenance measures for bridges. To fully utilize the bridge monitoring data and consider the uncertainty in the bridge performance degradation process, as well as to overcome the limitations of using the Gamma process to predict bridge performance, this paper proposes a bridge performance prediction method based on nonlinear stochastic processes. Considering that the digital twin technology can obtain the degradation data of key performance indexes such as stress levels, stiffness levels, and crack severity of bridges through monitoring data, this paper adopted a generalized nonlinear stochastic process to model the degradation data of bridge performance indexes obtained by the digital twin technology, defined the remaining time (RT) for the bridge performance index to reach the failure threshold, and then obtained the probability density function of the RT associated with the degradation model parameters. In the estimation of the parameter values of the bridge performance degradation model, this paper constructed the degradation model as a state-space model, updated the key parameters of the drift function in the state-space model by the Kalman filtering algorithm, and used the expectation maximization (EM) algorithm combined with the Rauch-TungStriebel (RTS) smoothing algorithm to update the hidden states and other parameters in the state space model. Finally, this paper predicted the RT and the RT probability density function for the bridge performance index to reach the failure threshold and the value of the bridge performance index at the next sampling point based on the estimated values of the degradation model parameters. The feasibility and superiority of the method of this paper were verified by numerical simulation and structural tests, respectively.
