ABSTRACT  
The stable and safe operation of Trailing suction hopper dredger (TSHD) is one of the most crucial considerations 
for ensuring its high dredging productivity. However, the instability and sudden failure of physical sensors pose 
challenges to the monitoring of dredging process. To address these issues, we propose a structure of digital twin- 
driven virtual sensor (DTDVS) for the construction safety of TSHD. Considering the potential internal relations 
among construction data, we compare the performance of four machine learning algorithms in predicting the 
torsional vibration in mechanical failure. The results showed that these algorithms provide high prediction ac -
curacy (R
2 
> 0.9). Then the DBN model with the best performance was selected as a part of the virtual sensors to 
predict and analyze the status of TSHD. The digital twin technology provides a more stable and environmentally 
friendly scheme for TSHD construction safety control. On the one hand, the DTDVS assists physical sensors to 
monitor the construction state, overcoming the limitation of the sensors on detection targets which are difficult 
or costly to measure directly. On the other hand, by analyzing the residual between the physical sensor and the 
virtual sensor, the construction behavior can be diagnosed, and the fault situation can be pre-warned accurately. 
This improves the time utilization of TSHD and provides an important guarantee for the construction safety.   
1. Introduction 
Dredging engineering involves excavating the soil of the river or 
seabed and then transport the excavated soil to a designated dumping 
area [ 1 ]. A piece of important dredging equipment, trailing suction 
hopper dredger, is often operated in the sea with complex soil and a 
harsh environment [ 2 ]. Therefore, it is challenging to accurately char -
acterize the TSHD behavior. During each construction period, the 
random load caused by gradual loading, uncertain soil quality, the wind 
and wave [ 3 ] may affect the mechanical properties, reduce the dredging 
efficiency and endanger the construction safety. Therefore, real-time 
monitoring, timely diagnosing and effective pre-warning of the con -
struction status are essential for the efficient operation of the TSHD. 
Physical sensors are important means to monitor the construction 
status. Damen [ 4 ] used a series of high-precision measuring instruments 
such as flowmeter and nuclear densimeter, to detect the construction 
status of dredgers and further obtained a large amount of real-time 
monitoring data. Park et al. [ 5 ] proposed a cloud-based real-time se -
curity monitoring system framework to collect and analyze field con -
ditions data and communicate through the cloud. Based on an optimal 
flow of real-time information, Skibniewski et al. [ 6 ] proposed a project 
management framework for dredging projects to enhances decision 
making and overall control of project performance. Although the 
physical sensors play an important role in construction safety control of 
the TSHD, they have fundamental limitations: (1) the physical sensors 
are at risk of instability and failure caused by complex construction 
environment; (2) establishing the simulation model solely based on the 
physical measurements constrains determining the relationship between 
physical quantities; (3) there are still several construction parameters 
that are too difficult or expensive to be directly measured, e.g., the real- 
time mud concentration in the trailing suction pipe. Furthermore, the 
traditional measurement techniques have a risk of nuclear radiation and 
environmental pollution due to the use of radioactive sources [ 7 ]. 
Therefore, it is necessary to propose a stable and reliable new sensor to 
address the above problems. 
The digital twin is the digital representation of the physical model 
[ 8 ], which realizes the reconstruction, deduction and analysis of the 
physical model in the digital scene [ 9 ]. The data flow between the 
physical system and the network is bidirectional and synchronous [ 10 ]. 
Tuegel et al. [ 11 ] proposed the concept of Digital twin in 2011, where

Later, the digital twin was applied to aerospace [ 12 – 14 ] and

safety was also explored. Wang et al. [ 18 ] proposed a digital twin 
reference model for fault diagnosis in rotating machinery. Then they 
proposed a model updating scheme based on parameter sensitivity

the digital twin technology provides a new way for fault monitoring. 
Deep learning plays a critical role in the digital twin. Hinton et al. 
[ 20 ] proposed the idea of deep learning in 2006. Compared with shallow 
neural networks, deep learning models directly use the original data as 
the input and then learn the features of the data layer by layer to achieve 
a more effective feature representation [ 21 ]. With the development of 
artificial intelligence, deep learning has been widely used in image 
processing [ 22 ], natural language processing [ 23 ], speech recognition 
[ 24 ], and other fields. In recent years, the use of deep learning has been 
examined for safety monitoring [ 25 ]. Bieman et al. [ 26 ] proposed a 
technique based on deep learning combined with video imagery of 
physical model tests to develop a non-intrusive measurement method. 
Furthermore, Bai et al. [ 27 , 28 ] proposed superimposing multiple 
models to improve the accuracy of productivity prediction for the TSHD. 
Tamilselvan et al. [ 29 ] and Tran et al. [ 30 ] also suggested a highly 
reliable health diagnosis method based on the DBN model which can be 
applied to the health diagnosis of aero-engines and power transformers. 
Huang et al. [ 31 ] combined the monitoring system with a forecasting 
model for operational monitoring and prediction of wave run-up on the 
seawalls. Tang et al. [ 32 , 33 ] also proposed a fault handling mechanism 
based on a rule base and a case base. The expert system designed 
identifies the cause of failures and then provides solutions. However, a 
rather large set of fault data and case support is required for their pro -
posed approach. As seen in the above-mentioned researches, the existing 
research on fault detection is often based on the monitoring data of 
traditional physical sensors. In these techniques, the data flows in only 
one direction and feedback to the physical sensors does not exist. 
Therefore, the existing methods are unable to take full advantage of 
digital twin technology and machine learning. And the applicability of 
the above fault detection methods to the TSHD system needs to be 
studied further. 
In this paper, we assert that developing virtual sensing technology 
facilitates intelligent and efficient construction safety control of TSHD. 
Then by analyzing the real-time monitoring data, we propose a virtual 
sensor framework for TSHD based on the digital twin. In the virtual 
model, the operation indexes of mechanical equipment are predicted 
and evaluated. The virtual sensors assist physical sensors in accurate and 
timely diagnosis and further enables pre-warning of the potential me -
chanical faults. 
2. General scheme of digital twin-driven virtual sensor 
Virtual sensors are different from traditional physical sensors. They 
mainly aim at dynamic quantities which are difficult, expensive, or 
harmful to measure directly. Furthermore, in cases where the accuracy 
of physical sensors is declined or they become faulty in the construction 
process, the virtual sensors can replace the physical sensors for contin -
uous monitoring of the construction status. Virtual signals are obtained 
through the internal relations among relevant construction indexes. The 
combination of virtual and physical sensors guarantees the stability of 
dynamic monitoring during construction. In addition, once a fault oc -
curs in the construction process, the difference of parameters would lead 
to great differences between physical signals and virtual signals.

2.1. Physical sensor 
Modern large-scale trailing suction ships are often equipped with a 
supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) system. The basic life 
characteristic parameters of the ship, such as its loading capacity, main 
engine power, and rake head angle are obtained by physical sensors. The 
dredging operation status of the TSHD during the construction process is 
also fully inspected. Table 1 shows the construction monitoring data 
collected by the physical sensors, it is seen that the monitoring data 
contains a total of 793 dimensions. Furthermore, the data is time- 
dependent and noisy hence it may interfere with the data analysis, as 
shown in Fig. 1 . Therefore, to reduce the influence of environmental 
factors and the instability of the instrument itself, it is necessary to 
preprocess the data before analysis to improve the data utilization 
efficiency. 
The trailing suction dredger is equipped with a large number of 
physical sensors, which can directly reflect the dredging construction in 
real time. Although monitoring data can be obtained by physical sen -
sors, if only the physical model is established as the main frame, it is 
difficult to determine the corresponding relationship between physical 
quantities, and the physical sensors have the risk of instability and 
failure. At the same time, some construction parameters are expensive or 
difficult to measure directly, such as the real-time mud concentration in 
the trailing suction pipe of the trailing suction dredger, the nuclear ra -
diation risk and environmental pollution caused by the use of radioac -
tive sources in the traditional measurement technology, and the risk of 
accidents and environmental damage in the measurement. Therefore, it 
is necessary to add the corresponding virtual sensors to assist the 
physical sensors through digital twin technology, so as to improve the 
accuracy of fault monitoring and reduce environmental pollution. 
2.2. Overall framework 
To overcome the shortcomings of physical sensors, the digital twin- 
driven virtual sensor approach is proposed. The digital twin includes 
three main components [ 34 ]: (a) physical model in real space; (b) digital 
model in virtual space; and (c) data and information connection that link 
the digital models with the physical systems. Here we propose the digital 
twin structure for heath management of TSHD, as illustrated in Fig. 2 . 
The specific implementation steps are the following: (1) the physical 
sensors installed on dredgers dynamically sense and collect the TSHD 
construction status, severing as the foundation of digital model estab -
lishment and intelligent analysis; (2) the information management and 
control platform based on machine learning is the key to digital models. 
The virtual sensor preprocesses the construction data and makes a pre -
diction based on the established model and further analyzes the results; 
(3) the virtual sensor provides feedback to the physical sensor. The real- 
time monitoring, diagnosis, and early warning analysis of the TSHD 
construction status are realized by integrating the two sensors. 
2.3. Data preprocessing 
2.3.1. Feature selection 
In the feature selection, features that are closely related to the target 
are extracted from a large number of unknown relationship variables. 
The features are then used in building a model and improve the effi -
ciency accuracy. The quality of the selected features depends on the 
correlation between the feature classes and the degree of feature 
redundancy. The higher the quality of the features, the more efficient the 
corresponding model. 
As mentioned above, the construction monitoring data is high 
dimensional data, however, it is not clear which of the 793 monitoring 
data are the key parameters and which are the redundant parameters. 
Here we propose a method combining Lasso algorithm with random 
forest algorithm to select the best candidate parameters. Firstly, the 
Lasso algorithm is used to screen out the features that have a high cor -
relation with the targets, including a large number of redundant fea -
tures. Then, the redundant features are removed using the correlation 
heat map to obtain better features. 
The basic idea of the Lasso algorithm is to minimize the sum of 
squares of residuals subject to the constraint on the sum of absolute 
values of the regression coefficients. This ensures generating a series of 
regression coefficients strictly equal to 0. The model consists of a linear 
model with a regularization term. Hence the minimized objective 
function is as the following [ 35 ]:

noise interference.

2.4. Prediction and analysis 
To ensure the harmony between the digital model and the physical 
world, it is essential to establish a digital twin model to match the 
physical system. Noting the potential internal relationship of the 
monitoring data of TSHD, here we combine the physical model with an 
artificial intelligent mode and propose the virtual sensor framework. 
The virtual sensor trains and predicts the detection target through ma -
chine learning to realize the auxiliary application of the physical sensors 
and to further improve the accuracy of the construction monitoring. 
Therefore, a highly accurate prediction model is critically important as it 
enables the application of virtual sensors.

3. Digital twin for TSHD construction safety 
The dredging process of the trailing suction ship is affected by the 
characteristics of the construction environment such as soil quality, 
hydrometeorology, etc. The complexity of these characteristics results in 
a risky and unstable dredging process. Digital twinning technology 
provide a new method for dynamic monitoring and health diagnosis. 
The physical and virtual sensors, as well as information transmission, 
are integrated into the TSHD construction safety control to realize 
monitoring, diagnosis, and early warning analysis of the construction 
status. If the target parameter data is abnormal, e.g., the data source is 
missing or part of the data is damaged, it can be supplemented using a 
data-driven perspective. Furthermore if the physical model of the 
problem is known, the digital model and the physical model can be in -
tegrated to evaluate the construction state of TSHD. 
3.1. Stable signal for construction monitoring 
Various physical sensors are installed on the dredger to monitor the 
construction process of TSHD. However, due to the unstable construc -
tion environment, there might be measurement deviations in the phys -
ical sensors. There is also a risk of instrument failure that may result in 
invalid monitoring data. Such data affects the stable monitoring of 
construction status and causes potential safety hazards. Besides, there 
are still some important construction parameters that are either difficult 
to measure directly, or their measurement costs are high. Currently, the 
γ -ray concentration meter widely is used in dredging ships which has 
potential radiation hazards and that causes safety and environmental 
risks. Such techniques cannot be also used in crowded areas. At this 
time, the virtual sensor based on digital twin can play an auxiliary role. 
The key to digital twinning technology lies in combining the digital 
and physical models. The virtual sensor obtains a stable virtual signal by 
predicting the target parameters based on the internal relationship be -
tween the monitoring data in real-time. In cases where there is a prob -
lem with the physical sensor, (see section A in Fig. 5 ) a certain 
monitoring data might be affected with a value of 0. At the same time, 
other sensors operate normally and collect stable and reliable moni -
toring data. The target data can be therefore approximated using DTDVS 
by model prediction This enables continuous operation of the moni -
toring. and ensures the safe operation of dredgers regardless of the faulty 
sensor operation.

significance to continuously monitor the operational state of the ma -
chinery and warn the mechanical failure. Given enough operational 
dataset the DTDVS can obtain a reliable virtual signal by a selecting 
appropriate model. Comparing the data of the virtual sensor and the

4. Application and analysis 
To verify the application of digital twin technology in the TSHD 
construction safety control, Tianjin Port 300,000-ton waterway Phase II 
project is considered as a case study. The channel has a total length of 44 
km and a total dredging volume of 13.5 million m
3
. The main soil in -
cludes muddy clay, silty clay, and sandy silt, etc. The “ Tongcheng ” rake 
suction ship (see Fig. 6 ), has a total installed power of 20,290 kW, a 
cabin capacity of 18,374 m
3
, and a maximum excavation depth of 85 m. 
In normal operating conditions, the TSHD operates 24 h a day without 
interruption, and generates a set of monitoring data every two seconds. 
More than 100 million monitoring data records are collected within 3 
days of construction operations. These data provide an important 
premise for machine learning models, and this paper takes them as 
research samples. 
4.1. Virtual sensor for torsional vibration 
The TSHD is often operated in complicated marine or port environ -
ments. Under the combined action of random alternating loads and 
current harmonics, torsional vibrations are likely to occur in mechanical 
equipment. Torsional vibration includes bending vibration mode and 
torsional vibration mode, etc. The torsional vibrations may increase the 
fatigue damage of bearings, reduce the service life of mechanical 
equipment, and produce high-level noise that harms the workers [ 42 ]; In 
severe cases, resonance may be created which can damage the shafting 
of the unit and affect the construction safety of the TSHD. Therefore, 
torsional vibration is an important monitoring objective of TSHD safety 
monitoring. Here we consider the torsional vibration as the target 
parameter to verify the effectiveness of the virtual sensor.

4.3. Discussion of the application benefits 
Using the digital twin technology facilitates avoiding the risk of 
adopting a single physical model in construction safety control. The 
DTDVS proposed in this paper assists the physical sensors to dynamically 
monitor the construction status of dredgers and to obtain stable virtual 
signals of monitoring targets using a machine learning model. The 
combination of physical and virtual sensors enables early warning of 
potential mechanical faults and effective avoidance of further accidental 
deterioration. Without using the digital twin technology, the mechanical 
failure may be extended causing unnecessary economic costs. 
The monitoring data of the dredger construction before and after the 
accident are also compared and analyzed, see Fig. 14 . As it is seen,

that the application of digital twin technology in TSHD construction 
safety control effectively improves the accuracy of construction safety 
control. 
5. Conclusions 
Based on the real-time monitoring data of trailing suction hopper 
dredger, this paper proposed a framework of a digital twin-driven vir -
tual sensor. In the proposed framework, the physical model was com -
bined with artificial intelligence. Taking advantage of the two-way data 
interaction of digital twins, a construction safety control system was also 
developed for the TSHD mechanical fault monitoring system, realizing 
the real-time diagnosis and early warning of mechanical faults. The 
contributions of this paper are listed as the following:  
(1) The construction monitoring data obtained by the TSHD physical 
sensors was analyzed and it was found that the TSHD 
construction data is high dimensional, noisy, and with a strong 
time correlation. Data preprocessing can effectively solve the 
above problems, which improves the prediction accuracy and 
efficiency of the model in the following virtual sensors.  
(2) A framework of the digital twin-driven virtual sensor was then 
proposed. The favorable performance of DTDVS can be deter -
mined by the high prediction accuracy (R
2 
> 0.9) of four machine 
learning algorithms on torsional vibration. The best performing 
DBN model was considered as the preferred model for the DTDVS 
prediction.  
(3) The application of DTDVS in TSHD construction safety control 
was then examined. The DTDVS assists the physical sensors to 
monitor the target which is either difficult or costly to be directly 
measured. The combination of physical and virtual sensors 
further provides stable and reliable signals in the monitoring 
system, which collectively improve the accuracy of the con -
struction safety control. 
(4) We further developed a TSHD construction monitoring, diag -
nosis, and early warning system according to the interactive 
characteristics of physical and virtual sensors based on digital 
twin technology. Taking torsional vibration types in mechanical 
faults as an example, real-time dynamic monitoring was carried 
out. The system successfully diagnosed the abnormal states and 
the early warning of a mechanical fault. 
Stable and reliable monitoring of TSHD construction status is an 
important task to improve the dredging efficiency and to ensure con -
struction safety. Digital twinning technology overcomes the limitation 
of the traditional measurement method which is at the risk of abnormal 
functioning of the physical sensor. The developed DTDVS was shown to 
be more stable, environmentally friendly, and efficient in enabling the 
safety of dredging operations. The engineering examples also confirmed 
DTDVS efficiency in determining the abnormal operation of machinery 
and providing early warnings, thus further issuing warnings to the 
operator to adjust the operating parameters of the dredger or carry out 
the related maintenance works. Further discussions on the potential 
internal relations and interaction mechanisms among different types of 
faults might be considered as the follow-up work.
