ABSTRACT
In the field of aviation, terminal building renovation and expansion projects frequently involve complex con -
struction processes and the structure is in a non-static state during the construction process, resulting in sig -
nificant safety risks accompanying these processes. In order to visualize and efficiently demonstrate the 
construction of airport terminal building renovation and expansion, and to provide a rapid decision-making 
analysis tool for the control of structural safety risks, a digital twin model for safety risk prediction of steel 
mesh frame structures is proposed. On this basis, taking the construction situation of an airport as an example, 
sensors were installed according to the results of the analysis of the finite element model, and a prediction model 
was established using the height parameters of twelve hydraulic jacks, the ambient temperature data and the 
stress data, in order to predict the stress changes of the key rods of the steel mesh frame. The results show that the 
proposed prediction model can accurately capture the stress distribution of the steel mesh frame during the 
overall jacking process. The stress prediction accuracy can reach 98 % for the key bars. Through this method 
combining digital twin and machine learning, this study provides a new perspective for safety risk management 
in airport terminal building renovation and expansion projects, and verifies its applicability and effectiveness in 
actual projects through empirical studies.
1. Introduction
Airports play a vital role in the transportation system, and as the 
economy develops and the demand for domestic and international 
movement of people and businesses grows steadily, the actual 
throughput of existing airports has exceeded the design capacity at that 
time, and they need to be modernized and expanded [ 1 ]. Airport 
expansion projects usually include the construction of new terminals, 
the expansion of existing terminals and the construction of new runways 
and taxiways. With the increase in the number of large-span steel 
structure forms and the rapid expansion of the construction scale, large- 
span steel structure is a popular structural form for the construction of 
the terminal building. For the large-span steel structure, due to the huge 
and complex structural construction process, construction methods and 
construction techniques are complicated, the probability of risk in the 
construction phase is higher than other structures, while the construc -
tion industry is increasingly demanding for safety management intelli -
gence. Moreover, once an accident occurs in a large and complex 
structure, its impact and consequences are extremely serious, causing 
huge property losses, casualties and serious social impacts [ 2 – 4 ]. 
Therefore, it is crucial to predict the structural safety risks and formulate 
corresponding safety measures in advance to reduce the structural safety 
risks during the construction process and safeguard the safety of con -
struction personnel and the surrounding environment.
The structural safety risks of steel structures have been widely dis -
cussed and investigated in a number of past studies. For example, Reh -
man et al. [ 5 ] used STAAD.ProV8i to analyze and design a steel silo with 
the aim of determining the plate stresses, contour and critical supports, 
beam end force summaries, and rebar shedding in the silo structure. Tian 
et al. [ 6 ] established a finite element model of the structure during 
lifting, simulated the lifting process, and analyzed the effect of the 
displacement difference at the lifting points, proposed a new asyn -
chronous analysis method called single and staggered points, and 
determined the optimal limit value of the displacement difference. Ruan 
et al. [ 7 ] proposed a construction method for sliding construction of 
replacement columns on large steel platforms with uneven elevations, 
which solved the construction challenges of steel sliding and column 
replacement on planes with different elevation angles. Yao et al. [ 8 ]

In recent years, some scholars believe that the traditional empirical 
methods, analytical methods, finite element analysis analysis methods 
can only obtain the mechanical parameters of the structure, can not 
obtain the key factors affecting the safety of the structure, and the 
prediction is low in precision and insufficiently intelligent. Machine 
learning techniques have been widely used in areas such as material 
property prediction [ 16 , 17 ] and image processing [ 18-20 ]. Some 
scholars have also carried out certain research in the field of construc -
tion engineering, real-time tracking of concrete vibration force through 
the relevant machine learning methods to realize the active control of 
concrete vibration quality [ 21 ]. The severity of damaged masonry 
buildings and their failure modes are assessed, among other things, by 
selecting appropriate crack characteristics and combining them with 
information about kinematics and static boundary conditions [ 22 ].
And with the rapid development of information technology, digital 
twin technology has become a key technical means to realize digital 
transformation and intelligent upgrading in the construction industry 
[ 23 ]. By integrating physical models, real-time data and analysis tools, 
digital twins map and simulate the behaviors and states of physical 
entities in virtual space, providing a new solution for monitoring and 
control of complex systems [ 24 ]. Although digital twins have been 
widely used in many fields such as smart manufacturing, smart city 
construction, healthcare, etc., the research in the field of safety of large- 
span steel structure construction is still in the exploratory stage.
Currently, the existing studies mainly focus on the theoretical 
methods and framework construction of steel structure construction 
safety assessment. For example, Liu et al. [ 25 ] proposed an intelligent 
safety assessment method for prestressed steel structures based on dig -
ital twins, which improves the accuracy of construction safety assess -
ment by fusing spatio-temporal multidimensional information. Zhu et al. 
[ 26 ] further combined digital twins with random forest algorithms to 
propose an intelligent analysis method for identifying key factors 
affecting the safety of prestressed steel structures. In addition, other 
studies have also combined digital twins with machine learning algo -
rithms to improve the accuracy and real-time performance of structural 
safety assessment [ 27 , 28 ]. However, the structural forms are basically 
cable network structures, and the digital twins of experimental and 
simulated states are mainly considered, while there are few cases of 
studying the actual construction of large-span steel mesh frame struc -
tures at present.
This study is based on the construction process of the overall jacking 
of the steel roof grid structure of an airport renovation and expansion 
project, using digital twin technology, activating and passivating the 
cells through the finite element software Midas/Gen by means of the 
“life-and-death cell method”, and simulating the construction of the 
overall jacking phase of the solid grid, using the completed grid as the 
initial state of the jacking phase, and then simulating the overall jacking, 
installing sensors on the calculated key force members to collect data for 
the trial jacking and formal jacking, and then monitoring the construc -
tion process based on the actual data. The initial state of the jacking 
stage is the assembled mesh frame, and then the simulation of overall 
jacking is carried out. Sensors are installed on the calculated key force 
members to collect the data of trial jacking and formal jacking to realize 
the monitoring of the construction process, and then features are 
extracted based on the actual data, and the prediction models of GA-BP 
neural network, Random Forest and XGBoost are constructed, and the 
prediction accuracy and generalization ability of the three networks are 
compared and analyzed. Comprehensive use of multiple methods can 
improve the accuracy and reliability of the stress prediction of the steel 
mesh frame, and also provides a reference for future projects using the 
overall jacking method for construction.
2. Methodology
Fig. 1 illustrates the different parts of the proposed framework. The 
framework is divided into three main parts. First, the characteristics of 
structural safety risks are analyzed. The second part uses tools such as 
finite elements to build a digital twin model identical to the real con -
struction project and data collection, and the third part builds a machine 
learning model to predict the safety risks during the construction of the 
terminal building.
The first part of this flowchart is to conduct a preliminary study to 
identify the various types of factors that may affect its structural safety 
risk and to explore the prediction strategy of structural safety risk based 
on the identified factors. The second part of the flowchart is to collect 
data from the construction through on-site fieldwork studies and with 
the help of various types of geometrical models created through BIM 
technology in the design phase to generate the corresponding finite 
element physical models and to arrange the sensors to form a digital 
twin model in the end. These fieldwork research studies aim to prepare a 
dataset of the actual state of the structural safety risk samples, whose 
specific characteristics have been characterized in the first part. In the 
third part, the dataset is utilized to build and train a machine learning 
model to learn the relationship between the features, and then the ma -
chine learning model is evaluated by appropriate performance metrics, 
and ultimately the structural safety risk is predicted using a superior 
predictive model.

2.1. Digital twin framework
Digital twin technology is the key technology to realize the cross- 
fertilization of time and space dimensions, which can collect dynamic 
data from physical and virtual environments, realize the integration of 
physical information [ 29 ], and use machine learning algorithms as the 
driving force for real-time storage and processing of massive data 
[ 30 , 31 ].
Digital twin models enable one-to-one real-time simulation and 
mapping of structures under construction [ 23 ]. As shown in Fig. 2 , a 
digital twin framework for construction risk prediction is established, i. 
e., modeling the geometric and physical dimensions of the solid struc -
ture, as well as the behavioral and rule dimensions that conform to the 
overall jacking construction scheme and related specifications, so as to 
realize the acquisition of structural information. Subsequently, through 
the real-time monitoring of sensors, the massive data is acquired and a 
prediction model is built to realize the prediction of structural safety 
risks, and ultimately realize the control and guidance of the overall 
construction.
2.2. Correlation prediction algorithms
Through the actual project and Midas finite element software anal -
ysis, it can be seen that the stresses and displacements in the key parts of 
the steel mesh roof cover using the overall jacking method under the 
time-varying conditions are affected by the height of the jacking frame. 
The analytical method and the method of empirical formulas and his -
torical data are suitable for relatively simple structures, while the finite 
element method cannot predict the structural stress and displacement at 
the next moment in real time, which is obviously not fully applicable for 
the need to pay attention to the safety risk of the steel mesh frame during 
the construction of the overall jacking, so it is necessary to realize the 
monitoring and prediction of the structural safety risk through the al -
gorithm based on machine learning. Most of the previous researchers 
use RF (Random Forest) algorithm and BPNN (Backpropagation neural 
network) when they use machine learning methods to study the safety 
problems related to steel structure, and this study is the first time to use 
XGBoost (Extreme Gradient Boosting) algorithm to construct the model.
2.2.1. BPNN
BPNN is a common artificial neural network model for supervised 
learning. It continuously adjusts the weights in the network through a 
back propagation algorithm to minimize the error between the output 
and the target. BP neural network usually consists of an input layer, a 
hidden layer, and an output layer, where each neuron is connected to all 
the neurons in the previous layer and has weights and biases. During 
training, the weights and biases are updated by a back propagation al -
gorithm to make the output of the network as close as possible to the true 
value. However, the loss function of traditional BP neural networks is 
usually non-convex, such as easy to fall into local optimum and slow 
convergence. To overcome these problems, researchers often use opti -
mization algorithms such as GA (Genetic Algorithm) and PSO (Particle 
Swarm Optimization) to improve BPNN. Genetic algorithm is an

2.2.2. RF
RF is an integrated learning algorithm that improves the accuracy 
and stability of predictions by constructing a combination of multiple 
decision trees. During the construction of RF, each tree is trained inde -
pendently based on a randomly selected subset of features and randomly 
sampled samples, and this randomness reduces overfitting, increases the 
generalization ability of the model, and also makes the whole forest 
more robust. Each decision tree makes a classification or regression 
prediction on its input samples, and then RF arrives at the final pre -
diction by integrating the predictions of all trees and voting or averaging 
them. This integration strategy helps to reduce the errors that may arise 
from a single decision tree and improves the stability and accuracy of the 
overall prediction. RF can be used not only for classification problems, 
but also for regression problems, and usually exhibits good performance. 
RF also has the advantages of being easy to implement, insensitive to 
feature selection, and capable of evaluating feature importance. 
Therefore, RF has gained much attention in practical applications 
[ 33 , 34 ].
2.2.3. XGBoost
XGBoost is an efficient and scalable gradient boosting decision tree 
algorithm that optimizes the loss function and model complexity to 
minimize the prediction error and control overfitting. In XGBoost, a new 
decision tree is added in each iteration to fit the residuals of the previous 
round, and the true value is continuously approximated by accumu -
lating the predictions of all trees. In order to improve the efficiency, 
XGBoost uses various optimization strategies such as column sampling, 
regularization, and second-order derivative information to accelerate 
the optimization process. In addition, XGBoost supports parallelized 
computation, which can make full use of multi-core CPUs or distributed 
computing resources to further improve the training speed. These fea -
tures enable XGBoost to perform well on large-scale datasets, and it has 
achieved excellent results in many machine learning competitions and 
real-world applications [ 35 , 36 ].

example, as the airport has exceeded its design capacity, in order to

of the airport ’ s aviation business volume. At the same time, it is 
conducive to the creation of local three-dimensional transportation, 
promote industrial agglomeration and development, and drive urban

4. Prediction models
4.1. Data acquisition and processing
In the actual construction process, the stress of key rods, the height of 
hydraulic jacks, the ambient temperature and other data are collected 
through sensors and hydraulic synchronization computer control system 
and other devices. The sensor and the corresponding jacking system are 
shown in Fig. 8 .
To establish the measured data set corresponding to the finite 
element model data, it is necessary to process and analyze the collected 
actual data, extract the key information, and combine it with the 
simulation data. The actual data collected at the construction site are 
generally characterized by high dimensionality, high noise, and serious 
covariance, and need to be pre-processed, including data screening, 
anomaly processing, and data noise reduction. And when necessary, the 
data need to be standardized to eliminate the effect of orders of 
magnitude and units. Because in the use of machine learning algorithms, 
if you do not remove the independent variables with a significant linear 
relationship, it will cause the model performance degradation.
4.1.1. Training sample
Measured data may be missing or abnormal due to sensor perfor -
mance and external factors [ 37 ], and processing of missing and 
abnormal data is an important prerequisite to ensure the effectiveness of 
model prediction. Each sensor works independently and does not 
interact with each other, but the detected temperature data may be 
slightly fluctuating due to direct sunlight or being shaded by rods, so the 
temperature data is averaged as a feature of the input data. At the same 
time, the height of the twelve jacking frames is also used as a feature of 
the output data, which together with the temperature data constitutes 
the input data, and the structural stress data monitored by each sensor 
constitutes the output feature of the data ( Table 4 ).
In order to analyze and study the structural safety risk of steel mesh 
frame roof cover, this study selects one of the most representative 
datasets for experiments. The monitoring data are the jacking height 
data from the beginning of jacking to the jacking pause with the stress 
data of the corresponding steel mesh frame key rods, and the sensor 
monitoring data are collected according to the jacking construction date 
of the project (10/3, 10/4, 10/6, and 10/7, four days in total).
First, the data set is screened to extract the jacking frame monitoring 
data, stress sensor monitoring data, and temperature sensor monitoring 
data of the site, and a total of 500 sets of valid data are obtained, and due 
to the large amount of data, only some sets of corresponding data are 
shown in this study as shown in Appendix Table A1 and A2 . Missing 
value processing is carried out for each feature of input data and output 
data in turn, and the processing strategy is selected according to the 
missing proportion p. Linear interpolation is utilized to make up the 
missing values when the missing proportion is lower than p. After that, 
outliers are processed, and outliers usually refer to observations that 
deviate far from the whole sample as a whole. Detect outliers according 
to the data distribution, calculate the mean μ and standard deviation σ of 
each data feature respectively, and the observed values are considered 
outliers when they are larger than μ + 3 σ or smaller than μ -3 σ . Linear 
interpolation filling method is used to deal with the outliers, i.e., linear 
interpolation is performed on the data adjacent before and after the 
outliers, and the interpolation results are used to replace the outliers.
After a thorough cleaning of the data, including dealing with missing 
values and outliers, we performed data noise reduction. For the jacking 
frame data, we observed relatively smooth changes in the values with a 
limited range of fluctuations, so we decided not to perform additional 
noise reduction on it. This decision was based on a careful analysis of the 
data characteristics to ensure the efficiency and accuracy of the subse -
quent analysis. In contrast, the data from the stress sensors showed 
significant fluctuations, which could have an impact on the accuracy of 
structural health monitoring. To address this issue, a classical data 
smoothing technique, the sliding mean filtering algorithm, is used. This 
algorithm reduces the effect of random noise by creating a sliding 
window of size m and averaging the data points within the window. In

5. Results and discussion
The established prediction model of the degree of inclination of the 
sinkhole is tested using the completed preprocessed sensor monitoring 
data. The prediction model adopts GA-BP neural network, RF algorithm 
and XGBoost framework, respectively. After each round of training and 
testing, the predicted stress values of key structural rods are output, and 
the predicted values are compared with the corresponding measured 
data. In order to visualize the prediction effect, the discounted graphs 
comparing the predicted values with the actual values and the model 
fitting effect graphs are drawn, with the first line being the GA-BPNN- 
based prediction model, the second line being the RF-based prediction 
model, and the third line being the XGBoost-based prediction model. For 
when the actual value is closer to the predicted value, the deviation of 
the corresponding line graph is smaller, and the fitting effect graph is 
closer to the dotted line in the figure. From the last line of the picture, it 
can be seen that except for some points sporadically distributed in the 
region farther away from the dotted line, the rest of the points are 
centrally distributed near the dotted line, indicating that the predicted 
value is very close to the actual value, and the consistency between the 
two is good, and the model achieves better prediction results. As shown 
in Fig. 10 .
In order to make the prediction effect more significant, quantitative 
indexes are used to evaluate the prediction effect of the above three 
models, MSE, MAE, MBE and R2, which are calculated as shown in the 
Table 6 . The table shows that the RF and XGBoost models perform 
similarly, and the goodness-of-fit R2 is significantly better than that of 
the GA-BPNN model. Although the validation set R2 of the RF model is 
closer to the training set R2, the MAE value is larger and R2 is smaller, so 
there is a certain degree of overfitting, while the XGBoost model has no 
obvious overfitting or underfitting.
The MSE values of the two models for structural stress prediction are 
less than 5, the MAE values are less than 0.2, the MBE values are less 
than 0.1, and the R2 values are more than 0.9. Combined with the mean 
values of the evaluation indexes, it can be seen that the prediction ac -
curacies of the models based on the RF algorithm are slightly lower than 
that of the model based on the XGBoost, but the differences in the pre -
diction accuracies of the two models are relatively small, and the pre -
diction results are sufficient to meet the needs of the project.
Further, given the total sample size of this study, the use of GA-BPNN 
calculations would result in poor accuracy. The intention of this study is 
to predict the stresses on critical bars during construction and to use a 
small amount of data during trial jacking for training, which requires a 
more comprehensive use of data in constructing the model. XGBoost 
comes with cross-validation during training, which excludes the prob -
lem of model overfitting. Therefore, the XGBoost algorithm chosen in 
this study is more effective in constructing the model.
6. Conclusions
In this paper, a structural safety risk prediction method for airport 
terminal building construction with comprehensive consideration of 
spatial and temporal changes is proposed, focusing on the overall jack -
ing process of steel mesh frame roof. The prediction of stresses in key 
parts of the steel mesh frame is realized through the steps of establishing 
a structural model, simulating the overall jacking construction, arran -
ging and installing sensors, data acquisition and processing, and estab -
lishing a stress prediction model. This method provides a new idea and 
method for the safety management of the terminal building construction 
project, which has certain theoretical and practical significance The 
main research conclusions and prospects are as follows:
The performance of three machine learning prediction models, GA- 
BPNN, RF and XGBoost, is compared, and the XGBoost model has the

In order to further enhance the application value and scientific 
contribution of this research, future work will focus on the following key 
directions: 
1. Multi-source data fusion and integration with real-time monitoring

research will be devoted to optimizing existing machine learning 
algorithms and adjusting parameters to improve prediction accuracy

the generalization ability of the model under different types of steel
