2. Related work 
At present, the rapid development of science and technology has promoted the progress of space technology, and more and more 
people have studied it. Among them, Moridi M A used wireless sensor network (WSN) to analyze and study tunnel and underground 
space technology. The authors introduce static node deployment of ZigBee topology in 12, 20, 30, 40 and 50 node scenarios for 
performance analysis and performance evaluation in underground environment. It includes throughput, packet delivery rate (PDR), 
end-to-end latency, energy consumption, and packet delivery security. However, this method requires higher operation in practice [1] . 
Ding Z studied a new type of mobile flood wall. In the detection of a subway station, the model test was used to verify the safety and 
anti-leakage capability of the model, and the safety factor k = 1.14 > 1 was obtained, which met the strength requirements. However, 
this process is complicated and requires a large amount of accurate data as support [2] . Lv X reveals the key factors affecting the 
construction of underground spaces and establishes theoretical relationships between torque, propulsion and soil strength parameters 
(i.e. unconfined compressive strength, shear strength and cohesion). It uses 3D flexible boundary loading device to carry out 75 rotary 
excavation model laboratory tests on 15 groups of silty clay. According to the test results, the theoretical relationship was simplified, 
the model parameters were determined, and 15 quantitative relationships with 5 different water contents (4.8%) were obtained. 
Finally, the quantitative correlation was verified by field application. The results show that it is feasible to predict soil in-service 
strength [3] . Yamaguchi K simulated three-dimensional groundwater flow around an abandoned mine to assess the impact of back -
filling the mine ’ s underground space on reducing acid mine drainage (AMD). The authors calculated steady-state groundwater flow for 
two days of high and low rainfall to calibrate the model. The calculated AMD groundwater level and flow rate are consistent with the 
measured results. Results when backfilling the excavated underground space showed that AMD ’ s flow was reduced by 5% to 30%. This 
suggests that backfilling the excavation space is one of the effective ways to reduce the amount of discarded AMD. However, this 
evaluation model has certain errors and the accuracy is not high enough [4] . Wang Z ’ s study used the CRD (Cross Diagram) method 
and the four-step method to excavate large-diameter shallow-buried double tunnels in soft soil foundations. According to the on-site 
monitoring results, the effects of construction factors such as tunnel excavation method and construction speed, and environmental 
factors such as soil conditions and continuous rainfall on the settlement of the ground surface and tunnel vault are analyzed. For 
construction factors, it is shown that the surface subsidence caused by the four-step method is 332% of that caused by the argillaceous 
siltstone CRD method. However, due to the complex environmental factors on site, the requirements for the machine are relatively 
high [5] . Peng J B studied the three-dimensional motion generated by ground fissures in Xi ’ an, among which vertical displacement has 
the greatest impact on subway engineering. The results of large-scale physical model tests show that the activity of ground fissures may 
lead to damages such as tunnel lining cracks, waterproof failure and subway track deformation. In order to alleviate such risks, the 
author proposed a series of engineering countermeasures, such as expanding the tunnel section to maintain clearance, adopting 
segmented tunnels, strengthening tunnel linings, and adopting various waterproof measures [6] . Lee Y K used the factor of safety (FOS) 
index to quantify the degree of safety of various rock structures. The authors used the Mohr-Coulomb and Hoek-Brown failure criteria

3. Overall planning method of underground space based on digital twin 
3.1. Digital twin model 
(1) Five elements of the digital twin model 
Digital twin is also called digital twin and digital mapping. It is a large number of physical and digital models correspondingly 
generated by enterprises in the process of implementing model-based systems engineering [8] . As the mapping of virtual space to 
physical products, it reflects the whole process of introduction, growth, maturity and decline of physical products. Digital twin consists 
of five elements: service system, physical entity, virtual model, information connection, and twin data. It evolves through mutual 
learning between related digital models. It also improves the decision making of the winners during the life cycle of the physical entity 
[ 9 , 10 ]. Fig. 1 shows the five-dimensional model of the digital twin. 
When working with digital twins, the physical entity is first analyzed in terms of requirements and application objects. Then, the 
corresponding virtual digital twin model is constructed, and the interactive connection is established to realize the data interaction 
between the virtual and the real and the service system [11] . It uses big data to analyze twin data, and finally provides users with the 
required service system. The detailed composition of the five elements of the digital twin is shown in Fig. 2 . 
Digital twins should follow the following rules. The "physical entity" is the application carrier. The entire model should be based on 
physical entities and run with them. "Virtual model" is the key core to ensure the realization of model functions. "Interconnection" is a 
topological network that connects various elements into a whole, and connects various elements to realize information exchange. The 
"twin data" is the key element that drives the operation of the entire model. "Service system" is a data analysis tool, responsible for data 
calculation and model improvement. It helps users to judge and make decisions.The above elements are derived from the framework of 
the digital twin technical architecture. In terms of digital twin fields, the five elements have realized the functional module of digital 
twin technology. 
(2) Construction criteria of digital twin model 
The digital twin model has the construction criteria of "four modernizations, four possibilities, and eight uses" [12] . In the process of 
operation, it should meet the requirements of "four possibilities" and "four modernizations". The final result should meet the standard 
of "eight uses". The digital twin model construction guidelines are shown in Fig. 3 . 
Digital twins use sensors to perceive real-time changes in physical entities by constructing virtual models that map physical entities 
and their inherent laws [13] . The digital twin model can predict the changing trends of physical entities and allow the physical entity 
space to perceive these changes in advance. When making decisions in the physical entity space, the optimal control scheme can be 
selected in advance for the physical entity. Therefore, it is very meaningful to apply digital twin technology to the intelligent overall 
planning of underground space.

(1) Time data table 
Time-based data tables are an important way to visualize and manage underground spaces. The data sheet is mainly responsible for 
statistics and summary analysis in underground space safety management. It is helpful for managers to grasp the situation of un -
derground space changing with time [19] . Under the safety monitoring of underground space, the data table can summarize the data 
changes of key targets over a period of time. For example, for the monitoring of the integrated pipe gallery in the underground space, 
the time data table can make statistics on the temperature and humidity of the integrated pipe gallery based on time. This makes it easy 
for managers to control the temperature and humidity of the integrated pipe gallery over time and solve problems. As shown in Table 4 .

are 12 and 14, respectively

effect on hidden dangers and dangerous sources during construction . The time analysis chart plays a vital role in monitoring the safety 
management of underground space, analyzing the hidden dangers, and counting the sources of danger. The following takes the air-

4.2. Numerical simulation of underground space construction process 
(1) Analysis of the initial state of the surface 
In conventional structural design, when calculating the internal force of the superstructure and the additional settlement of the 
foundation, the superstructure and the foundation are calculated as two independent components. First of all, it uses the building 
foundation as a fixed support, and calculates the internal force of the structure and the reaction force of the foundation under the load 
of the superstructure. Then, the foundation reaction force is reacted to the elastic foundation soil, and then the foundation internal 
force and additional foundation settlement are calculated.Such as daily earthquakes, the interaction of soil and structure during 
earthquakes is a common problem However, this method does not take into account the effects of soil-structure interaction. As a result, 
some parts of the superstructure are unsafe. The simulation based on the digital twin can restore a real stress state of the earth’s surface. 
While there are buildings on the surface, it can also simulate the actual force characteristics and underground structures.

5. Discussion 
This article uses digital twin technology to design research on underground space . This paper is devoted to studying the opti -
mization model based on digital twin and applying it to the intelligent overall planning of underground space. Because of the 
insufficient equipment and data collection before, the intelligent planning of the underground space was delayed. Provide some 
references for overall underground space planning on the basis of traditional planning. It not only expands the application scope of the 
digital twin model, but also is a new attempt for the overall planning of underground space. Through the simulation of underground 
space construction and fire accidents, the digital twin model is mined as an important method to study underground space. In order to 
optimize the overall planning of underground space and realize intelligent management, it needs to use digital twin technology as a 
guide to analyze each part of the construction and development of underground space in detail. 
Through the analysis of this paper, it is shown that the application of digital twin technology to the intelligent overall planning of 
underground space is very effective. Digital twin technology can play an important role in the construction, monitoring and disaster 
prevention of underground space. This article first explains the digital twin model and introduces the five elements of the digital twin 
model. Then it briefly describes the algorithm of the digital twin, which evaluates the risk of safety accidents in underground space. It 
determines the methods of underground space development and construction and disaster prevention monitoring by simulating the 
construction process and safety accidents. The visualization data of underground space, the law of construction settlement and the 
most suitable imaging equipment for disaster prevention monitoring are obtained. 
6. Conclusions 
This article uses digital twin technology to design research on underground space . Through the analysis of this paper, the following 
conclusions are drawn: (1) The informatization and visualization method based on digital twin is an important means of underground 
space planning and management. Due to the characteristics of closed space, large negative externality, and difficulty in rescue, the 
traditional methods have been unable to meet the modern planning and management of underground space. The visualization 
technology under the guidance of digital twin technology can provide a strong guarantee for the overall planning of underground 
space. (2) Digital twin technology realizes digital collaboration through attribute information and associated information. It turns the 
underground space into a visual space that can be fully perceived. This is conducive to the construction of a new intelligent overall 
planning model for underground space. (3) The digital twin technology can monitor the underground space in real time, and monitor 
and analyze the real-time data. It will report abnormal data in time and correct errors. This helps to provide managers with more 
accurate, efficient and convenient management and decision-making. This provides greater possibilities for data judgment and 
analysis, the display of planning results, and the prevention and control of safety accidents. (4) During the construction of the un -
derground space, based on the digital twin technology, the maximum settlement of the column bottom is 21.897 mm. The maximum 
settlement difference between adjacent column foundations is 2.886 mm. This is in line with the requirements of the current code for 
the settlement of the building column bottom of 3 cm and the difference between the settlement of the adjacent column base of 1 cm. 
When excavating a large area of soil, the settlement of the foundation is 5.6 mm. When the pile side soil is removed, the settlement of 
the surface building is 0.8 mm. This is in line with the 1 cm adjacent column foundation settlement difference requirement stipulated 
by the current code. This shows that the construction scheme based on digital twin technology is feasible, safe and standardized, and 
the impact on the surface buildings is also very small.
