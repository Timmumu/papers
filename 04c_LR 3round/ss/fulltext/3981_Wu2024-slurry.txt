ABSTRACT  
Abnormal large-diameter slurry shield attitudes (SA) lead to safety and quality problems in shield construction. 
To achieve intelligent prediction and optimization of the large-diameter slurry SA, a combination of Bayesian 
optimization categorical boosting (BO-CatBoost) and a multiobjective evolutionary algorithm based on decom -
position (MOEAD) is proposed for the prediction and optimization of the tunnel large-diameter slurry SA, as well 
as the corresponding digital twin framework. Using the Yangtze River large-diameter slurry shield project in 
Wuhan as an example, data were collected for three soil conditions, namely, silty fine sand, angular gravelly soils 
and chalky clays, and gravelly soil. The results show that: (1) The R2 values of the three predicted types of soils 
are all above 0.9, the RMSE values are less than 1.2, and the MAE values are less than 0.9. (2) Based on the results 
of the SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP), the propulsive thrust had the greatest influence on the large- 
diameter slurry SA. (3) The large-diameter slurry SA optimization improves when the number of adjusted 
construction parameters increases, with a maximum optimization rate of 23.436%. In addition, a digital twin 
framework based on BO-CatBoost-MOEAD is proposed, which can effectively control large-diameter slurry SAs 
and improve overall project safety and quality.   
1. Introduction 
With the rapid development of the transportation industry, which 
has prompted tunnel engineering technology to develop toward large 
cross-sections, large depths and long distances, the application of large 
section tunnels has become an important direction for domestic traffic 
construction [ 1 , 2 ]. Slurry-balanced large shields are suitable for envi -
ronments with abundant groundwater and complex hydrogeological 
conditions [ 3 ]. Therefore, the use of slurry-balanced large shields has 
been increasingly applied in tunnel construction and buildings in recent 
years [ 4 ], and some scholars have begun to study slurry-balanced 
large-diameter shields [ 5 , 6 ]. The number of tunnels constructed with 
slurry shields is also increasing. A large shield has a large palm surface, 
and due to complex geologic conditions, cutter plate cutting encounters 
complex geological conditions, increasing the difficulty of controlling 
the SA [ 7 ]. Abnormal SA can result in axis deviation [ 8 ], snaking [ 9 ], 
tube sheet extrusion [ 10 ], and even shield head destabilization, causing 
serious safety and quality problems. Therefore, research on 
large-diameter slurry SAs is especially urgent and important. In actual 
engineering, large-diameter slurry SAs are mostly adjusted manually by 
drivers based on experience, which has disadvantages such as lag, and 
large-diameter slurry SAs must be adjusted in a timely and intelligent 
manner. 
Scholars at home and abroad have performed considerable research 
on the attitude prediction of large-diameter shields, as shown in Table 1 . 
The methods used to predict large-diameter slurry SAs in the existing 
research can be categorized into three types: theoretical formula 
methods, finite element simulations and intelligent algorithms [ 11 ]. In 
the theoretical formula method, SA anomalies in a specific tunnel 
project are analyzed. The factors affecting the SA of a particular tunnel 
are analyzed according to the geological conditions, construction 
schedule, and other conditions of the particular project. Guan et al. [ 12 ] 
developed an inclined shaft shield head adjustment model to study the 
effects of changes in the tunnel design slope angle on the head adjust -
ment capacity. Shi et al. [ 13 ] developed a position – attitude error model

the prediction of shield tunnel parameters. Zhang et al. [19] proposed

network (ANN) to forecast ground settlement during shield tunneling.

chine, and gradient boosting regression to predict the tunneling dy -
namics of shields via a genetic algorithm and principal component 
analysis. Zhang et al. [21] proposed an algorithm incorporating particle 
swarm optimization (PSO) and random forest (RF) to optimize the 
shielding work parameters when the settlement exceeds the tolerance 
value. Wang et al. [22] constructed an extreme gradient boosting 
(XGBoost) prediction model and predicted and analyzed the changes in 
axis deviation using shield construction parameters. The above methods 
contribute to SA prediction. However, when the data volume is overly 
large, these methods require large amounts of training time and mem -
ory. If the memory is limited, running the method may not be possible, 
and thus, the SA cannot be predicted and adjusted. Therefore, a more 
applicable method is needed to quickly process a large amount of data 
for the efficient and accurate prediction of large SAs. 
To reduce prediction error during training, categorical boosting 
(CatBoost) was proposed by Yandex in 2018; CatBoost enables ordered 
target statistics and ordered augmentation [26]. CatBoost occupies less 
memory at runtime, has a shorter training time [26,27], and can better 
avoid prediction bias than can machine learning methods such as 
XGBoost, thus improving the veracity of the model [28]. These benefits 
are complemented by CatBoost’s effectiveness with diverse data types, 
reducing the need for hyperparameter tuning and minimizing over -
fitting risks for strong generalizability [29]. Since it was first proposed in 
2018, CatBoost has achieved success in machine learning studies on 
large datasets, such as the classification and prediction of data [26]. 
Yang et al. [30] proposed a novel model mixed sparrow search algorithm 
and CatBoost, which has better accuracy in credit rating classification

To ensure shield construction safety and obtain more stable large- 
diameter slurry SAs, multiobjective optimization of large-diameter 
slurry SAs is needed. Sun et al. [ 36 ] proposed a shield tunnel param -
eter matching model that utilizes a support vector machine (SVM) and 
an enhanced PSO algorithm to optimize the tunnel parameters. Ge et al. 
[ 37 ] proposed a deep belief network (DBN) approach optimized by the 
whale optimization algorithm (WOA) for predicting ground subsidence 
and segmental floatation. These methods optimize the performance 
metrics of shields very well. However, more than one index exists for 
expressing large SAs, and multiobjective optimization must be 
completed. The multiobjective evolutionary algorithm based on 
decomposition (MOEAD) provides a solution by decomposing the opti -
mization problem into N scalar subproblems and solving them simul -
taneously through population evolution [ 38 ]. Compared to traditional 
methods such as multiobjective genetic local search (MOGLS) and 
nondominated sorting genetic algorithm II (NSGA-II), MOEAD exhibits 
enhanced search efficiency and lower computational complexity, 
particularly in continuous multiobjective optimization problems [ 39 , 
40 ]. Thus, MOEAD is widely used in classification [ 41 ], scheduling [ 42 ], 
and similar operations. Qu et al. [ 39 ]. used NSGA-II and MOEAD to 
transform the Navy ’ s problem of efficient missile boat scheduling into a 
multiobjective optimization problem and to search for an approximate 
Pareto-optimal set for the problem. Tan et al. [ 43 ] presented a robust 
optimization algorithm utilizing MOEAD to concurrently optimize both 
the total distance and the number of vehicles required for trans -
portation. To enhance the effectiveness and success rate of MOEAD in 
multiobjective optimization, this paper utilizes the nonlinear mapping 
correlation obtained from the CatBoost prediction model as the fitness 
function [ 44 ]. Therefore, in this paper, an SA intelligent prediction alert 
and control optimization model is established based on 
CatBoost-MOEAD. 
Additionally, hyperparameter tuning is an important step in machine 
learning and significantly impacts the performance of machine learning 
methods. Manually tuning the parameters is an extremely long and 
complex process. Grid search (GS) and random search (RS) are 
commonly employed for hyperparameter tuning. Although GS is known 
for its simplicity and reliability in low-dimensional space, it may not 
yield satisfactory results in high-dimensional space [ 45 ]. RS is simple 
and easily parallelizable, yet its outcomes are not guaranteed to be 
optimal. In contrast to both GS and RS, BO outperforms the other 
methods in terms of optimization performance. Moreover, BO leverages 
the results obtained from previous tests on hyperparameter values to 
inform the selection of the next hyperparameter values, thereby 
enhancing the optimization rate [ 46 ]. BO provides clear access to the 
optimized values of all the hyperparameters, saving time while 
improving the model accuracy [ 47 ]. Sun et al. [ 48 ] used the Bayesian 
algorithm to optimize hyperparameters for logistic regression and RF 
models, and the accuracy of both models improved after optimization. 
Shen et al. [ 49 ] used a BO approach to optimize the hyperparameters of 
a deep neural network (DNN) for predicting engine output NOx emis -
sions. Hence, the BO-CatBoost-MOEAD hybrid algorithm is used to 
construct a large-diameter slurry SA prediction and optimization model. 
However, CatBoost can show how much the inputs affect the outputs 
as a whole but cannot illustrate how changes in the inputs affect the 
outputs [ 24 , 50 ]. SHAP uses a game-theoretic approach to analyze how 
different values of each input feature affect the model results [ 51 ], 
which can ensure that the prediction results have good global and local 
interpretability [ 52 ]. Therefore, SHAP is often used in various fields 
[ 53-55 ]. Fabio et al. [ 51 ] used SHAP to determine that features related 
to type of crime, age at first arrest and drug use were most relevant for 
predicting internal misbehavior. Lin et al. [ 52 ] used SHAP to enhance 
prediction model accuracy by selecting features with significant con -
tributions from the initial feature set. Therefore, in this paper, SHAP is 
used to analyze the prediction results by specifically assessing the im -
pacts of different construction parameters on large-diameter slurry SAs. 
This analysis facilitates the effective control of key factors. 
This study addresses the following primary research questions: (1) 
How can a nonlinear relationship between the input parameters and 
large-diameter slurry SA be established? How can the real-time con -
struction parameters of large-diameter slurry shields be used to predict 
the attitude of the shield? (2) How can the impact of each input 
parameter on the large-diameter slurry SA be the key optimization 
parameter? (3) How can multiobjective optimization of a large SA be 
achieved and regulated to obtain a stable large-diameter slurry SA with 
real-time warning and control? Based on these questions, BO-CatBoost is 
used in this study to create an accurate prediction model for large- 
diameter slurry SAs, accounting for diverse factors influencing this 
model. A multiobjective optimization model is constructed based on 
MOEAD to regulate the deviation of the large-diameter slurry SA. 
The contributions of this study are as follows: (1) A combined BO- 
CatBoost approach is proposed to express the nonlinear mapping cor -
relation between the influencing factors and the prediction target as a 
fitness function, and real-time shield construction parameters are used 
to predict the attitude safety of large-diameter slurry shields. (2) SHAP 
interpretability analysis is utilized to further reveal the key factors 
influencing large-diameter slurry shields and provides theoretical 
guidance for improving the attitude control of large-diameter slurry 
shields. (3) The proposed and developed BO-CatBoost-MOEAD intelli -
gent prediction and optimization method can provide a deeper under -
standing of the relationships between construction parameters and 
large-diameter slurry SAs while effectively realizing the multiobjective 
optimization of large SA control. The number of input parameters can be 
adjusted in different scenarios to improve the optimization effect. The 
digital twin framework based on BO-CatBoost-MOEAD proposed in this 
paper can predict large-diameter slurry SA deviations in advance, 
providing a basis for staff to adjust the SA in real time.

theoretical foundation of the algorithms used in this paper. Section 3

2.8. SHAP 
The SHAP model was introduced in 1953 by Shapley L S [ 71 ]. It is a 
concept of cooperative games and solution values for those games. The 
SHAP value is a method of assigning the optimal solution for maximizing 
benefits or exercising the optimal voting rights. The method utilizes the 
idea of game theory to measure the magnitude of the impacts of features 
on the model output by calculating the marginal contribution of each 
feature ’ s SHAP value to the model. The SHAP value may be positive or 
negative, where a positive value enhances the predicted outcome and a 
negative value reduces the predicted outcome [ 72 ]. Additionally, the 
importance of a feature increases with both the absolute value of its 
contribution and its significance in the model [ 73 ].

3. Methodology and technical processes 
A large-diameter slurry SA prediction and optimization model is 
established based on BO-CatBoost-MOEAD. This model can achieve 
highly accurate predictions of large SAs and suggest regulations for bad 
attitude conditions. First, the evaluation indexes and factors influencing 
the large-diameter slurry SA are identified to determine the model in -
puts and outputs, and the data of these indexes are preprocessed. Sec -
ond, historical data are employed to train the BO-CatBoost prediction 
model, ensuring precise forecasts of large-diameter slurry SAs. Finally, 
MOEAD is utilized for multiobjective regulation of the large-diameter 
slurry SA to obtain a more stable large-diameter slurry SA. Fig. 1 
shows the detailed framework flow of this process.

4. Case study 
4.1. Project background 
This project site crosses the Yangtze River, totaling a length of 
3373.667 m. The shield tunneling interval from Lingwucun Station to 
Guobo Center South Station is designed as a single-hole, two-lane tunnel 
with a large-diameter slurry shield machine of 12.56 m in diameter, and 
the shield tunnel is constructed by the large-diameter slurry shield 
machine. The tunnel of the interval adopts a single hole and double line 
arrangement. Each ring of the tube sheet consists of 8 pieces of tube 
sheet in the form of 5 + 2 + 1. The internal structure of the shield tunnel 
combines precast and cast-in-place elements and is divided into six 
parts. Fig. 2 (a) shows the subway network map of Wuhan city, and Fig. 2 
(b) shows the location map of Lingwucun Station ~ Guobo Center South 
Station. Buried at depths ranging from 14.11 m to 32.887 m, the tunnel 
traverses strongly weathered mudstone, moderately weathered 
mudstone, and fine silt. The localized geological profile is shown in 
Fig. 3 . The surrounding rock and environmental conditions of the con -
struction interval from Lingwucun Station to Guobo Center South Sta -
tion are more complicated, and controlling the attitude and position of 
the shield machine is more difficult. Fig. 4 (a) shows the interface of the 
SA monitoring system, and Fig. 4 (b) provides a visual of the shield 
tunneling simulation. 
In this paper, CHHD, CHVD, STHD, STVD, PA and HA are selected as 
the SA prediction targets. 
4.2. Input parameter selection and data acquisition and preprocessing 
1. Input Parameter Selection 
Shield parameters are chosen considering geological conditions, 
operational characteristics of the shield boring machine, practical en -
gineering experience insights, and relevant references [ 78 ]. Increased 
cutterhead rotation heightens soil disturbance, impacting SA. Total 
thrust serves as an indirect indicator of interactions between the shield 
tunneling machine and the excavation surface, with increased thrust 
leading to increased soil disturbance and consequent effects on SA [ 22 ]. 
Push trust (A – F) is the main execution parameter for the shield operator 
to correct the SA. Maintaining soil stability and minimizing SA varia -
tions can be achieved by judiciously controlling the grouting pressure 
and volume. The chamber pressure evaluates excavation surface sta -
bility and serves as a representative shield machine parameter [ 79 ]. 
Drawing on expert insights, literature [ 78 ], and practical engineer -
ing experience, an index system encompassing 19 shield input param -
eters and 6 SA output parameters is proposed, as detailed in Table 2 . 
2. Data Acquisition 
Using Lingwucun Station ~ Guobo Center South Station in Wuhan as 
the analysis object, the section was constructed by a slurry-balanced 
shield machine. Real-time data logging and field monitoring facili -
tated the collection of data under three soil conditions: silty fine sand, 
angular gravelly soils and chalky clays, and gravelly soil, with approx -
imately 3000 sets of data for each. Using angular gravelly soils and 
chalky clays as examples, Table 2 lists the detailed information of the 
obtained data. 
3. Data Preprocessing 
Due to shield machine downtime for maintenance, sensor signal 
interference, and other factors, the detected data exhibit many anoma -
lies that must be preprocessed before the data can be used for subse -
quent prediction and optimization. The outliers in the raw data are 
eliminated according to Eqs. (16) and (17) , as described in Section 3.1 . 
Fig. 5 is a box diagram of the input and output parameters for the 
angular gravelly soils and chalky clays.

5.2. Shield attitude control digital twin framework 
The digital twin is used to open the channel from the physical system 
to the virtual system, as well as the virtual mapping of the physical 
system. In this paper, we emphasize big data analysis, so a digital twin 
framework for SA is constructed from three aspects: (1) the physical 
space of shield construction, (2) the information space, and (3) the 
interaction between the physical and information space. Fig. 12 shows 
the SA digital twin framework constructed in this paper.  
(1) The physical space of shield construction includes a physical 
entity business layer and a data layer. The physical entity busi -
ness layer extracts and expresses the processes, problems and 
knowledge of the entire shield construction process, constructs 
the business chain, and achieves perceptual access and fusion of 
multisource real-time data. The data layer utilizes collection, 
communication, digital integration and other technologies to 
manifest information, construct the whole process real-time big 
data chain, and achieve mapping from physical space to infor -
mation space.  
(2) The information space of shield construction includes a digital 
twin shell layer and an analysis layer. The digital twin shell layer 
utilizes fusion processing, node statutes, multisource heteroge -
neous data integration and conversion and other technologies to 
form a reusable digital chain, based on which the digital chain 
identifies, configures and manages digital twins. The digital twin 
analysis layer takes the big data analysis model as the core, in -
tegrates the knowledge model to achieve the intelligent analysis 
modeling of data – knowledge fusion, and achieves big data 
analysis of the shield construction attitude by combining the 
specific business requirements of SA monitoring, prediction and 
optimization.  
(3) The interactive mapping of the physical and information spaces 
of the digital twin of the shield construction attitude achieves 
mutual mapping, timely interaction and efficient synergy 
through the integration of sensing, computing, communication, 
control and other information technologies and automatic con -
trol technologies. Additionally, it achieves on-demand responses, 
fast iteration and dynamic optimization through the process of 
entity voxelization and voxel materialization. 
According to the SA adjustment control process and digital twin 
technology, an SA control twin system is designed to supplement and 
enhance the existing shield machine operating system, which primarily 
includes an attitude warning function and a parameter recommendation 
function. The interface design of the construction parameter recom -

SA, and the lower four boxes display the SA monitoring value within 24 
h. The upper right side shows the plan view of the construction section at

6. Conclusion and future work 
In this study, a BO-CatBoost-MOEAD-based SA prediction and 
optimization model is established with 6 SA parameters and 19 influ -
ence parameters based on the shield part of the Wuhan subway project 
as an example. The BO-CatBoost algorithm is used to establish the 
nonlinear mapping correlation between the influencing factors and the 
prediction objectives, and it is used as the fitness function of the mul -
tiobjective optimization algorithm to improve the optimization effi -
ciency of MOEAD multiobjective optimization. The shield construction 
parameters are adjusted according to the prediction and optimization 
results to control the SA and enhance shield construction safety and 
quality. In addition, a digital twin framework for SA is constructed to

better control the SA. 
(1) BO-CatBoost is used to train and simulate the measured engi -
neering data, and the obtained prediction model has higher ac -
curacy and lower error than do the BPNN, SVM, RF, and XGBoost 
prediction models. The coefficients of determination of the six SA 
prediction targets are greater than 0.9, the RMSEs are less than 
1.2, and the MAEs are less than 0.9 in all three soils. Hence, the 
BO-CatBoost model developed in this paper fit well, and the 
prediction results had small errors, indicating that this model can 
better predict the nonlinear relationships between the inputs and 
outputs.  
(2) The SHAP analysis shows that the push group A–F thrust (X4–X9) 
has the most significant effect on the large-diameter slurry SA, 
followed by the cutter rotating speed (X2). Therefore, the main 
adjustment of these parameters can effectively control the SA.  
(3) A multiobjective optimization method for large-diameter slurry 
SA based on BO-CatBoost-MOEAD is proposed. The more pa -
rameters that are adjusted, the better the optimization effect is. 
The maximum optimization rate can reach 23.436% compared 
with the original data. Moreover, a digital twin framework for 
intelligent prediction warning and SA optimization based on BO- 
CatBoost-MOEAD is proposed in this paper. The large-diameter 
slurry SA is predicted in advance based on existing data. Once 
the SA exceeds the warning range, the MOEAD optimization 
model is used to obtain the optimized shield construction pa -
rameters to adjust the SA. 
Although the proposed BO-CatBoost-MOEAD model exhibits good 
prediction and optimization performance, it still has several limitations. 
The three types of soils used in this paper have relatively uniform dis -
tributions with little variation in soil properties; therefore, this model 
may not be effective in predicting and optimizing large-diameter slurry 
SAs for complex soils. This paper is based on the results of SHAP analysis 
to select several main parameters that have greater impacts on the large- 
diameter slurry SA to adjust and objectively optimize the large-diameter 
slurry SA. However, the penetration degree and tool size may affect the 
large-diameter slurry SA in the actual construction process. The pre -
diction and optimization model of large-diameter slurry SA proposed in 
this paper needs to import real-time monitoring data manually; it cannot 
import monitoring data and realize prediction and optimization inde -
pendently. Therefore, in future studies, a more comprehensive system of 
factors influencing large-diameter slurry SA will be established, and 
more shield projects will be adopted to increase the amount of data 
available for different soil types to improve the generalizability of the 
model. At the same time, the data monitoring platform will be connected 
to the proposed prediction and optimization model to realize indepen -
dent prediction and optimization.
