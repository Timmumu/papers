Although many signal processing and data-driven methods have been proposed to construct the HI, most of the existing methods are based on manual feature extraction techniques and require the prior knowledge of experts, or rely on a large amount of failure data. The proposed method was tested on a benchmark bearing dataset and compared with several other traditional HI construction models. Experimental results indicate that the constructed HI exhibited a monotonically increasing degradation trend and had good performance in terms of detecting incipient faults.
